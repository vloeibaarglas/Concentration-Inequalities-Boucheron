**Central idea.** For $p \geq 1/2$, the Bernoulli log-MGF is sub-Gaussian on $\lambda \geq 0$: $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(X-p)} \leq \lambda^2p(1-p)/2$. Writing the deficit as $\phi(\lambda)$, $\phi(0) = \phi'(0) = 0$ and $\phi''(\lambda) \leq 0$ reduces to $(1-p+pe^\lambda)^2 \geq e^\lambda$, which holds for $p \geq 1/2$ since $g(s) = (1-p+ps)^2 - s$ is convex with $g(1) = 0$, $g'(1) = 2p-1 \geq 0$. The lower tail follows by applying this to $1 - X_i \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(1-p)$.

**Solution.** Let $\varepsilon > 0$ and $X \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(p)$ with $p \geq 1/2$. If $p = 1$, then $B = n$ a.s.\ and $\mathbb{P}\{B-np \ge n\varepsilon\} = 0$; assume $p < 1$. Define
$$\phi(\lambda) = \log(1-p+pe^\lambda) - \lambda p - \frac{\lambda^2}{2}p(1-p), \qquad \lambda \geq 0,$$
so that $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(X-p)} \leq \lambda^2p(1-p)/2$ is $\phi(\lambda) \leq 0$. Now $\phi(0) = 0$,
$$\phi'(\lambda) = \frac{pe^\lambda}{1-p+pe^\lambda} - p - \lambda p(1-p), \quad \phi'(0) = 0,$$
$$\phi''(\lambda) = p(1-p)\left[\frac{e^\lambda}{(1-p+pe^\lambda)^2} - 1\right].$$
Thus $\phi''(\lambda) \leq 0$ iff $e^\lambda \leq (1-p+pe^\lambda)^2$. With $s = e^\lambda \geq 1$ and $g(s) = (1-p+ps)^2 - s$: $g(1) = 0$, $g'(s) = 2p(1-p+ps) - 1$ with $g'(1) = 2p - 1 \geq 0$, and $g''(s) = 2p^2 > 0$. Since $g'$ is strictly increasing and $g'(1) \geq 0$, $g$ is increasing on $[1,\infty)$, so $g(s) \geq 0$ for $s \geq 1$, with strict inequality for $s > 1$. Hence $\phi''(\lambda) < 0$ for $\lambda > 0$, so $\phi'$ is strictly decreasing, $\phi'(\lambda) < 0$ for $\lambda > 0$, and $\phi(\lambda) < 0$ for $\lambda > 0$:
$$\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(X-p)} < e^{\lambda^2p(1-p)/2}, \qquad \lambda > 0.$$
For $B = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ with $X_i$ i.i.d. $\mathrm{Bernoulli}(p)$, $\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(B-np)} < e^{n\lambda^2p(1-p)/2}$, so by Chernoff,
$$\mathbb{P}\{B - np \geq n\varepsilon\} < \exp\!\left(\tfrac{n\lambda^2p(1-p)}{2} - \lambda n\varepsilon\right).$$
With $\lambda = \varepsilon/(p(1-p)) > 0$, this is $< \exp(-n\varepsilon^2/(2p(1-p)))$.

If $p \leq 1/2$ and $Y_i = 1 - X_i \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(1-p)$ with $1 - p \geq 1/2$, then $\{B - np \leq -n\varepsilon\} = \{\sum_i Y_i - n(1-p) \geq n\varepsilon\}$, and the upper-tail bound applied to $\sum_i Y_i$ gives $\mathbb{P}\{B - np \leq -n\varepsilon\} < \exp(-n\varepsilon^2/(2(1-p)p))$.
