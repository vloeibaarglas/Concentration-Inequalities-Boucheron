**Central idea.** The upper bound $\mathbb{E}\max_i G_i \le \sqrt{2\log N}$ is the book's maximal inequality (Jensen + union bound, optimized over $\lambda$). Sharpness follows from a Gaussian lower-tail bound: with $t_N = \sqrt{2\log N - 2\log\log N}$, the union bound in reverse shows $\mathbb{P}\{\max_i G_i \ge t_N\} \to 1$, so $\mathbb{E}\max_i G_i \ge t_N(1-o(1))$.

**Solution.** *Upper bound.* For $\lambda > 0$, by Jensen's inequality and the union bound,
$$\exp\!\left(\lambda\,\mathbb{E}\max_{1\le i\le N} G_i\right) \le \mathbb{E}\!\left[e^{\lambda \max_i G_i}\right] = \mathbb{E}\!\left[\max_i e^{\lambda G_i}\right] \le \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{E}[e^{\lambda G_i}] = Ne^{\lambda^2/2},$$
using $\mathbb{E}[e^{\lambda G_i}] = e^{\lambda^2/2}$ for standard normal $G_i$. Taking logarithms,
$$\mathbb{E}\max_{1\le i\le N} G_i \le \frac{\log N}{\lambda} + \frac{\lambda}{2}.$$
Minimizing over $\lambda > 0$ (attained at $\lambda = \sqrt{2\log N}$) gives
$$\mathbb{E}\max_{1\le i\le N} G_i \le \sqrt{2\log N}, \qquad\text{hence}\qquad \frac{\mathbb{E}\max_i G_i}{\sqrt{2\log N}} \le 1.$$

*Lower bound.* For $t \ge 1$, bounding the Gaussian density from below on $[t, t+1/t]$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{G_1 \ge t\} \ge \int_t^{t+1/t} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-x^2/2}\,dx \ge \frac{1}{t\sqrt{2\pi}}\,e^{-(t+1/t)^2/2} \ge c_0\,\frac{e^{-t^2/2}}{t},$$
with $c_0 = e^{-3/2}/\sqrt{2\pi}$, since $(t+1/t)^2 = t^2+2+1/t^2 \le t^2+3$.

Take $t_N = \sqrt{2\log N - 2\log\log N}$ (for $N \ge 3$). Then $e^{-t_N^2/2} = \frac{\log N}{N}$, so with $p_N = \mathbb{P}\{G_1 \ge t_N\}$,
$$Np_N \ge c_0\,\frac{\log N}{t_N} \ge c_0\sqrt{\frac{\log N}{2}} \longrightarrow \infty.$$
By independence,
$$\mathbb{P}\!\left\{\max_{1\le i\le N} G_i \ge t_N\right\} = 1-(1-p_N)^N \ge 1-e^{-Np_N} \longrightarrow 1.$$
Since $\max_i G_i \ge t_N\mathbf{1}_{\{\max_i G_i \ge t_N\}} - (\max_i G_i)^-$ pointwise (the subtracted term corrects the case $\max_i G_i < 0$), and $(\max_i G_i)^- \le G_1^-$ gives $\mathbb{E}[(\max_i G_i)^-] \le 1/\sqrt{2\pi}$,
$$\mathbb{E}\max_{1\le i\le N} G_i \ge t_N\,(1-e^{-Np_N}) - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}},$$
and therefore
$$\frac{\mathbb{E}\max_i G_i}{\sqrt{2\log N}} \ge \frac{\sqrt{2\log N - 2\log\log N}}{\sqrt{2\log N}}\,(1-e^{-Np_N}) - \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi\log N}} \longrightarrow 1.$$

Combined with the upper bound, the limit equals $1$.
