**Central idea.** Diagonalize $A$ (symmetric, zero diagonal) to $Z \stackrel{d}{=} \sum_k \mu_k(Y_k^2-1)$ with $Y_k$ iid $N(0,1)$. The *upper* bound comes from the mgf: $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z} \leq \lambda^2\|A\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2/(1-2|\lambda|\,\|A\|)$, placing $Z$ in $\Gamma_+(2\|A\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2, 2\|A\|)$, then Exercise 2.21. The *lower* bound uses decoupling: with $Y'$ an independent copy, $Z - Z' = 2\sum_k\mu_k U_k V_k$ for iid Gaussians $(U_k, V_k)$; conditioning on $V$ makes the sum Gaussian, and Jensen gives the $\sqrt{q}\,\|A\|_{\mathrm{HS}}$ term while the top eigendirection gives the $q\,\|A\|$ term.

**Solution.** Write $a = \|A\|_{\mathrm{HS}}$, $b = \|A\|$. Since $A$ is symmetric with zero diagonal, $A = \sum_k \mu_k u_k u_k^{\top}$ in an orthonormal basis; with $Y_k = u_k^{\top}X$ iid $N(0,1)$ and $\sum_k\mu_k = \mathrm{tr}(A) = 0$,
$$Z = \sum_k \mu_k(Y_k^2-1), \qquad a^2 = \sum_k\mu_k^2, \qquad b = \max_k|\mu_k|.$$
Let $\gamma_q = (\mathbb{E}|G|^q)^{1/q}$ for $G \sim N(0,1)$; then $\gamma_q \geq \sqrt{q}/2$ for $q \geq 2$ (from $\mathbb{E}|G|^q = 2^{q/2}\Gamma(\frac{q+1}{2})/\sqrt{\pi}$ and Stirling's lower bound $\Gamma(x) \geq \sqrt{2\pi/x}\,(x/e)^x$).

*Upper bound.* For $|u| < 1$, $-\log(1-u)-u \leq \frac{u^2}{2(1-|u|)}$ (for $u > 0$ this is Exercise 2.8(a); for $u \leq 0$ put $\psi(u)=\frac{u^2}{2}-(-\log(1-u)-u)$, so $\psi'(u)=-\frac{u^2}{1-u}\le0$ and $\psi(u)\ge\psi(0)=0$). With $\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda\mu(Y^2-1)} = e^{-\lambda\mu}(1-2\lambda\mu)^{-1/2}$, for $|\lambda| < 1/(2b)$,
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z} = \sum_k \tfrac12\big(-\log(1-2\lambda\mu_k)-2\lambda\mu_k\big) \leq \frac{\lambda^2 a^2}{1-2|\lambda|\,b}.$$
So $Z$ and $-Z$ lie in $\Gamma_+(2a^2, 2b)$. By Exercise 2.21 (direct part, $C = 4$), for $q \geq 2$,
$$(\mathbb{E}|Z|^q)^{1/q} \leq 2^{1/q}\cdot 4\big(\sqrt{2q}\,a + 2q\,b\big) \leq 12\big(\sqrt{q}\,a + q\,b\big).$$

*Lower bound (decoupling).* Let $Y'$ be an independent copy of $Y$ and $Z' = \sum_k\mu_k({Y_k'}^2-1)$. By Minkowski, $(\mathbb{E}|Z-Z'|^q)^{1/q} \leq 2(\mathbb{E}|Z|^q)^{1/q}$. Set $U_k = (Y_k-Y_k')/\sqrt{2}$, $V_k = (Y_k+Y_k')/\sqrt{2}$: these are iid $N(0,1)$ (orthogonal transformation), and $Y_k^2-{Y_k'}^2 = 2U_kV_k$, so $Z - Z' = 2\sum_k\mu_kU_kV_k$. Conditionally on $V$, the sum $\sum_k\mu_kU_kV_k$ is $N(0, \sum_k\mu_k^2V_k^2)$, hence
$$\mathbb{E}\Big|\sum_k\mu_kU_kV_k\Big|^q = \gamma_q^q\,\mathbb{E}\Big(\sum_k\mu_k^2V_k^2\Big)^{q/2}.$$
By Jensen ($q/2 \geq 1$), $\mathbb{E}(\sum_k\mu_k^2V_k^2)^{q/2} \geq (\sum_k\mu_k^2)^{q/2} = a^q$. Keeping only $k^*$ with $|\mu_{k^*}| = b$ gives $\mathbb{E}(\sum_k\mu_k^2V_k^2)^{q/2} \geq b^q\gamma_q^q$. Therefore, since $(\mathbb{E}|Z|^q)^{1/q} \geq (\mathbb{E}|\sum_k\mu_kU_kV_k|^q)^{1/q}$ (the factor of $2$ cancels),
$$(\mathbb{E}|Z|^q)^{1/q} \geq \max\big(\gamma_q a,\ \gamma_q^2 b\big) \geq \tfrac12\Big(\tfrac{\sqrt{q}}{2}a + \tfrac{q}{4}b\Big) \geq \tfrac18\big(\sqrt{q}\,a + q\,b\big).$$
The two-sided estimate holds with $c = 1/8$, $C = 12$, uniformly in $n$ and $q \geq 2$. ∎
