**Central idea.** Write $Z = \|G\|_2$ with $G \sim N(0, I_D)$. The upper bound is Jensen ($\sqrt{\cdot}$ concave). For the lower bound, the Gaussian Poincaré inequality gives $\mathrm{Var}(Z) \le \mathbb{E}\|\nabla \|G\|_2\|^2 = 1$, since the Euclidean norm is $1$-Lipschitz with unit gradient a.e.; then $(\mathbb{E}Z)^2 = D - \mathrm{Var}(Z) \ge D - 1$, and $\sqrt{D-1} \ge \sqrt{D} - 1$ for $D \ge 1$.

**Solution.** Since $Z^2 \sim \chi^2_D$, we have $Z \stackrel{d}{=} \|G\|_2$ where $G = (G_1, \ldots, G_D)$ has i.i.d.\ $N(0,1)$ coordinates; in particular $\mathbb{E}[Z^2] = D$.

*Upper bound.* By Jensen's inequality ($\sqrt{\cdot}$ concave),
$$\mathbb{E}Z = \mathbb{E}\sqrt{Z^2} \le \sqrt{\mathbb{E}[Z^2]} = \sqrt{D}.$$

*Lower bound.* Let $f(x) = \|x\|_2$. Then $\nabla f(x) = x/\|x\|_2$ for $x \ne 0$, so $\|\nabla f(x)\|_2 = 1$ a.e. The Gaussian Poincaré inequality gives
$$\mathrm{Var}(Z) = \mathrm{Var}(f(G)) \le \mathbb{E}\|\nabla f(G)\|_2^2 = 1.$$
Hence $(\mathbb{E}Z)^2 = \mathbb{E}[Z^2] - \mathrm{Var}(Z) \ge D - 1$, and since $\mathbb{E}Z \ge 0$,
$$\mathbb{E}Z \ge \sqrt{D-1}.$$
For $D \ge 1$, $\sqrt{D-1} \ge \sqrt{D} - 1$: both sides are nonnegative (as $\sqrt{D} \ge 1$), and squaring gives $D - 1 \ge D - 2\sqrt{D} + 1$, i.e.\ $2\sqrt{D} \ge 2$. Therefore $\mathbb{E}Z \ge \sqrt{D} - 1$. ∎
