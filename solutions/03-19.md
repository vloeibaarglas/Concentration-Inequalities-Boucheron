**Central idea.** Bound $\mathrm{Var}(\sqrt{X})$ by $\mathbb{E}[(\sqrt{X}-\sqrt{\mu})^2]$ with $\mu=\mathbb{E}X$, rewrite $(\sqrt{X}-\sqrt{\mu})^2=\frac{(X-\mu)^2}{(\sqrt{X}+\sqrt{\mu})^2}\le\frac{(X-\mu)^2}{X+\mu}$, and observe $\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{(X-\mu)^2}{X+\mu}\right]\le\frac{\mathrm{Var}(X)}{\mu}$ because $\frac{1}{\mu}-\frac{1}{X+\mu}=\frac{X}{\mu(X+\mu)}\ge 0$ for $X\ge 0$.

**Solution.** If $\mu:=\mathbb{E}X=0$ then $X=0$ a.s.\ (as $X\ge 0$) and there is nothing to prove. Assume $\mu>0$. Since $\mathrm{Var}(Y)=\min_{c}\mathbb{E}[(Y-c)^{2}]$,
$$\mathrm{Var}(\sqrt{X})\le\mathbb{E}\left[(\sqrt{X}-\sqrt{\mu})^{2}\right].$$
Now $(\sqrt{X}-\sqrt{\mu})^{2}(\sqrt{X}+\sqrt{\mu})^{2}=(X-\mu)^{2}$, so
$$(\sqrt{X}-\sqrt{\mu})^{2}=\frac{(X-\mu)^{2}}{(\sqrt{X}+\sqrt{\mu})^{2}}\le\frac{(X-\mu)^{2}}{X+\mu},$$
using $(\sqrt{X}+\sqrt{\mu})^{2}=X+\mu+2\sqrt{X\mu}\ge X+\mu$ (and $X+\mu\ge\mu>0$). Hence
$$\mathrm{Var}(\sqrt{X})\le\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{(X-\mu)^{2}}{X+\mu}\right].$$
Moreover,
$$\frac{\mathbb{E}[(X-\mu)^{2}]}{\mu}-\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{(X-\mu)^{2}}{X+\mu}\right]=\mathbb{E}\left[(X-\mu)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{\mu}-\frac{1}{X+\mu}\right)\right]=\frac{1}{\mu}\,\mathbb{E}\left[(X-\mu)^{2}\cdot\frac{X}{X+\mu}\right]\ge 0,$$
since $X\ge 0$. Therefore $\mathrm{Var}(\sqrt{X})\le\mathrm{Var}(X)/\mu\le a$, the last step by the hypothesis $\mathrm{Var}(X)\le a\mu$. ∎
