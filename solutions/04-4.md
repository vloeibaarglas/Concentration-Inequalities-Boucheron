**Central idea.** Factor the joint densities into conditionals, $p(x_{1:n}) = \prod_{i=1}^n p(x_i|x_{1:i-1})$ (and likewise for $q$): the log-likelihood ratio splits into a sum of $n$ conditional log-likelihood ratios. The tower property of expectation then turns the $i$-th term's $P$-expectation into the $P_{1:i-1}$-expectation of the conditional relative entropy $D(P_{X_i|1:i-1}\|Q_{X_i|1:i-1})$.

**Solution.** Assume $P \ll Q$ (otherwise $D(P\|Q) = \infty$ and the identity holds trivially with the convention $\infty = \infty$ whenever some term is infinite). Write $p_{1:n}$, $q_{1:n}$ for the joint densities and $p_{i|1:i-1}$, $q_{i|1:i-1}$ for the conditional densities of $X_i$ given $X_{1:i-1}$ (with the $i=1$ case understood as the marginal). Then
$$\frac{p_{1:n}(x_{1:n})}{q_{1:n}(x_{1:n})} = \prod_{i=1}^n \frac{p_{i|1:i-1}(x_i|x_{1:i-1})}{q_{i|1:i-1}(x_i|x_{1:i-1})},$$
so with logarithms,
$$D(P\|Q) = \mathbb{E}_P\left[\log\frac{p_{1:n}(X_{1:n})}{q_{1:n}(X_{1:n})}\right] = \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{E}_P\left[\log\frac{p_{i|1:i-1}(X_i|X_{1:i-1})}{q_{i|1:i-1}(X_i|X_{1:i-1})}\right].$$
For fixed $i$, conditioning on $X_{1:i-1}$ and using the tower property,
\begin{align*}
\mathbb{E}_P\left[\log\frac{p_{i|1:i-1}(X_i|X_{1:i-1})}{q_{i|1:i-1}(X_i|X_{1:i-1})}\right]
&= \mathbb{E}_{P_{1:i-1}}\left[\mathbb{E}_{P_{X_i|1:i-1}}\left[\left.\log\frac{p_{i|1:i-1}(X_i|x_{1:i-1})}{q_{i|1:i-1}(X_i|x_{1:i-1})}\,\right|\,X_{1:i-1} = x_{1:i-1}\right]\right] \\
&= \mathbb{E}_{P_{1:i-1}}\left[D\left(P_{X_i|1\ldots i-1}\,\|\,Q_{X_i|1\ldots i-1}\right)\right],
\end{align*}
since the inner expectation is exactly the relative entropy of the conditional distributions given $x_{1:i-1}$. Summing over $i$ gives the chain rule. ∎
