**Central idea.** Split $\partial A$ by direction: for fixed $i$, each nonempty fiber of $A$ over $z\in A_i$ has a maximum, contributing $(x,e_i,+1)\in\partial A$, and a minimum, contributing $(x,e_i,-1)\in\partial A$ — at least $2|A_i|$ boundary points in direction $i$. Summing over $i$ and applying AM–GM and Loomis–Whitney (Exercise 4.6) gives $|\partial A|\ge2\sum_i|A_i|\ge2d|A|^{(d-1)/d}$.

**Solution.** Write $B=\{e_1,\dots,e_d\}$ and partition $\partial A=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^d S_i$ with $S_i=\{(x,e_i,s)\in\partial A\}$. Fix $i$ and $z\in A_i$, where $A_i$ is the projection of $A$ along coordinate $i$. The fiber
$$F_z=\{t\in\mathbb{Z}:(z_1,\dots,z_{i-1},t,z_{i+1},\dots,z_d)\in A\}$$
is a finite nonempty subset of $\mathbb{Z}$. Let $t^+_z=\max F_z$, $t^-_z=\min F_z$, and let $x^+_z,x^-_z\in A$ be the corresponding points. Then $x^+_z+e_i\notin A$ (otherwise $t^+_z+1\in F_z$) and $x^-_z-e_i\notin A$ (otherwise $t^-_z-1\in F_z$), so
$$(x^+_z,e_i,+1)\in S_i,\qquad (x^-_z,e_i,-1)\in S_i.$$
For fixed $z$ these two triples are distinct (they differ in $s$), and for distinct $z$ the triples are distinct (they differ in the projection). Hence $|S_i|\ge2|A_i|$, and
$$|\partial A|=\sum_{i=1}^d|S_i|\ge2\sum_{i=1}^d|A_i|.$$
By AM–GM and the discrete Loomis–Whitney inequality (Exercise 4.6),
$$\sum_{i=1}^d|A_i|\ge d\Big(\prod_{i=1}^d|A_i|\Big)^{1/d}\ge d\big(|A|^{\,d-1}\big)^{1/d}=d|A|^{\frac{d-1}{d}}.$$
Therefore $|\partial A|\ge2d|A|^{(d-1)/d}$. ∎
