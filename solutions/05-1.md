**Central idea.** Interpolate between variance ($p=1$) and entropy ($p \uparrow 2$) via $\Psi_p(Z) = \|Z\|_2^2 - \|Z\|_p^2$. The normalized ratio $\Psi_p(Z)/((1/p)-(1/2))$ is nondecreasing in $p$ by H\"older's log-convexity of $L^p$ norms, which reduces to the scalar inequality $(1-u)\gamma \le 1-u^\gamma$ for $u \in [0,1]$, $\gamma \in (0,1)$. Its limit as $p \uparrow 2$ is $2\,\mathrm{Ent}(Z^2)$ (l'H\^opital), while at $p=1$ it equals $2\,\mathrm{Var}(Z)$. A Rademacher $Z$ has $Z^2 \equiv 1$, so $\mathrm{Ent}(Z^2)=0 < 1=\mathrm{Var}(Z)$, showing nonnegativity is essential.

**Solution.** Let $Z \ge 0$ with $\mathbb{E}[Z^2] < \infty$ (otherwise there is nothing to prove) and put $\Psi_p(Z) = \mathbb{E}[Z^2]-(\mathbb{E}[Z^p])^{2/p}$ for $p \in [1,2)$. Note $\Psi_1(Z) = \mathrm{Var}(Z)$.

*Monotonicity.* Fix $1 \le p < q < 2$ and write $\alpha = 1/p-1/2 > \beta = 1/q-1/2 > 0$, $X = \|Z\|_p^2$, $Y = \|Z\|_q^2$, $a = \|Z\|_2^2$. We show $\frac{a-X}{\alpha} \le \frac{a-Y}{\beta}$, i.e.\ $(a-X)\beta \le (a-Y)\alpha$. By log-convexity of $L^p$ norms on a probability space, with $\frac1q = \frac{1-\theta}{p}+\frac{\theta}{2}$,
$$\|Z\|_q \le \|Z\|_p^{1-\theta}\|Z\|_2^{\theta}, \qquad \theta = \frac{\alpha-\beta}{\alpha} \in (0,1),$$
so $Y \le X^{\beta/\alpha}a^{(\alpha-\beta)/\alpha}$. It suffices that $(a-X)\beta \le \big(a-X^{\beta/\alpha}a^{(\alpha-\beta)/\alpha}\big)\alpha$. Dividing by $a > 0$ (if $a=0$ all terms vanish) and setting $u = X/a \in [0,1]$ (since $\|Z\|_p \le \|Z\|_2$), $\gamma = \beta/\alpha \in (0,1)$, this is $(1-u)\gamma \le 1-u^{\gamma}$. Indeed $f(u) := 1-u^{\gamma}-\gamma(1-u)$ satisfies $f(1)=0$ and $f'(u) = \gamma(1-u^{\gamma-1}) < 0$ on $(0,1)$ (as $\gamma < 1$ gives $u^{\gamma-1} > 1$), hence $f \ge 0$ on $[0,1]$. So $\Psi_p(Z)/((1/p)-(1/2))$ is nondecreasing in $p$.

*Limit.* (Note: the hint as stated omits the normalization — $\Psi_p(Z) \to \Psi_2(Z) = 0$ as $p \uparrow 2$. The correct statement, proved here, is for the normalized ratio $\Psi_p(Z)/((1/p)-(1/2))$.) Put $F(p) = \mathbb{E}[Z^p]$. By l'H\^opital (justified e.g.\ when $\mathbb{E}[Z^2(1+|\log Z|)] < \infty$; the general case follows by truncation),
$$\lim_{p \uparrow 2}\frac{\Psi_p(Z)}{1/p-1/2} = \lim_{p\to 2} p^2 e^{G(p)}G'(p) = 4\,\mathbb{E}[Z^2]\,G'(2),$$
where $G(p) = \frac2p\log F(p)$ and $G'(2) = -\frac12\log\mathbb{E}[Z^2]+\frac{\mathbb{E}[Z^2\log Z]}{\mathbb{E}[Z^2]}$. Hence the limit equals $4\mathbb{E}[Z^2\log Z]-2\mathbb{E}[Z^2]\log\mathbb{E}[Z^2] = 2\,\mathrm{Ent}(Z^2)$, since $\mathrm{Ent}(Z^2) = 2\mathbb{E}[Z^2\log Z]-\mathbb{E}[Z^2]\log\mathbb{E}[Z^2]$.

*Conclusion.* At $p=1$, $\Psi_1(Z)/((1)-(1/2)) = 2\,\mathrm{Var}(Z)$. Monotonicity gives $2\,\mathrm{Var}(Z) \le \Psi_p(Z)/((1/p)-(1/2))$ for $p \in [1,2)$; letting $p \uparrow 2$ yields $2\,\mathrm{Var}(Z) \le 2\,\mathrm{Ent}(Z^2)$, i.e.\ $\mathrm{Var}(Z) \le \mathrm{Ent}(Z^2)$.

*Nonnegativity is needed.* Let $Z$ be Rademacher ($\pm 1$ with probability $1/2$). Then $\mathrm{Var}(Z) = 1$ but $Z^2 \equiv 1$ gives $\mathrm{Ent}(Z^2) = 0$. ∎
