**Central idea.** Induction on $n$: writing $S_n=S_{n-1}+\varepsilon_n\alpha_n$ and averaging over $\varepsilon_n=\pm1$ kills odd powers in the binomial expansion, leaving a recurrence in the even moments. The induction closes via the combinatorial inequality $\binom{2p}{2j}(2j-1)!!\le(2p-1)!!\binom{p}{j}$, i.e. $2^kk!/(2k)!\le1$. Optimality: with $\alpha_i=1/\sqrt{n}$ the CLT gives $\mathbb{E}|S_n|^{2p}\to\mathbb{E}|G|^{2p}=(2p)!/(2^pp!)$.

**Solution.** Let $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^n\varepsilon_i\alpha_i$ and $M^{(n)}_p=\mathbb{E}|S_n|^{2p}$. Since $|S_n|^{2p}=S_n^{2p}$,
$$\mathbb{E}|S_n|^{2p}=\tfrac12\mathbb{E}[(S_{n-1}+\alpha_n)^{2p}]+\tfrac12\mathbb{E}[(S_{n-1}-\alpha_n)^{2p}].$$
Binomial expansion gives $(S_{n-1}\pm\alpha_n)^{2p}=\sum_{k=0}^{2p}\binom{2p}{k}S_{n-1}^k(\pm\alpha_n)^{2p-k}$; averaging over the signs, odd powers of $\pm\alpha_n$ cancel:
$$M^{(n)}_p=\sum_{j=0}^{p}\binom{2p}{2j}M^{(n-1)}_j\,\alpha_n^{2p-2j},\qquad M^{(n)}_0=1.$$
We prove $M^{(n)}_p\le(2p-1)!!\,(\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_i^2)^p$ by induction on $n$, where $(2p-1)!!=(2p)!/(2^pp!)$. For $n=1$, $M^{(1)}_p=|\alpha_1|^{2p}\le(2p-1)!!\,|\alpha_1|^{2p}$ since $(2p-1)!!\ge1$. Let $A=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\alpha_i^2$ and assume $M^{(n-1)}_j\le(2j-1)!!\,A^j$ for $j\le p$. Then
$$M^{(n)}_p\le\sum_{j=0}^{p}\binom{2p}{2j}(2j-1)!!\,A^j\alpha_n^{2p-2j}.$$
Since $(A+\alpha_n^2)^p=\sum_{j=0}^p\binom{p}{j}A^j\alpha_n^{2(p-j)}$, it suffices that $\binom{2p}{2j}(2j-1)!!\le(2p-1)!!\binom{p}{j}$ for all $j$. Indeed,
$$\frac{\binom{2p}{2j}(2j-1)!!}{(2p-1)!!\binom{p}{j}}=\frac{(2p)!/(2p-2j)!\,2^jj!}{(2p)!/2^pj!(p-j)!}=\frac{2^{\,p-j}(p-j)!}{(2(p-j))!}=\frac{1}{(2k-1)!!}\le1,$$
with $k=p-j$ and $(2j)!/(2^jj!)=(2j-1)!!$. This completes the induction.

*Optimality.* Take $\alpha_i=1/\sqrt{n}$, so $\sum_i\alpha_i^2=1$ and $S_n=\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\sum_{i=1}^n\varepsilon_i$. By the CLT, $S_n\Rightarrow G\sim N(0,1)$; Hoeffding's inequality gives uniform exponential tails, hence $\mathbb{E}|S_n|^{2p}\to\mathbb{E}|G|^{2p}$. Since $\mathbb{E}[G^{2p}]=(2p-1)!!=(2p)!/(2^pp!)$,
$$\frac{\mathbb{E}|\sum_i\varepsilon_i\alpha_i|^{2p}}{(\sum_i\alpha_i^2)^p}\longrightarrow\frac{(2p)!}{2^pp!}\qquad(n\to\infty),$$
so no smaller dimension-free constant works. ∎
