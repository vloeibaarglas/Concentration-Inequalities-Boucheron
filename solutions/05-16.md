**Central idea.** Herbst's argument via the Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality: applying the LSI to $e^{\lambda Z/2}$ gives the differential inequality $\lambda H'-H\leq \frac{\lambda^2}{2}R(\lambda)$ for the log-mgf $H(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}$, where $R(\lambda)$ is the tilted mean of $\|\nabla f\|^2$. The entropy variational formula $\mathbb{E}_Q[W]\leq \log\mathbb{E}_P[e^W]+D(Q\|P)$ (Corollary 4.15) bounds $R(\lambda)$ through $K(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{(\lambda/\theta)\|\nabla f\|^2}$, closing the ODE as $(1-\lambda\theta/2)(\lambda H'-H)\leq \frac{\lambda\theta}{2}K(\lambda)$; integrating with monotonicity of $K(\lambda)/\lambda$ and $-\log(1-x)\leq x/(1-x)$ yields the claim.

**Solution.** We argue formally, assuming the expectations below are finite (justified by applying the argument to the truncations $f_M=(-M)\vee(f\wedge M)$, for which $\|\nabla f_M\|\leq\|\nabla f\|$ a.e., and passing to the limit). Put $H(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}$, so $H(0)=H'(0)=0$.

Apply the Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality to $g=e^{\lambda Z/2}$:
$$\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})=\mathrm{Ent}(g^2)\leq 2\mathbb{E}\|\nabla g\|^2=\frac{\lambda^2}{2}\mathbb{E}\big[\|\nabla f\|^2e^{\lambda Z}\big].$$
Since $\frac{d}{d\lambda}\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}=\mathbb{E}[Ze^{\lambda Z}]/\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}$,
$$\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})=\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}\,\big(\lambda H'(\lambda)-H(\lambda)\big),$$
so with $R(\lambda)=\mathbb{E}[\|\nabla f\|^2e^{\lambda Z}]/\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}$,
$$\lambda H'(\lambda)-H(\lambda)\leq \frac{\lambda^2}{2}R(\lambda).\tag{1}$$

Let $dQ_\lambda=e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}dP/e^{H(\lambda)}$, so $R(\lambda)=\mathbb{E}_{Q_\lambda}[\|\nabla f\|^2]$. By the entropy variational formula, for $W=(\lambda/\theta)\|\nabla f\|^2$,
$$\mathbb{E}_{Q_\lambda}[W]\leq \log\mathbb{E}_P[e^W]+D(Q_\lambda\|P),$$
and $D(Q_\lambda\|P)=\mathbb{E}_{Q_\lambda}[\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)-H(\lambda)]=\lambda H'(\lambda)-H(\lambda)$. Hence, with $K(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}\exp\big(\tfrac{\lambda}{\theta}\|\nabla f\|^2\big)$ (finite for $\lambda/\theta<\lambda_0$),
$$R(\lambda)\leq \frac{\theta}{\lambda}K(\lambda)+\theta H'(\lambda)-\frac{\theta}{\lambda}H(\lambda).\tag{2}$$
This is the content of Corollary 4.15. Combining (1)–(2),
$$\lambda H'-H\leq \frac{\lambda\theta}{2}\big(K(\lambda)+\lambda H'-H\big),$$
i.e., for $\lambda\theta<2$,
$$(1-\tfrac{\lambda\theta}{2})(\lambda H'-H)\leq \tfrac{\lambda\theta}{2}K(\lambda).\tag{3}$$

Write $K(\lambda)=\lambda L(\lambda)$ with $L(\lambda)=K(\lambda)/\lambda$. Then (3) gives
$$\left(\frac{H(\lambda)}{\lambda}\right)'=\frac{\lambda H'-H}{\lambda^2}\leq \frac{\theta}{2(1-\lambda\theta/2)}L(\lambda).$$
Now $L$ is nondecreasing: with $M(u)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{uV}$, $V=\|\nabla f\|^2$, we have $L(\lambda)=\frac{1}{\theta}\frac{M(\lambda/\theta)}{\lambda/\theta}$, and $\frac{d}{du}\frac{M(u)}{u}=\frac{N(u)}{u^2}$ where $N(u)=uM'(u)-M(u)$ satisfies $N(0)=0$, $N'(u)=uM''(u)\geq 0$ (as $M''(u)$ is a variance). Since $H(\lambda)/\lambda\to H'(0)=0$,
$$\frac{H(\lambda)}{\lambda}\leq \frac{\theta}{2}L(\lambda)\int_0^{\lambda}\frac{ds}{1-s\theta/2}=-L(\lambda)\log(1-\tfrac{\lambda\theta}{2})\leq L(\lambda)\frac{\lambda\theta/2}{1-\lambda\theta/2},$$
using $-\log(1-x)\leq x/(1-x)$ for $x\in[0,1)$. Multiplying by $\lambda$ and recalling $\lambda L(\lambda)=K(\lambda)$,
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}\leq \frac{\lambda\theta}{2(1-\lambda\theta/2)}\log\mathbb{E}\exp\!\left(\tfrac{\lambda}{\theta}\|\nabla f\|^2\right).\qquad\blacksquare$$

*Application.* Let $P$ be the orthogonal projection onto a $k$-dimensional subspace and $f(x)=\|Px\|^2$. Then $\nabla f(x)=2Px$, so $\|\nabla f\|^2=4\|Px\|^2=4f$, and $Z=\|PX\|^2$ is $\chi^2_k$ with $\mathbb{E}Z=k$. Moreover $\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda\|\nabla f\|^2}=\mathbb{E}e^{4\lambda Z}=(1-8\lambda)^{-k/2}<\infty$ for $\lambda<1/8$, so $\lambda_0=1/8$. Taking e.g. $\theta=8$ (so $\lambda\theta<2$ and $\lambda/\theta<\lambda_0$ for $\lambda<1/4$),
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-k)}\leq \frac{4\lambda}{1-4\lambda}\Big(-\frac{k}{2}\log(1-\lambda)\Big)\leq 8k\lambda^2,\qquad 0<\lambda\leq 1/8,$$
using $-\log(1-\lambda)\leq 2\lambda$. By Chernoff's bound, with $\lambda=t/(16k)$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{\|PX\|^2-k\geq t\}\leq \exp\!\left(-\frac{t^2}{32k}\right),\qquad 0\leq t\leq 2k,$$
the usual sub-Gaussian tail for $\chi^2_k$, obtained despite $\nabla f$ being unbounded.
