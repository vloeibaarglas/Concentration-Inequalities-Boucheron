**Central idea.** Theorem 5.20 is the Rademacher version, $\mathbb{E}\|\sum c_j\varepsilon_j\|^2\leq 2(\mathbb{E}\|\sum c_j\varepsilon_j\|)^2$. Approximate each Gaussian $X_i$ by a normalized Rademacher sum $X_i^{(m)}=m^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^m\varepsilon_{ij}$; then $Z^{(m)}=\|\sum_i b_iX_i^{(m)}\|$ is a Rademacher norm to which Theorem 5.20 applies. Uniform boundedness of fourth moments gives uniform integrability, so the inequality passes to the CLT limit $Z^{(m)}\xrightarrow{d}Z$.

**Solution.** Let $\varepsilon_{ij}$, $1\leq i\leq n$, $1\leq j\leq m$, be independent Rademacher variables and put $X_i^{(m)}=m^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^m\varepsilon_{ij}$. By the multivariate CLT, $(X_1^{(m)},\ldots,X_n^{(m)})\xrightarrow{d}(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ with $X_i$ iid $N(0,1)$. With $Z^{(m)}=\|\sum_{i=1}^n b_iX_i^{(m)}\|$ and $Z=\|\sum_{i=1}^n b_iX_i\|$, continuity of the norm gives $Z^{(m)}\xrightarrow{d}Z$.

Now $Z^{(m)}=\|\sum_{i,j}c_{ij}\varepsilon_{ij}\|$ with $c_{ij}=b_i/\sqrt{m}$. Theorem 5.20 applied to these $mn$ vectors yields
$$\mathbb{E}\big[(Z^{(m)})^2\big]\leq 2\big(\mathbb{E}Z^{(m)}\big)^2,\qquad m\geq 1.\tag{*}$$

To pass to the limit, note $\mathbb{E}|X_i^{(m)}|^4=m^{-2}\mathbb{E}[(\sum_{j=1}^m\varepsilon_{ij})^4]=m^{-2}(m+3m(m-1))=3-2/m\leq 3$, since $\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_j^4]=1$, $\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_j^2\varepsilon_k^2]=1$ ($j\neq k$), and all terms with an odd power vanish. Hence, with $( \sum_i a_i)^4\leq n^3\sum_i a_i^4$,
$$\mathbb{E}\big[(Z^{(m)})^4\big]\leq \mathbb{E}\Big[\Big(\sum_{i=1}^n\|b_i\|\,|X_i^{(m)}|\Big)^4\Big]\leq 3n^3\sum_{i=1}^n\|b_i\|^4<\infty$$
uniformly in $m$, so $\{(Z^{(m)})^2\}$ is uniformly integrable. With $Z^{(m)}\xrightarrow{d}Z$ this gives $\mathbb{E}[(Z^{(m)})^2]\to\mathbb{E}[Z^2]$ and $\mathbb{E}Z^{(m)}\to\mathbb{E}Z$. Letting $m\to\infty$ in $(*)$,
$$\mathbb{E}[Z^2]\leq 2(\mathbb{E}Z)^2,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad \sqrt{\mathbb{E}[Z^2]}\leq \sqrt{2}\,\mathbb{E}Z.\qquad\blacksquare$$

*Remark.* The constant $\sqrt{2}$ is not optimal; a sharper argument gives $\sqrt{\pi/2}$ (see Latała–Oleszkiewicz (1999) for the best constants in moment comparison for Gaussian vectors).
