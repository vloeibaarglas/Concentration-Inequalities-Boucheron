**Central idea.** Gross's proof of hypercontractivity via the Gaussian log-Sobolev inequality: for $f\geq 0$ smooth put $g_t=T_{e^{-t}}f$ and $q=q(t)$; differentiating $\Phi(t)=\frac{1}{q}\log\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q}]$ produces $\frac{q'}{q^2}\frac{\mathrm{Ent}(g_t^{\,q})}{\mathbb{E}g_t^{\,q}}+\frac{\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q-1}Lg_t]}{\mathbb{E}g_t^{\,q}}$, and the LSI in the form $\mathrm{Ent}(g^r)\leq -\frac{r^2}{2(r-1)}\mathbb{E}[g^{r-1}Lg]$ (from $\mathrm{Ent}(u^2)\leq 2\mathbb{E}[(u')^2]$ with $u=g^{r/2}$ plus Gaussian integration by parts) makes this $\leq 0$ precisely because $q'(t)=2(q(t)-1)$. Hence $\|T_{e^{-t}}f\|_{q(t)}$ is nonincreasing, and $|T_{e^{-t}}f|\leq T_{e^{-t}}|f|$ extends it to general $f$.

**Solution.** Let $X,X'$ be independent $N(0,1)$. For $|\gamma|\leq 1$, Jensen's inequality gives $(T_\gamma f(y))^2\leq \mathbb{E}[f(\gamma y+\sqrt{1-\gamma^2}X)^2]$, whence with $X$ in place of $y$,
$$\mathbb{E}[(T_\gamma f(X))^2]\leq \mathbb{E}[f(\gamma X+\sqrt{1-\gamma^2}X')^2]=\mathbb{E}[f(X)^2],$$
since $\gamma X+\sqrt{1-\gamma^2}X'\sim N(0,1)$: $T_\gamma$ is an $L^2$-contraction.

It suffices to prove the claim for $f$ smooth and strictly positive: for general $f$ with $\mathbb{E}|f|^p<\infty$, Jensen gives $|T_{e^{-t}}f|\leq T_{e^{-t}}|f|$, so $\|T_{e^{-t}}f\|_{q(t)}\leq \|T_{e^{-t}}|f|\|_{q(t)}$, and $|f|$ is approximated in $L^p$ by smooth strictly positive functions.

Let thus $g\geq 0$ be smooth (with enough decay) and put $g_t=T_{e^{-t}}g$. First,
$$\frac{d}{dt}T_{e^{-t}}g=L\,T_{e^{-t}}g,\qquad Lf=f''-xf'.$$
Indeed, with $\gamma=e^{-t}$, $\sigma=\sqrt{1-\gamma^2}$, $u(t,y)=\mathbb{E}[g(\gamma y+\sigma X)]$,
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\mathbb{E}\big[g'(\gamma y+\sigma X)(-\gamma y+\tfrac{\gamma^2}{\sigma}X)\big],$$
while $Lu=\gamma^2\mathbb{E}[g''(\gamma y+\sigma X)]-\gamma y\,\mathbb{E}[g'(\gamma y+\sigma X)]$; the two agree since Gaussian integration by parts ($\mathbb{E}[X\psi(X)]=\mathbb{E}[\psi'(X)]$) gives $\mathbb{E}[Xg'(\gamma y+\sigma X)]=\sigma\,\mathbb{E}[g''(\gamma y+\sigma X)]$.

Next, the LSI implies, for $r>1$,
$$\mathrm{Ent}(g^r)\leq -\frac{r^2}{2(r-1)}\mathbb{E}\big[g^{\,r-1}Lg\big].\tag{1}$$
Indeed, with $h=\partial_x g^{r/2}=\frac{r}{2}g^{r/2-1}g'$, the Gaussian LSI $\mathrm{Ent}(u^2)\leq 2\mathbb{E}[(u')^2]$ at $u=g^{r/2}$ gives $\mathrm{Ent}(g^r)\leq \frac{r^2}{2}\mathbb{E}[g^{r-2}(g')^2]$. Gaussian integration by parts in the form $\mathbb{E}[\phi''\psi]=\mathbb{E}[X\phi'\psi]-\mathbb{E}[\phi'\psi']$ yields $\mathbb{E}[g^{r-1}g'']=\mathbb{E}[Xg^{r-1}g']-(r-1)\mathbb{E}[g^{r-2}(g')^2]$, so $\mathbb{E}[g^{r-1}Lg]=-(r-1)\mathbb{E}[g^{r-2}(g')^2]$, and (1) follows.

Now let $q=q(t)=1+e^{2t}(p-1)$ and $\Phi(t)=\frac{1}{q}\log\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q}]$, so $e^{\Phi(t)}=\|g_t\|_{q(t)}$. Differentiating (using $\dot g_t=Lg_t$),
\begin{align*}
\Phi'(t)&=-\frac{q'}{q^2}\log\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q}]+\frac{1}{q}\frac{\mathbb{E}[qg_t^{\,q-1}Lg_t+q'g_t^{\,q}\log g_t]}{\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q}]}\\
&=\frac{q'}{q^2}\frac{\mathrm{Ent}(g_t^{\,q})}{\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q}]}+\frac{\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q-1}Lg_t]}{\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q}]},
\end{align*}
since $\frac{q'}{q}\mathbb{E}[g^q\log g]=\frac{q'}{q^2}\mathbb{E}[g^q\log(g^q)]$. Applying (1) with $r=q$,
$$\Phi'(t)\leq \frac{\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q-1}Lg_t]}{\mathbb{E}[g_t^{\,q}]}\left[1-\frac{q'(t)}{2(q(t)-1)}\right].$$
But $q'(t)=2e^{2t}(p-1)=2(q(t)-1)$, so the bracket vanishes and $\Phi'(t)\leq 0$: $t\mapsto \|T_{e^{-t}}g\|_{q(t)}$ is nonincreasing. As $T_1=\mathrm{id}$ and $q(0)=p$,
$$\big(\mathbb{E}|T_{e^{-t}}f(X)|^{q(t)}\big)^{1/q(t)}\leq \big(\mathbb{E}|f(X)|^p\big)^{1/p},\qquad t\geq 0,$$
first for smooth $f\geq 0$, then for general $f$ via $|T_{e^{-t}}f|\leq T_{e^{-t}}|f|$ and approximation. ∎
