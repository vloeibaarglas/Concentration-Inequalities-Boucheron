**Central idea.** Tensorization by induction on $n$, imitating Theorem 5.16: write $T_\gamma=T_\gamma^{(n)}\circ T_\gamma^{(<n)}$ with $T_\gamma^{(n)}$ acting on $x_n$ and $T_\gamma^{(<n)}$ on the first $n-1$ coordinates; apply the induction hypothesis in the first $n-1$ variables, then Minkowski's integral inequality (using $q(t)\geq p$) to swap the integrals, then the one-dimensional Exercise 5.18 in $x_n$.

**Solution.** As in Exercise 5.18 it suffices to treat $f\geq 0$ smooth, since $|T_{e^{-t}}f|\leq T_{e^{-t}}|f|$. Write $T_\gamma=T_\gamma^{(1)}\otimes\cdots\otimes T_\gamma^{(n)}$, where $T_\gamma^{(i)}$ acts on the $i$-th coordinate; these commute. Put $T_\gamma^{(<n)}=T_\gamma^{(1)}\cdots T_\gamma^{(n-1)}$, so $T_\gamma=T_\gamma^{(n)}\circ T_\gamma^{(<n)}$. Let $d\gamma_k$ denote the standard Gaussian law on $\mathbb{R}^k$ and $q=q(t)\geq p$.

We induct on $n$; $n=1$ is Exercise 5.18. Assume the claim for $n-1$. For fixed $x_n$, the induction hypothesis applied to $g(\cdot)=(T_\gamma^{(n)}f)(\cdot,x_n)$ gives
$$\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}\big|T_\gamma^{(<n)}g\big|^q\,d\gamma_{n-1}\leq \left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}|g|^p\,d\gamma_{n-1}\right)^{q/p}.$$
Hence, with $h=T_\gamma^{(n)}f$,
$$\|T_\gamma f\|_{L^q(\gamma_n)}^q\leq \int_{\mathbb{R}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}|h(x_{<n},x_n)|^p\,d\gamma_{n-1}(x_{<n})\right)^{q/p}d\gamma_1(x_n).\tag{*}$$
Since $q/p\geq 1$, Minkowski's integral inequality yields
$$\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}|h|^p\,d\gamma_{n-1}\right)^{q/p}d\gamma_1\leq \left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}}|h|^q\,d\gamma_1\right)^{p/q}d\gamma_{n-1}\right)^{q/p}.$$
For each fixed $x_{<n}$, Exercise 5.18 applied in the $x_n$ variable gives $\|h(x_{<n},\cdot)\|_{L^q(\gamma_1)}=\|T_\gamma^{(n)}f(x_{<n},\cdot)\|_{L^q(\gamma_1)}\leq \|f(x_{<n},\cdot)\|_{L^p(\gamma_1)}$. Therefore the right-hand side of $(*)$ is at most
$$\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}\|f(x_{<n},\cdot)\|_{L^p(\gamma_1)}^p\,d\gamma_{n-1}(x_{<n})\right)^{q/p}=\|f\|_{L^p(\gamma_n)}^q,$$
i.e.
$$\left(\mathbb{E}\big|T_{e^{-t}}f(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\big|^{q(t)}\right)^{1/q(t)}\leq \left(\mathbb{E}\big|f(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\big|^p\right)^{1/p},\qquad t\geq 0.\qquad\blacksquare$$
