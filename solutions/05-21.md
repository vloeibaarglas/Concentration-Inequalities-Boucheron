**Central idea.** The functions $f_\lambda$ are eigenfunctions of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck semigroup: $T_\gamma f_\lambda = f_{\lambda\gamma}$. Their $L^p(\gamma)$ norms are explicit Gaussians, so the operator norm ratio $\|T_{e^{-t}}f_\lambda\|_q/\|f_\lambda\|_2$ is $\exp(\lambda^2[(q-1)e^{-2t}-1]/2)$, which blows up as $\lambda\to\infty$ exactly when $q > 1+e^{2t}$ — the threshold from Exercise 5.18 with $p=2$.

**Solution.** Recall $T_\gamma f(y) = \mathbb{E}[f(\gamma y + \sqrt{1-\gamma^2}X)]$ with $X$ standard Gaussian. For $f_\lambda(x) = e^{\lambda x - \lambda^2/2}$,
\begin{align*}
T_\gamma f_\lambda(y) &= \mathbb{E}\big[e^{\lambda(\gamma y + \sqrt{1-\gamma^2}X) - \lambda^2/2}\big] \\
&= e^{\lambda\gamma y - \lambda^2/2}\,\mathbb{E}\big[e^{\lambda\sqrt{1-\gamma^2}X}\big] \\
&= e^{\lambda\gamma y - \lambda^2/2}\,e^{\lambda^2(1-\gamma^2)/2}
= e^{\lambda\gamma y - \lambda^2\gamma^2/2} = f_{\lambda\gamma}(y).
\end{align*}
Since $f_\lambda > 0$, for $p > 1$,
$$\mathbb{E}[|f_\lambda(X)|^p] = \mathbb{E}\big[e^{p\lambda X - p\lambda^2/2}\big] = e^{p^2\lambda^2/2 - p\lambda^2/2} = e^{p(p-1)\lambda^2/2},$$
using $\mathbb{E}[e^{aX}] = e^{a^2/2}$. Hence $\mathbb{E}[|f_\lambda(X)|^p]^{1/p} = e^{(p-1)\lambda^2/2} < \infty$, so each $f_\lambda$ is admissible.

Take $f = f_\lambda$ with $\lambda \ge 0$ and $\gamma = e^{-t}$. Then $T_{e^{-t}}f_\lambda = f_{\lambda e^{-t}}$, and
$$\frac{\mathbb{E}[|T_{e^{-t}}f_\lambda(X)|^q]^{1/q}}{\mathbb{E}[|f_\lambda(X)|^2]^{1/2}} = \frac{e^{(q-1)(\lambda e^{-t})^2/2}}{e^{\lambda^2/2}} = \exp\!\left(\frac{\lambda^2}{2}\big[(q-1)e^{-2t} - 1\big]\right).$$
If $q > 1 + e^{2t}$, then $(q-1)e^{-2t} - 1 > 0$, and the right-hand side tends to $+\infty$ as $\lambda \to \infty$. Therefore
$$\sup_{f:\,\mathbb{E}[|f(X)|^2]<\infty} \frac{\mathbb{E}[|T_{e^{-t}}f(X)|^q]^{1/q}}{\mathbb{E}[|f(X)|^2]^{1/2}} = \infty \qquad\text{whenever } q > 1+e^{2t}.$$
Exercise 5.18 with $p=2$ gives $q(t) = 1+e^{2t}$ with operator norm at most $1$; the computation above shows no larger exponent works. Hence the hypercontractive bounds of Exercises 5.18 and 5.19 are tight. ∎
