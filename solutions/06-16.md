**Central idea.** Example 6.30 gives a sub-gamma tail for $Y = \sqrt Z$: $\mathbb{P}\{Y \ge \mathbb{E}Y+t\} \le \exp(-t^2/(24(np^2+\log n)+20t))$. Write $\{Z > \mathbb{E}Z+t\} = \{\sqrt Z > \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}+s\}$ with $s = t/(\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z+t}+\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z})$; Jensen's inequality ($\mathbb{E}\sqrt Z \le \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}$) lets us apply the $Y$-bound at deviation $s$. The hypothesis $t \le (K^2-1)\mathbb{E}Z$ caps the denominator $D = \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z+t}+\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z} \le (K+1)\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}$, and rewriting $s^2/(A+20s) = t^2/(AD^2+20tD)$ yields the claimed Bernstein-type bound for $Z$.

**Solution.** Example 6.30 shows that $Y = \sqrt Z$ satisfies, with $A := 24(np^2+\log n)$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{Y \ge \mathbb{E}Y+s\} \le \exp\!\left(-\frac{s^2}{A+20s}\right) \qquad\forall\,s > 0,$$
since $4\mathbb{E}W \le 24(np^2+\log n)$ where $W = 3\max_j B_j$. Let $K > 1$ and $0 < t \le (K^2-1)\mathbb{E}Z$ (for $t \le 0$ there is nothing to prove). Then
$$\{Z > \mathbb{E}Z+t\} = \{\sqrt Z > \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z+t}\} = \{\sqrt Z > \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}+s\},$$
where $s := \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z+t}-\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z} = \frac{t}{\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z+t}+\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}} > 0$. By Jensen's inequality $\mathbb{E}\sqrt Z \le \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}$, so $\{\sqrt Z > \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}+s\} \subseteq \{Y \ge \mathbb{E}Y+s\}$, and
$$\mathbb{P}\{Z > \mathbb{E}Z+t\} \le \exp\!\left(-\frac{s^2}{A+20s}\right).$$
Put $D := \sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z+t}+\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}$, so $s = t/D$ and
$$\frac{s^2}{A+20s} = \frac{t^2/D^2}{A+20t/D} = \frac{t^2}{AD^2+20tD}.$$
From $t \le (K^2-1)\mathbb{E}Z$ we get $\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z+t} \le K\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}$, hence $D \le (K+1)\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}$. Therefore
\begin{align*}
AD^2+20tD &\le A(K+1)^2\mathbb{E}Z + 20t(K+1)\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}\\
&= (K+1)^2\mathbb{E}Z\left(A+\frac{20t}{(K+1)\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}}\right),
\end{align*}
and consequently
$$\mathbb{P}\{Z > \mathbb{E}Z+t\} \le \exp\!\left(-\frac{t^2}{(K+1)^2\mathbb{E}Z\left(24np^2+24\log n+\frac{20t}{(K+1)\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z}}\right)}\right).\ \blacksquare$$
