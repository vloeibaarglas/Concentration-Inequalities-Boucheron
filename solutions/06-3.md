**Central idea.** Since $\mathrm{rank}(A-B)=k$, a unitary congruence kills the last $n-k$ rows and columns of $A-B$, so $A,B$ share their $(n-k)\times(n-k)$ bottom-right block $A_{22}$. Cauchy interlacing (Exercise 6.2) sandwiches the eigenvalue counting functions of $A$ and $B$ around that of $A_{22}$, each within $k$; hence $F_A$ and $F_B$ differ by at most $k/n$ uniformly.

**Solution.** $L_A=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_{\alpha_i}$, $L_B=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_{\beta_i}$ with $\alpha_1\leq\cdots\leq\alpha_n$, $\beta_1\leq\cdots\leq\beta_n$ the eigenvalues; $F_A(t)=\frac1n\#\{i:\alpha_i\leq t\}$, similarly $F_B$.

*Reduction.* Let $k=\mathrm{rank}(A-B)$. Choose unitary $U$ with $U^*(A-B)U=\begin{bmatrix}D&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$, $D$ $k\times k$. Eigenvalues are unitarily invariant, so replace $(A,B)$ by $(U^*AU,U^*BU)$; then with matching block sizes
$$A=\begin{bmatrix}A_{11}&A_{12}\\A_{21}&A_{22}\end{bmatrix},\qquad B=\begin{bmatrix}B_{11}&A_{12}\\A_{21}&A_{22}\end{bmatrix},$$
$A_{22}$ Hermitian $(n-k)\times(n-k)$, since $A-B$ vanishes outside the top-left $k\times k$ block.

*Interlacing.* Let $\gamma_1\leq\cdots\leq\gamma_{\,n-k}$ be the eigenvalues of $A_{22}=PAP$ ($P$ the coordinate projection of rank $n-k$). By Exercise 6.2 with $m=n-k$,
$$\alpha_j\leq\gamma_j\leq\alpha_{j+k},\qquad \beta_j\leq\gamma_j\leq\beta_{j+k},\qquad j=1,\ldots,n-k.$$

*Counting functions.* Put $N_A(t)=\#\{i:\alpha_i\leq t\}=nF_A(t)$, $N_B$, $N_\gamma$ analogously. From $\alpha_j\leq\gamma_j$: $\gamma_j\leq t\Rightarrow\alpha_j\leq t$, so $N_\gamma(t)\leq N_A(t)$. From $\gamma_j\leq\alpha_{j+k}$: $\alpha_{j+k}\leq t\Rightarrow\gamma_j\leq t$, so
$$N_\gamma(t)\geq\#\{j\leq n-k:\alpha_{j+k}\leq t\}=\#\{i\geq k+1:\alpha_i\leq t\}\geq N_A(t)-k.$$
Thus $N_A(t)-k\leq N_\gamma(t)\leq N_A(t)$, and likewise $N_B(t)-k\leq N_\gamma(t)\leq N_B(t)$. Hence
$$N_A(t)\leq N_\gamma(t)+k\leq N_B(t)+k,\qquad N_B(t)\leq N_A(t)+k,$$
so $|N_A(t)-N_B(t)|\leq k$ for all $t$. Dividing by $n$,
$$\|F_A-F_B\|_\infty\leq\frac{k}{n}.\ \blacksquare$$
