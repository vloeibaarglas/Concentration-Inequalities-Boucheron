**Central idea.** For $Z=\max_{M\in\mathcal{T}}\sum_{i,j}M_{i,j}\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j$, flipping $\varepsilon_k\to-\varepsilon_k$ changes the maximizing quadratic form by $4\varepsilon_k\sum_jM^*_{k,j}\varepsilon_j$ (symmetry, zero diagonal), so the Efron–Stein quantity $V^+_Z$ is at most $8Y^2$, giving $\mathrm{Var}(Z)\le8\mathbb{E}Y^2$. Writing $Y=\max_{M,\|\alpha\|_2\le1}\sum_{i,j}\alpha_iM_{i,j}\varepsilon_j$ as the supremum of a Rademacher process, the same flip applied to $W=Y^2$ gives $\sum_k(W-W^{(k)})_+^2\le16BW$ via $\|M^*\alpha^*\|\le\sqrt B$ — i.e. $W$ is $(16B,0)$-weakly self-bounding. Theorem 6.16, through the differential inequality $\lambda G'-G\le\frac{\lambda\theta}{1-\lambda\theta}\rho(\lambda/\theta)$ established in its proof, converts $V^+_W\le8BW$ into the Bernstein mgf bound for $W$. For $Z$, the sharp entropy inequality of Exercise 6.1 plus entropy duality gives $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}\le\frac{\lambda\theta}{2-\lambda\theta}\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda V^+_Z/\theta}$; with $V^+_Z\le8W$ and the choice $\theta=8\sqrt B$ this yields the corrected (scale-invariant) fourth inequality — the printed one is false (see Erratum).

**Erratum.** The first three displays of the statement are correct, but the fourth, $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}\le\frac{16\lambda^2}{2(1-64B\lambda)}\mathbb{E}[Y^2]$, is false as printed (it is not scale invariant: $B\lambda$ scales like $M$, so replacing $\mathcal{T}$ by $c\mathcal{T}$, $c\to0$, would make $Z$ sub-Gaussian at every $\lambda$). Counterexample: $\mathcal{T}=\{c(J-I)\}$ with $n=1000$, $c=10^{-9}$, so $Z=c((\sum_i\varepsilon_i)^2-n)$, $\mathbb{E}Z=0$, $B=c^2(n-1)^2$, $\mathbb{E}[Y^2]=c^2n(n-1)$; at $\lambda=0.6/(cn)$ one has $64B\lambda\approx3.8\times10^{-5}<1$, the exact left side (binomial sum) is $24.77$, and the printed right side is $2.88$. The printed form arises from dropping the term $G(\lambda/8B)$ below, which Jensen's inequality does not allow. Corrected statement (proved below): for $0\le\lambda<1/(4\sqrt B)$,
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}\le\frac{4\lambda^2\,\mathbb{E}[Y^2]}{(1-4\sqrt B\lambda)^2},\qquad\text{hence}\qquad\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}\le\frac{8\lambda^2}{2(1-8\sqrt B\lambda)}\,\mathbb{E}[Y^2]\quad(0\le\lambda<1/(8\sqrt B)),$$
i.e. the scale parameter is $8\sqrt B=8\max_M\|M\|$, not $64B$. (The Theorem 6.16 route of the hint gives instead $\frac{8\lambda^2}{(1-64B\lambda)(1-\lambda)}\mathbb{E}[Y^2]$ for $64B\lambda<1$ and $\lambda<1$; this is also valid but its range $\lambda<1$ is not scale invariant — see the final remark.)

**Solution.** Write $\varepsilon^{(k)}$ for $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon_k$ flipped, and $Z^{(k)},W^{(k)}$ for the corresponding values. For Rademacher variables an independent copy $\varepsilon'_k$ equals $\varepsilon_k$ or $-\varepsilon_k$ with probability $1/2$ each, so with $V^+=\sum_k\mathbb{E}'[(Z-Z'_k)_+^2]$,
$$V^+=\tfrac12\sum_k(Z-Z^{(k)})_+^2,$$
and similarly for $W$.

Let $M^*$ attain the maximum in $Z$. Since $Z^{(k)}\ge\sum_{i,j}M^*_{i,j}\varepsilon^{(k)}_i\varepsilon^{(k)}_j$,
$$Z-Z^{(k)}\le\sum_{i,j}M^*_{i,j}\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j-\sum_{i,j}M^*_{i,j}\varepsilon^{(k)}_i\varepsilon^{(k)}_j.$$
Only terms with $i=k$ or $j=k$ survive (the diagonal vanishes); using $M^*_{i,k}=M^*_{k,i}$,
$$Z-Z^{(k)}\le2\varepsilon_k\Bigl(\sum_{j\ne k}M^*_{k,j}\varepsilon_j+\sum_{i\ne k}M^*_{i,k}\varepsilon_i\Bigr)=4\varepsilon_k\sum_jM^*_{k,j}\varepsilon_j.$$
Hence $(Z-Z^{(k)})_+\le4\bigl|\sum_jM^*_{k,j}\varepsilon_j\bigr|$ and
$$\sum_k(Z-Z^{(k)})_+^2\le16\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_jM^*_{k,j}\varepsilon_j\Bigr)^2\le16\max_{M}\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_jM_{k,j}\varepsilon_j\Bigr)^2=16Y^2.$$
Therefore $V^+_Z\le8Y^2$ — i.e. $8Y^2$ upper-bounds the Efron–Stein estimate — and by Efron–Stein,
$$\mathrm{Var}(Z)\le\mathbb{E}V^+_Z\le8\mathbb{E}[Y^2].$$

For fixed $M$, $(\sum_i(\sum_j\varepsilon_jM_{i,j})^2)^{1/2}=\|M\varepsilon\|_2=\max_{\|\alpha\|_2\le1}\sum_{i,j}\alpha_iM_{i,j}\varepsilon_j$, so with $\mathcal{T}$ finite,
$$Y=\max_{M\in\mathcal{T},\,\|\alpha\|_2\le1}\sum_{i,j}\alpha_iM_{i,j}\varepsilon_j,$$
a supremum of a Rademacher process. Let $W=Y^2$ and let $(M^*,\alpha^*)$ attain the maximum; put $S=\sum_{i,j}\alpha^*_iM^*_{i,j}\varepsilon_j$ so $W=S^2$. Since $(M^*,\alpha^*)$ is feasible for the flipped vector, $W^{(k)}\ge(S^{(k)})^2$ where $S^{(k)}=S-2t_k\varepsilon_k$ with $t_k=\sum_i\alpha^*_iM^*_{i,k}$. Thus
$$W-W^{(k)}\le S^2-(S^{(k)})^2=4t_k\varepsilon_kS-4t_k^2\le4|t_k|\,|S|=4|t_k|Y,$$
so $(W-W^{(k)})_+^2\le16Wt_k^2$. Summing and using $\sum_kt_k^2=\|M^*\alpha^*\|_2^2\le\|M^*\|^2\|\alpha^*\|_2^2\le B$,
$$\sum_k(W-W^{(k)})_+^2\le16BW,$$
i.e. $W=Y^2$ is $(16B,0)$-weakly self-bounding. Consequently $V^+_W\le8BW$ and
$$\mathrm{Var}(Y^2)\le\mathbb{E}V^+_W\le8B\,\mathbb{E}[Y^2].$$

For the mgf bounds we use the differential inequality from the proof of Theorem 6.16: with $G(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(W-\mathbb{E}W)}$ and $\rho(u)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{uV^+}$,
$$\lambda G'(\lambda)-G(\lambda)\le\frac{\lambda\theta}{1-\lambda\theta}\,\rho(\lambda/\theta),\qquad\lambda\theta<1.$$
Since $V^+_W\le8BW$, $\rho(u)\le\log\mathbb{E}e^{8BuW}=G(8Bu)+8Bu\,\mathbb{E}W$. Taking $\theta=8B$ gives $\rho(\lambda/(8B))\le G(\lambda)+\lambda M$ with $M=\mathbb{E}W$, whence
$$\lambda G'-G\le\frac{8B\lambda}{1-8B\lambda}(G+\lambda M).$$
With integrating factor $(1-8B\lambda)/\lambda$ (since $\int\frac{d\lambda}{\lambda(1-8B\lambda)}=\log\frac{\lambda}{1-8B\lambda}$) and $G(u)=O(u^2)$ as $u\to0$,
$$\frac{d}{d\lambda}\Bigl(G(\lambda)\frac{1-8B\lambda}{\lambda}\Bigr)\le8BM,\qquad\text{so}\qquad G(\lambda)\le\frac{8B\lambda^2}{1-8B\lambda}M,$$
i.e.
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Y^2-\mathbb{E}Y^2)}\le\frac{\lambda^2}{1-8B\lambda}\,8B\,\mathbb{E}[Y^2],\qquad 8B\lambda<1.$$

The identity follows from $\sum_i(\sum_j\varepsilon_jM_{i,j})^2=\sum_{j,k}\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\sum_iM_{i,j}M_{i,k}$ and $\sum_iM_{i,j}M_{i,k}=(M^TM)_{j,k}=(M^2)_{j,k}$ by symmetry, so
$$\mathbb{E}[Y^2]=\mathbb{E}\Bigl[\sup_{M\in\mathcal{T}}\sum_{i,j}\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j(M^2)_{i,j}\Bigr].$$

Finally, the fourth inequality, in the corrected form of the Erratum. Let $H(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}$, $F_Z(\lambda)=\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}$, and $\rho_Z(u)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{uV^+_Z}$ (all finite: $Z$ is bounded). By Exercise 6.1 (its tensorized entropy inequality, valid for bounded $Z$), for $\lambda\ge0$,
$$\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})\le\frac{\lambda^2}{2}\,\mathbb{E}\bigl[e^{\lambda Z}V^+_Z\bigr].$$
By the duality formula for entropy (Corollary 4.15), $\mathbb{E}[UY]\le\mathrm{Ent}(Y)+\mathbb{E}Y\log\mathbb{E}e^U$ for $Y>0$; with $Y=e^{\lambda Z}$, $U=\lambda V^+_Z/\theta$ ($\theta>0$),
$$\mathbb{E}\bigl[V^+_Ze^{\lambda Z}\bigr]\le\frac{\theta}{\lambda}\Bigl[\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})+F_Z(\lambda)\,\rho_Z(\lambda/\theta)\Bigr].$$
Since $\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})=F_Z(\lambda)(\lambda H'(\lambda)-H(\lambda))$, combining the two displays gives $(1-\lambda\theta/2)(\lambda H'-H)\le\frac{\lambda\theta}{2}\rho_Z(\lambda/\theta)$, i.e.
$$\frac{d}{d\lambda}\Bigl(\frac{H(\lambda)}{\lambda}\Bigr)=\frac{\lambda H'-H}{\lambda^2}\le\frac{\theta}{2-\lambda\theta}\cdot\frac{\rho_Z(\lambda/\theta)}{\lambda},\qquad0<\lambda<2/\theta.$$
Fix $\theta$ and $\lambda_1<2/\theta$. Since $\rho_Z$ is convex with $\rho_Z(0)=0$, $u\mapsto\rho_Z(u)/u$ is nondecreasing, and $s\mapsto\theta/(2-s\theta)$ is increasing; so on $(0,\lambda_1]$ the right side is at most its value at $\lambda_1$. Integrating from $0$ (where $H(s)/s\to0$) to $\lambda_1$,
$$H(\lambda_1)\le\frac{\lambda_1\theta}{2-\lambda_1\theta}\,\rho_Z(\lambda_1/\theta).$$
Now $V^+_Z\le8W$ and, by the bound $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\mu W}\le\mu M+G(\mu)$ with the third inequality in its sharper intermediate form (obtained the same way from the entropy inequality of Exercise 6.1 and $V^+_W\le8BW$: $\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\mu W})\le4B\mu^2\mathbb{E}[We^{\mu W}]$, i.e. $\mu(1-4B\mu)L'\le L$ for $L=\log\mathbb{E}e^{\mu W}$, which integrates to $L(\mu)\le\mu M/(1-4B\mu)$ for $4B\mu<1$, using $L(\mu)/\mu\to M$ as $\mu\to0$),
$$\rho_Z(\lambda/\theta)\le\log\mathbb{E}e^{8\lambda W/\theta}\le\frac{8\lambda M/\theta}{1-32B\lambda/\theta}.$$
Hence, for $\lambda\theta<2$ and $32B\lambda<\theta$,
$$H(\lambda)\le\frac{8\lambda^2M}{(2-\lambda\theta)(1-32B\lambda/\theta)}.$$
Take $\theta=8\sqrt B$: both conditions become $\lambda<1/(4\sqrt B)$, and $(2-8\sqrt B\lambda)(1-4\sqrt B\lambda)=2(1-4\sqrt B\lambda)^2$, so
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}\le\frac{4\lambda^2\,\mathbb{E}[Y^2]}{(1-4\sqrt B\lambda)^2}\le\frac{8\lambda^2}{2(1-8\sqrt B\lambda)}\,\mathbb{E}[Y^2],$$
the first for $0\le\lambda<1/(4\sqrt B)$, the second for $0\le\lambda<1/(8\sqrt B)$ (using $(1-x)^2\ge1-2x$). Numerical check on $\mathcal{T}=\{J-I\}$ ($n=10,100,1000$, exact binomial sums, $\lambda=\theta'/(4\sqrt B)$ for $\theta'\in\{0.1,\dots,0.99\}$): the ratio left/right of the first bound is at most $0.21$.

**Remark (the Theorem 6.16 route and the printed fourth inequality).** Applying Theorem 6.16 directly with $\theta=64B$ and $V^+_Z\le8W$ gives
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda V^+_Z/(64B)}\le\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda W/(8B)}=\frac{\lambda}{8B}M+G\!\left(\frac{\lambda}{8B}\right)\le\frac{\lambda}{8B}M+\frac{\lambda^2/(8B)}{1-\lambda}M=\frac{\lambda}{8B(1-\lambda)}M,$$
by the third inequality, whence
$$H(\lambda)\le\frac{64B\lambda}{1-64B\lambda}\cdot\frac{\lambda}{8B(1-\lambda)}M=\frac{8\lambda^2}{(1-64B\lambda)(1-\lambda)}\,\mathbb{E}[Y^2],\qquad64B\lambda<1,\ \lambda<1.$$
This is valid, but only for $\lambda<1$ in addition to $64B\lambda<1$, a restriction that is not scale invariant (for $c\mathcal{T}$ with $c\to0$ the natural range of $\lambda$, of order $1/(c\max_M\|M\|)$, grows like $1/c$, while $\lambda<1$ stays fixed). The printed $\frac{16\lambda^2}{2(1-64B\lambda)}\mathbb{E}[Y^2]$ would require $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda V^+_Z/(64B)}\le\frac{\lambda}{8B}\mathbb{E}[Y^2]$, i.e. dropping $G(\lambda/(8B))\ge0$ (Jensen gives $\log\mathbb{E}e^X\ge\mathbb{E}X$), and the counterexample in the Erratum shows the printed inequality is in fact false. ∎
