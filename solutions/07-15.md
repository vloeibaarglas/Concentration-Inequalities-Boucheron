**Central idea.** If $f(x)=k$ then $k$ coordinates certify it: by heredity of $\Pi$, any $z$ agreeing with $x$ on more than $a$ of those coordinates has $f(z)>a$, so every $z\in A_a$ differs from $x$ on at least $k-a$ certificate coordinates. Taking $\alpha$ uniform on the certificate gives $d_T(x,A_a)\ge (f(x)-a)/\sqrt{f(x)}$; since $t\mapsto(t-a)/\sqrt{t}$ is increasing, large values of $f$ force large convex distance, and Talagrand's convex distance inequality (Theorem 7.9) turns this into the two-sided tail bounds about a median.

**Solution.** Recall $d_T(x,A)=\sup_{\|\alpha\|_2=1,\,\alpha\ge0}\inf_{y\in A}\sum_j\alpha_j\mathbf{1}_{\{x_j\ne y_j\}}$ and Theorem 7.9: $\mathbb{P}\{A\}\,\mathbb{P}\{d_T(X,A)\ge t\}\le e^{-t^2/4}$ for product $\mathbb{P}$. A median $MZ$ satisfies $\mathbb{P}\{Z\le MZ\}\ge1/2$ and $\mathbb{P}\{Z\ge MZ\}\ge1/2$.

If $f(x)=k$, there are $i_1,\dots,i_k$ with $(x_{i_1},\dots,x_{i_k})$ satisfying $\Pi_k$ (Section 3.3); by heredity, any $z$ agreeing with $x$ on $r$ of these coordinates has $f(z)\ge r$.

*First inequality.* Let $k=f(x)$. If $k\le a$ there is nothing to prove. If $k>a$, take $\alpha_j=1/\sqrt{k}$ on the certificate coordinates and $0$ elsewhere, so $\|\alpha\|_2=1$. For $z\in A_a=\{f\le a\}$: $z$ agrees with $x$ on at most $a$ certificate coordinates (otherwise $f(z)>a$ by heredity), hence differs on at least $k-a$ of them, and
$$d_\alpha(x,z)=\sum_j\alpha_j\mathbf{1}_{\{x_j\ne z_j\}}\ge\frac{k-a}{\sqrt{k}}.$$
Thus $d_T(x,A_a)\ge\inf_{z\in A_a}d_\alpha(x,z)\ge(k-a)/\sqrt{k}$, i.e. $f(x)\le a+\sqrt{f(x)}\,d_T(x,A_a)$.

*Upper tail.* Let $m=MZ$ and $A=A_m$, so $\mathbb{P}\{A\}\ge1/2$. On $\{f(X)\ge m+t\}$, the first part gives $d_T(X,A)\ge\frac{f(X)-m}{\sqrt{f(X)}}=g(f(X))$ with $g(s)=(s-m)/\sqrt{s}$, which is increasing for $s\ge m$ since $g'(s)=(s+m)/(2s^{3/2})>0$ (the hint with $a=m$). Hence $d_T(X,A)\ge g(m+t)=t/\sqrt{m+t}$, and by Theorem 7.9,
$$\mathbb{P}\{Z\ge m+t\}\le\mathbb{P}\{d_T(X,A)\ge t/\sqrt{m+t}\}\le\frac{e^{-t^2/(4(m+t))}}{\mathbb{P}\{A\}}\le2e^{-\frac{t^2}{4(m+t)}}.$$

*Lower tail.* Let $A=\{f\le m-t\}$ (if empty, $\mathbb{P}\{A\}=0$ and there is nothing to prove). For $y$ with $f(y)\ge m$, let $J$ be a certificate with $|J|=\ell\ge m$ and take $\alpha_j=1/\sqrt{\ell}$ on $J$, $0$ elsewhere. For $x\in A$: $x$ agrees with $y$ on at most $f(x)\le m-t$ coordinates of $J$ by heredity, so differs on at least $\ell-m+t$ of them, giving $d_\alpha(y,x)\ge(\ell-m+t)/\sqrt{\ell}=h(\ell)$ with $h(s)=(s-m+t)/\sqrt{s}$. For $s\ge m$, $h'(s)=(s+m-t)/(2s^{3/2})>0$ (in the nontrivial case $t\le m$, else $\{Z\le m-t\}=\varnothing$ as $Z\ge0$), so $h(\ell)\ge h(m)=t/\sqrt{m}$. Thus $\{f(X)\ge m\}\subseteq\{d_T(X,A)\ge t/\sqrt{m}\}$, and with $\mathbb{P}\{Z\ge m\}\ge1/2$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{Z\le m-t\}=\mathbb{P}\{A\}\le\frac{e^{-(t^2/m)/4}}{\mathbb{P}\{f(X)\ge m\}}\le2e^{-\frac{t^2}{4m}}.$$
If $m=0$, $\{Z\le -t\}=\varnothing$ for $t>0$ since $Z\ge0$, so the lower bound holds trivially. ∎
