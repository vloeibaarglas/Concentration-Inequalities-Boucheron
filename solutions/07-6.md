**Central idea.** The hypothesis says $f$ is $1$-Lipschitz for the $\ell^1$-product metric $d(x,y)=\sum_i d_i(x_i,y_i)$. By Exercise 7.5 the Laplace functional tensorizes, $L(\lambda)\le\prod_i L_i(\lambda)$, and by Exercise 7.4 each factor satisfies $L_i(\lambda)\le e^{D_i^2\lambda^2/8}$, so $L(\lambda)\le e^{\lambda^2 V/8}$ with $V=\sum_i D_i^2$. A Chernoff bound (the mechanism behind Exercise 7.3) applied to $\pm(f-\mathbb{E}f)$ then gives sub-Gaussian tails; optimizing over $\lambda$ yields $2e^{-2t^2/V}$, which implies the claimed bound.

**Solution.** The condition $|f(x)-f(y)|\le\sum_{i=1}^n d_i(x_i,y_i)$ says exactly that $f$ is $1$-Lipschitz for the product metric $d(x,y)=\sum_i d_i(x_i,y_i)$ of Exercise 7.5. Replacing $f$ by $f-\mathbb{E}f$ (which preserves the Lipschitz condition), assume $\mathbb{E}f(X)=0$.

Let $L(\lambda)$ be the Laplace functional of $(\mathcal{X},d,\mathbb{P})$ and $L_i(\lambda)$ that of $(\mathcal{X}_i,d_i,\mathbb{P}_i)$. By Exercise 7.5, $L(\lambda)\le\prod_{i=1}^n L_i(\lambda)$, and by Exercise 7.4, $L_i(\lambda)\le\exp(D_i^2\lambda^2/8)$ since $\mathrm{diam}(\mathcal{X}_i)=D_i$. Hence, with $V=\sum_{i=1}^n D_i^2$,
$$L(\lambda)\le\exp\!\left(\frac{\lambda^2 V}{8}\right),\qquad \lambda>0.$$

For $\lambda>0$, Chernoff's bound gives (this is the estimate underlying Exercise 7.3)
$$\mathbb{P}\{f(X)>t\}=\mathbb{P}\{e^{\lambda f(X)}>e^{\lambda t}\}\le e^{-\lambda t}\,\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda f(X)}\le e^{-\lambda t}L(\lambda)\le\exp\!\left(-\lambda t+\frac{\lambda^2V}{8}\right),$$
using that $f$ is $1$-Lipschitz with $\mathbb{E}f(X)=0$. Minimizing $-\lambda t+\lambda^2V/8$ over $\lambda>0$ (minimizer $\lambda=4t/V$) yields
$$\mathbb{P}\{f(X)-\mathbb{E}f(X)>t\}\le e^{-2t^2/V}.$$
Applying the same to $-f$ (also $1$-Lipschitz with mean zero) and using $\mathbb{P}\{|f-\mathbb{E}f|>t\}\le\mathbb{P}\{f-\mathbb{E}f>t\}+\mathbb{P}\{-(f-\mathbb{E}f)>t\}$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{|f(X_1,\ldots,X_n)-\mathbb{E}f(X_1,\ldots,X_n)|>t\}\le2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2t^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n D_i^2}\right)\le2\exp\!\left(-\frac{t^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n D_i^2}\right),$$
since $e^{-2t^2/V}\le e^{-t^2/V}$. ∎
