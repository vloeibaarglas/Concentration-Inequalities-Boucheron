**Central idea.** The layer-cake identity $|x-y|=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|\mathbf{1}_{\{x>t\}}-\mathbf{1}_{\{y>t\}}|\,dt$ turns $\mathbb{E}|X-Y|$ into an integral over $t$ of $\mathbb{P}\{X>t\}+\mathbb{P}\{Y>t\}-2\mathbb{P}\{X>t,Y>t\}$; the marginals fix the first two terms, so minimizing amounts to maximizing $\mathbb{P}\{X>t,Y>t\}$ pointwise, which the Fréchet–Hoechding upper bound $\min(\mathbb{P}\{X>t\},\mathbb{P}\{Y>t\})$ caps — and the comonotonic coupling $X=F^{-1}(U)$, $Y=G^{-1}(U)$ attains it for every $t$ simultaneously. (Note: the hint's formula has a sign error; the correct identity is $|X-Y|=2\max(X,Y)-X-Y$, or equivalently $|X-Y|=X+Y-2\min(X,Y)$.)

**Solution.** For real numbers $x,y$,
$$|x-y|=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\big|\mathbf{1}_{\{x>t\}}-\mathbf{1}_{\{y>t\}}\big|\,dt,$$
since the integrand equals $1$ exactly when $t$ lies strictly between $x$ and $y$ (an interval of length $|x-y|$), and $0$ otherwise. By Tonelli's theorem (nonnegative integrand, no integrability needed),
$$\mathbb{E}|X-Y|=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\mathbb{E}\big|\mathbf{1}_{\{X>t\}}-\mathbf{1}_{\{Y>t\}}\big|\,dt.$$
For each fixed $t$,
$$\mathbb{E}\big|\mathbf{1}_{\{X>t\}}-\mathbf{1}_{\{Y>t\}}\big|=\mathbb{P}\{X>t\}+\mathbb{P}\{Y>t\}-2\,\mathbb{P}\{X>t,\,Y>t\}.$$
The marginal constraints $X\sim F$, $Y\sim G$ fix $\mathbb{P}\{X>t\}=1-F(t)$ and $\mathbb{P}\{Y>t\}=1-G(t)$. Hence, for each $t$, minimizing the integrand over all couplings is equivalent to maximizing $\mathbb{P}\{X>t,Y>t\}$. By the Fréchet–Hoeffding bound,
$$\mathbb{P}\{X>t,\,Y>t\}\le\min\big(\mathbb{P}\{X>t\},\mathbb{P}\{Y>t\}\big)=\min\big(1-F(t),\,1-G(t)\big).$$
The comonotonic coupling attains this bound for every $t$ simultaneously: with $U$ uniform on $[0,1]$, $X=F^{-1}(U)$, $Y=G^{-1}(U)$, and using $F^{-1}(s)>x\iff s>F(x)$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{F^{-1}(U)>t,\,G^{-1}(U)>t\}=\mathbb{P}\{U>\max(F(t),G(t))\}=1-\max(F(t),G(t)),$$
which equals $\min(1-F(t),1-G(t))$. Therefore the comonotonic coupling minimizes $\mathbb{E}|X-Y|$ over all couplings with marginals $F,G$ (the argument is valid even when $\mathbb{E}|X|=\infty$, with both sides possibly $+\infty$).

The minimal value is
$$\mathbb{E}\big|F^{-1}(U)-G^{-1}(U)\big|=\int_0^1\big|F^{-1}(t)-G^{-1}(t)\big|\,dt,$$
by the law of the unconscious statistician for $U\sim\mathrm{Uniform}[0,1]$. ∎
