**Central idea.** Hoeffding's covariance identity writes $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$ as a double integral of covariances of indicators $\mathbf{1}_{\{X>u\}},\mathbf{1}_{\{Y>v\}}$; each is bounded by the strong mixing coefficient $\alpha$ and by the marginal tails (Fréchet–Hoeffding). The layer-cake identity $\min(c,a,b)=\int_0^c\mathbf{1}_{\{t<a\}}\mathbf{1}_{\{t<b\}}\,dt$ plus Tonelli converts the $\min(\alpha,\cdot,\cdot)$ double integral into the quantile integral $\int_0^{\alpha}F^{-1}(1-t)G^{-1}(1-t)\,dt$. For signed variables, split $X=X^+-X^-$, $Y=Y^+-Y^-$ and pair diagonal-with-diagonal and off-diagonal-with-off-diagonal; the elementary bound $\min(\alpha,a,b)+\min(\alpha,c,d)\le\min(2\alpha,a+c,b+d)$ upgrades $\alpha$ to $2\alpha$ while $\mathbb{P}\{X>u\}+\mathbb{P}\{X<-u\}\le\mathbb{P}\{|X|>u\}$ folds both tails into the quantiles of $|X|,|Y|$.

**Solution.** Let $\bar F_X(u)=\mathbb{P}\{X>u\}$. For Borel $A,B$,
$$\big|\mathrm{Cov}(\mathbf{1}_A,\mathbf{1}_B)\big|\le\min\big(\mathbb{P}(A),\mathbb{P}(B)\big),\tag{*}$$
since $\mathrm{Cov}(\mathbf{1}_A,\mathbf{1}_B)=\mathbb{P}(A\cap B)-\mathbb{P}(A)\mathbb{P}(B)$ lies between $-\mathbb{P}(A)\mathbb{P}(B)$ and $\mathbb{P}(A\cap B)$, both bounded in absolute value by $\min(\mathbb{P}(A),\mathbb{P}(B))$.

*Fréchet's inequality.* Let $X,Y\ge0$ be square-integrable. By Tonelli,
$$\mathbb{E}[XY]=\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\mathbb{P}\{X>u,\,Y>v\}\,du\,dv\le\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\min\big(\bar F_X(u),\bar G_Y(v)\big)\,du\,dv$$
by Fréchet–Hoeffding. For $U$ uniform on $[0,1]$, using $F^{-1}(t)>x\iff t>F(x)$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{F^{-1}(U)>u,\,G^{-1}(U)>v\}=1-\max(F(u),G(v))=\min\big(\bar F_X(u),\bar G_Y(v)\big),$$
so the right-hand side equals $\mathbb{E}[F^{-1}(U)G^{-1}(U)]=\int_0^1F^{-1}(t)G^{-1}(t)\,dt$ (finite, $\le\|X\|_2\|Y\|_2$). Since $\mathbb{E}X=\int_0^1F^{-1}$ and $\mathbb{E}Y=\int_0^1G^{-1}$ are fixed by the marginals,
$$\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)\le\int_0^1F^{-1}(t)G^{-1}(t)\,dt-\int_0^1F^{-1}(t)\,dt\int_0^1G^{-1}(t)\,dt,$$
the covariance under the comonotonic coupling.

*Rio's inequality, $X,Y\ge0$.* Hoeffding's identity (Tonelli, using $\mathbb{E}[XY]=\iint\mathbb{P}\{X>u,Y>v\}$ and $\mathbb{E}X\,\mathbb{E}Y=\iint\mathbb{P}\{X>u\}\mathbb{P}\{Y>v\}$):
$$\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\mathrm{Cov}\big(\mathbf{1}_{\{X>u\}},\mathbf{1}_{\{Y>v\}}\big)\,du\,dv.$$
By $(*)$ and the definition of $\alpha$ (with $A=(u,\infty)$, $B=(v,\infty)$),
$$\big|\mathrm{Cov}\big(\mathbf{1}_{\{X>u\}},\mathbf{1}_{\{Y>v\}}\big)\big|\le\min\big(\alpha,\,\mathbb{P}\{X>u\},\,\mathbb{P}\{Y>v\}\big),$$
hence
$$|\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)|\le\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty\min\big(\alpha,\,\mathbb{P}\{X>u\},\,\mathbb{P}\{Y>v\}\big)\,du\,dv.\tag{1}$$

*Quantile form.* For $c,a,b\ge0$, $\min(c,a,b)=\int_0^c\mathbf{1}_{\{t<a\}}\mathbf{1}_{\{t<b\}}\,dt$. With $\bar F(u)=\mathbb{P}\{X>u\}$, Tonelli gives
$$\iint_{u,v>0}\min(\alpha,\bar F(u),\bar G(v))\,du\,dv=\int_0^{\alpha}\Big[\int_0^\infty\mathbf{1}_{\{F(u)<1-t\}}\,du\Big]\Big[\int_0^\infty\mathbf{1}_{\{G(v)<1-t\}}\,dv\Big]\,dt.$$
Since $X\ge0$, $\{u\ge0:F(u)<1-t\}=[0,F^{-1}(1-t))$ up to a null set, so the bracket equals $F^{-1}(1-t)$. Thus
$$|\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)|\le\int_0^{\alpha}F^{-1}(1-t)\,G^{-1}(1-t)\,dt.\tag{2}$$

*Signed $X,Y$.* Now let $F,G$ be the CDFs of $|X|,|Y|$, and write $X=X^+-X^-$, $Y=Y^+-Y^-$. Then
$$\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=\mathrm{Cov}(X^+,Y^+)+\mathrm{Cov}(X^-,Y^-)-\mathrm{Cov}(X^+,Y^-)-\mathrm{Cov}(X^-,Y^+).$$
For $u>0$, $\{X^+>u\}=\{X>u\}$ and $\{X^->u\}=\{X<-u\}$, so Hoeffding's identity gives
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{Cov}(X^+,Y^+)&=\iint_{u,v>0}\mathrm{Cov}(\mathbf{1}_{\{X>u\}},\mathbf{1}_{\{Y>v\}})\,du\,dv,\\
\mathrm{Cov}(X^-,Y^-)&=\iint_{u,v>0}\mathrm{Cov}(\mathbf{1}_{\{X<-u\}},\mathbf{1}_{\{Y<-v\}})\,du\,dv,
\end{align*}
and similarly for the off-diagonal pairs with $\{Y^->v\}=\{Y<-v\}$. Pairing diagonal with diagonal,
\begin{align*}
&|\mathrm{Cov}(X^+,Y^+)|+|\mathrm{Cov}(X^-,Y^-)|\\
&\qquad\le\iint_{u,v>0}\Big[\min(\alpha,\mathbb{P}\{X>u\},\mathbb{P}\{Y>v\})+\min(\alpha,\mathbb{P}\{X<-u\},\mathbb{P}\{Y<-v\})\Big]\,du\,dv.
\end{align*}
The elementary bound $\min(\alpha,a,b)+\min(\alpha,c,d)\le\min(2\alpha,a+c,b+d)$, together with $\mathbb{P}\{X>u\}+\mathbb{P}\{X<-u\}\le\mathbb{P}\{|X|>u\}=:\bar F(u)$ (disjoint union) and likewise for $Y$, yields
$$|\mathrm{Cov}(X^+,Y^+)|+|\mathrm{Cov}(X^-,Y^-)|\le\iint_{u,v>0}\min\big(2\alpha,\bar F(u),\bar G(v)\big)\,du\,dv.$$
The same argument for the off-diagonal pair gives
$$|\mathrm{Cov}(X^+,Y^-)|+|\mathrm{Cov}(X^-,Y^+)|\le\iint_{u,v>0}\min\big(2\alpha,\bar F(u),\bar G(v)\big)\,du\,dv.$$
By the Tonelli computation above with $2\alpha$ in place of $\alpha$ (valid since $\alpha\le\frac14$, so $1-t\in(0,1]$ on $[0,2\alpha]$),
$$\iint_{u,v>0}\min\big(2\alpha,\bar F(u),\bar G(v)\big)\,du\,dv=\int_0^{2\alpha}F^{-1}(1-t)\,G^{-1}(1-t)\,dt.$$
Consequently
$$|\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)|\le2\int_0^{2\alpha}F^{-1}(1-t)\,G^{-1}(1-t)\,dt.\ \blacksquare$$
