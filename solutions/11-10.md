**Central idea.** By homogeneity reduce to $\lambda_1 = 1$. Abel summation rewrites the weighted sum as a convex combination of the partial sums $S_j = \sum_{i\le j}\varepsilon_iv_i$ (with weights $b_j = \lambda_j-\lambda_{j+1} \ge 0$ summing to $1$), so $\|\sum_i\lambda_i\varepsilon_iv_i\| \le \max_j\|S_j\|$. Lévy's maximal inequality — via first-passage sets and conditional symmetry of the increments — bounds $\mathbb{P}\{\max_j\|S_j\| > t\} \le 2\mathbb{P}\{\|S_n\| > t\}$.

**Solution.** If $\lambda_1 = 0$ both sides are $0$. Otherwise divide by $\lambda_1$: with $\mu_i = \lambda_i/\lambda_1$ (still $1 = \mu_1 \ge \cdots \ge \mu_n \ge 0$),
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\Big\|\sum_i\lambda_i\varepsilon_iv_i\Big\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\Big\} = \mathbb{P}\Big\{\Big\|\sum_i\mu_i\varepsilon_iv_i\Big\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t/\lambda_1\Big\},$$
so it suffices to prove the claim for $\lambda_1 = 1$ and then use $t/\lambda_1 \ge t$ (as $\lambda_1 \le 1$). Assume henceforth $\lambda_1 = 1$. Separability of $\mathbb{B}$ ensures all norms below are measurable.

*Abel summation.* Set $\lambda_{n+1} = 0$ and $b_j = \lambda_j - \lambda_{j+1} \ge 0$ for $j = 1,\dots,n$. With $S_j = \sum_{i=1}^j\varepsilon_iv_i$,
$$\sum_{j=1}^n b_jS_j = \sum_{j=1}^n(\lambda_j-\lambda_{j+1})\sum_{i\le j}\varepsilon_iv_i = \sum_{i=1}^n\varepsilon_iv_i\sum_{j\ge i}(\lambda_j-\lambda_{j+1}) = \sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i\varepsilon_iv_i.$$
Since $\sum_{j=1}^n b_j = \lambda_1 = 1$, convexity of the norm gives
$$\Big\|\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i\varepsilon_iv_i\Big\|_{\mathbb{B}} \le \sum_{j=1}^n b_j\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}} \le \max_{1\le j\le n}\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}},$$
hence $\{\|\sum_i\lambda_i\varepsilon_iv_i\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\} \subset \{\max_{j\le n}\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\}$.

*Lévy's maximal inequality.* Let $\mathcal{F}_j = \sigma(\varepsilon_1,\dots,\varepsilon_j)$ and let $A_j = \{\|S_1\|_{\mathbb{B}} \le t, \dots, \|S_{j-1}\|_{\mathbb{B}} \le t, \|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\}$ be the first-passage sets; they are disjoint, $\mathcal{F}_j$-measurable, and $\bigcup_jA_j = \{\max_{j\le n}\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\}$. Conditionally on $\mathcal{F}_j$, $Y := S_n - S_j$ is independent of $\mathcal{F}_j$ and symmetric. On $A_j$, $\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t$, and since $\|S_j+Y\|_{\mathbb{B}} + \|S_j-Y\|_{\mathbb{B}} \ge 2\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}}$, at least one of $\|S_j+Y\|_{\mathbb{B}}$, $\|S_j-Y\|_{\mathbb{B}}$ exceeds $t$. By conditional symmetry these two have the same conditional law given $\mathcal{F}_j$, so on $A_j$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{\|S_n\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t \mid \mathcal{F}_j\} = \mathbb{P}\{\|S_j+Y\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t \mid \mathcal{F}_j\} \ge \tfrac12.$$
Thus $\mathbb{P}(A_j \cap \{\|S_n\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\}) = \mathbb{E}[1_{A_j}\mathbb{P}\{\|S_n\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t \mid \mathcal{F}_j\}] \ge \tfrac12\mathbb{P}(A_j)$. Summing over $j$,
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\max_{1\le j\le n}\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\Big\} = \sum_{j=1}^n\mathbb{P}(A_j) \le 2\sum_{j=1}^n\mathbb{P}(A_j \cap \{\|S_n\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\}) \le 2\,\mathbb{P}\{\|S_n\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\}.$$

Combining with the Abel step,
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\Big\|\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i\varepsilon_iv_i\Big\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\Big\} \le \mathbb{P}\Big\{\max_{j\le n}\|S_j\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\Big\} \le 2\,\mathbb{P}\Big\{\Big\|\sum_{i=1}^n\varepsilon_iv_i\Big\|_{\mathbb{B}} > t\Big\},$$
for all $t > 0$. ∎
