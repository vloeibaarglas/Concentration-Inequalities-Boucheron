**Central idea.** Write $Y_i=|Y_i|X_i$ with $X_i=\mathrm{sign}(Y_i)$. Conditioning on the signs and applying Jensen's inequality to the convex map $z\mapsto\|z\|^p$ replaces each $|Y_i|$ by the deterministic $c_i=\mathbb{E}[|Y_i|\mid Y_i\neq0]\geq\mathbb{E}|Y_i|\geq1/C$ (the conditioning handles the possible atom at $0$, where $|Y_i|$ and $X_i$ are dependent); the contraction principle for the independent symmetric signs $X_i$ then removes the multipliers $c_i$ at the cost of $C^p$.

**Solution.** We read $X_i=\mathrm{sign}(Y_i)$ for every $i$ (the `$X_n$' is a typo; the $Y_i$ are independent symmetric, hence so are the pairs $(|Y_i|,X_i)$). Then $Y_i=|Y_i|X_i$ a.s., each $X_i$ is symmetric on $\{-1,0,1\}$, and $\mathbb{P}(Y_i\neq0)>0$ for all $i$ (else $\mathbb{E}|Y_i|=0<1/C$).

Put $c_i=\mathbb{E}[|Y_i|\mid Y_i\neq0]=\mathbb{E}|Y_i|/\mathbb{P}(Y_i\neq0)\geq\mathbb{E}|Y_i|\geq1/C$. Since $|Y_i|\perp(X_j)_{j\neq i}$, $\mathbb{E}[|Y_i|\mid X]=\mathbb{E}[|Y_i|\mid X_i]=c_i\mathbf{1}_{\{X_i\neq0\}}$, whence $\mathbb{E}[|Y_i|\mid X]X_i=c_iX_i$ a.s.

If $\mathbb{E}\|\sum_iY_iv_i\|^p=\infty$ there is nothing to prove; assume it finite (then $\mathbb{E}\|\sum_iY_iv_i\|<\infty$ as $p\geq1$). By conditional Jensen,
$$\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big\|\sum_iY_iv_i\Big\|^p\Big]=\mathbb{E}_X\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big\|\sum_i|Y_i|X_iv_i\Big\|^p\Big|X\Big]\geq\mathbb{E}\Big\|\sum_ic_iX_iv_i\Big\|^p.$$

It remains to show $\mathbb{E}\|\sum_ic_iX_iv_i\|^p\geq C^{-p}\mathbb{E}\|\sum_iX_iv_i\|^p$. Put $\alpha_i=1/(Cc_i)\in(0,1]$. The contraction principle for independent symmetric variables gives, with $w_i=c_iv_i$,
$$\mathbb{E}\Big\|\sum_i\alpha_iX_iw_i\Big\|^p\leq\mathbb{E}\Big\|\sum_iX_iw_i\Big\|^p;$$
since $\alpha_iw_i=v_i/C$, this is $C^{-p}\mathbb{E}\|\sum_iX_iv_i\|^p\leq\mathbb{E}\|\sum_ic_iX_iv_i\|^p$. For $X_i\in\{-1,0,1\}$ this follows from the one-step estimate: for $0\leq\alpha\leq1$,
$$\tfrac12\big(\|v+\alpha x\|^p+\|v-\alpha x\|^p\big)\leq(1-\alpha)\|v\|^p+\tfrac{\alpha}{2}\big(\|v+x\|^p+\|v-x\|^p\big)\leq\tfrac12\big(\|v+x\|^p+\|v-x\|^p\big),$$
using $\|v\pm\alpha x\|\leq(1-\alpha)\|v\|+\alpha\|v\pm x\|$, convexity of $t\mapsto t^p$, and $\|v\|^p\leq\frac12(\|v+x\|^p+\|v-x\|^p)$; telescoping over $i$ (the atom $\mathbb{P}(X_i=0)$ contributes $\|v\|^p$ to both sides) yields the claim. ∎
