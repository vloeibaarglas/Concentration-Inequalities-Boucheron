**Central idea.** Follow the pattern of Corollary 11.15: integrate the refined Hoffmann–Jørgensen tail bound against $px^{p-1}dx$, split the quadratic term $\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\beta x\}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\gamma x\}$ at a quantile $t$ (so the tail piece is absorbed), and bound $t$ by Markov. The $O(p)$ constant comes from the parameter choice $\alpha=\beta\sim 1/p$, $\gamma\approx 1$: then $\gamma^p\to 1/e$ stays bounded away from zero, while $\alpha^{-1},\beta^{-1}=O(p)$.

**Solution.** Let $Z_k=\sup_{s\in\mathcal{T}}|\sum_{i=1}^k X_{i,s}|$, $Z_n^*=\max_{k\le n}Z_k$, and $M=\max_{i\le n,s\in\mathcal{T}}|X_{i,s}|$. Then $Z=Z_n\le Z_n^*$, so it suffices to bound $\|Z_n^*\|_p$.

By the refined Hoffmann–Jørgensen inequality used in the proof of Corollary 11.15 (which follows from Lemma 11.13 and the symmetry of the $X_{i,s}$), for $\alpha,\beta,\gamma>0$ with $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=1$ and all $x>0$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{Z_n^*>x\}\le\mathbb{P}\{M>\alpha x\}+4\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\beta x\}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\gamma x\}.$$

To justify the absorption below, truncate: $Z_{n,m}^*=\min(Z_n^*,m)$, $U_m=\mathbb{E}[(Z_{n,m}^*)^p]<\infty$. Since $\mathbb{P}\{Z_{n,m}^*>x\}=\mathbb{P}\{Z_n^*>x\}$ for $x<m$,
\begin{align*}
U_m &= p\int_0^m x^{p-1}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n^*>x\}\,dx\\
&\le p\int_0^m x^{p-1}\mathbb{P}\{M>\alpha x\}\,dx + 4p\int_0^m x^{p-1}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\beta x\}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\gamma x\}\,dx\\
&\le \frac{\mathbb{E}[M^p]}{\alpha^p} + \frac{4p}{\beta^p}\int_0^{m\beta} y^{p-1}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>y\}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\tfrac{\gamma}{\beta}y\}\,dy,
\end{align*}
via $y=\beta x$. Split the last integral at $0<t<m\beta$ (if $t\ge m\beta$ the tail piece below is empty). On $[0,t]$, $\mathbb{P}\{\cdot\}\le1$ gives $\frac{4p}{\beta^p}\cdot\frac{t^p}{p}=\frac{4t^p}{\beta^p}$. On $[t,m\beta]$,
$$\frac{4p}{\beta^p}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>t\}\int_0^{m\beta} y^{p-1}\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>\tfrac{\gamma}{\beta}y\}\,dy = \frac{4\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>t\}}{\gamma^p}\,\mathbb{E}[\min(Z_n,m\gamma)^p]\le\frac{4\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>t\}}{\gamma^p}U_m,$$
using $z=\frac{\gamma}{\beta}y$ and $\min(Z_n,m\gamma)\le\min(Z_n^*,m)$ (since $Z_n\le Z_n^*$ and $\gamma\le1$). Hence, with $V=\mathbb{E}[M^p]$,
$$U_m \le \frac{V}{\alpha^p} + \frac{4t^p}{\beta^p} + \frac{4\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>t\}}{\gamma^p}U_m.$$

Choose $t\ge0$ with $\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>t\}\le\gamma^p/8$ (possible since $\mathbb{P}\{Z_n>t\}\to0$); then the last coefficient is $\le\frac12$ and
$$U_m \le \frac{2V}{\alpha^p} + \frac{8t^p}{\beta^p}.$$
By Markov, the quantile $t$ satisfies $t\le \frac{8\mathbb{E}Z_n}{\gamma^p}$. Thus
$$U_m \le \frac{2V}{\alpha^p} + \frac{8^{p+1}(\mathbb{E}Z_n)^p}{\beta^p\gamma^{p^2}}, \qquad U_m^{1/p} \le \frac{2^{1/p}}{\alpha}V^{1/p} + \frac{8^{(p+1)/p}}{\beta\gamma^p}\mathbb{E}Z_n.$$

Take $\alpha=\beta=\frac{1}{2(p+1)}$, $\gamma=\frac{p}{p+1}$ (so $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=1$). Then $\frac{2^{1/p}}{\alpha}\le4(p+1)$, and since $(\frac{p}{p+1})^p=\frac{1}{(1+1/p)^p}>\frac1e$,
$$\frac{8^{(p+1)/p}}{\beta\gamma^p} \le \frac{24}{\frac{1}{2(p+1)}\cdot\frac1e} = 48e(p+1),$$
using $8^{(p+1)/p}=8\cdot8^{1/p}\le24$ for $p\ge2$. Hence
$$\|Z_{n,m}^*\|_p \le 48e(p+1)\big(\|M\|_p + \mathbb{E}Z_n\big).$$
Letting $m\to\infty$, monotone convergence gives $\|Z_n^*\|_p\le\kappa_p(\|M\|_p+\mathbb{E}Z_n)$ with $\kappa_p=48e(p+1)$. Since $Z=Z_n\le Z_n^*$,
$$\mathbb{E}[Z^p]^{1/p} \le \kappa_p\big(\mathbb{E}Z + \mathbb{E}[M^p]^{1/p}\big),$$
and $\kappa_p/p = 48e(1+1/p)\le 96e$ remains bounded as $p\to\infty$. ∎
