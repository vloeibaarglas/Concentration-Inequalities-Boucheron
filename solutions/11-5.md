**Central idea.** Standard symmetrization with independent copies $X'_i$ of $X_i$: centering lets Jensen move $\mathbb{E}_{X'}$ inside $\Psi(\sup_s|\cdot|)$; the identity $\sum_i\varepsilon_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s}) \stackrel{d}{=} \sum_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})$ swaps Rademachers in and out; convexity of $\Psi$ then splits $\Psi(\frac{A+B}{2}) \le \frac{\Psi(A)+\Psi(B)}{2}$ (left inequality) and $\Psi(A+B) \le \frac{\Psi(2A)+\Psi(2B)}{2}$ (right inequality), with $\Psi$ increasing to pass through the triangle inequality.

**Solution.** Let $X'_1,\dots,X'_n$ be independent copies of $X_1,\dots,X_n$, independent of everything else. Each $X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s}$ is symmetric, so with $\varepsilon_i$ independent Rademachers,
$$\sum_{i=1}^n \varepsilon_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s}) \;\stackrel{d}{=}\; \sum_{i=1}^n (X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s}) \qquad\text{as processes in }s.$$
(The $\Psi$-expectations below are $+\infty$-valued where not integrable; each claimed inequality is trivial when its right-hand side is $+\infty$.)

*Right inequality.* Since $\mathbb{E}X'_{i,s}=0$, $\sum_iX_{i,s} = \mathbb{E}_{X'}[\sum_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})]$. The map $(z_s) \mapsto \sup_s|z_s|$ is convex, so by Jensen (twice, using that $\Psi$ is convex and increasing),
$$\Psi\!\left(\sup_s\Big|\sum_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right) \le \mathbb{E}_{X'}\Psi\!\left(\sup_s\Big|\sum_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})\Big|\right).$$
Taking $\mathbb{E}_X$ and applying the symmetrization identity,
$$\mathbb{E}\Psi\!\left(\sup_s\Big|\sum_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right) \le \mathbb{E}\Psi\!\left(\sup_s\Big|\sum_i\varepsilon_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})\Big|\right).$$
With $A = \sup_s|\sum_i\varepsilon_iX_{i,s}|$, $B = \sup_s|\sum_i\varepsilon_iX'_{i,s}|$, the triangle inequality and monotonicity give $\Psi(\sup_s|\sum_i\varepsilon_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})|) \le \Psi(A+B)$, and convexity gives $\Psi(A+B) = \Psi(2\cdot\frac{A+B}{2}) \le \frac{\Psi(2A)+\Psi(2B)}{2}$. Since $(X_i)$ and $(X'_i)$ have the same law, $\mathbb{E}\Psi(2B) = \mathbb{E}\Psi(2A)$, whence
$$\mathbb{E}\Psi\!\left(\sup_s\Big|\sum_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right) \le \mathbb{E}\Psi\!\left(2\sup_s\Big|\sum_i\varepsilon_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right).$$

*Left inequality.* Write $\sum_i\varepsilon_iX_{i,s} = \mathbb{E}_{X'}[\sum_i\varepsilon_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})]$. By Jensen as above,
$$\Psi\!\left(\tfrac12\sup_s\Big|\sum_i\varepsilon_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right) \le \mathbb{E}_{X'}\Psi\!\left(\tfrac12\sup_s\Big|\sum_i\varepsilon_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})\Big|\right).$$
Taking $\mathbb{E}_{X,\varepsilon}$ and using the symmetrization identity in reverse,
$$\mathbb{E}\Psi\!\left(\tfrac12\sup_s\Big|\sum_i\varepsilon_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right) \le \mathbb{E}_{X,X'}\Psi\!\left(\tfrac12\sup_s\Big|\sum_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})\Big|\right).$$
Now $\frac12\sup_s|\sum_i(X_{i,s}-X'_{i,s})| \le \frac{A'+B'}{2}$ with $A' = \sup_s|\sum_iX_{i,s}|$, $B' = \sup_s|\sum_iX'_{i,s}|$; by monotonicity and convexity, $\Psi(\frac{A'+B'}{2}) \le \frac{\Psi(A')+\Psi(B')}{2}$, and $\mathbb{E}\Psi(B') = \mathbb{E}\Psi(A')$. Hence
$$\mathbb{E}\Psi\!\left(\tfrac12\sup_s\Big|\sum_i\varepsilon_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right) \le \mathbb{E}\Psi\!\left(\sup_s\Big|\sum_iX_{i,s}\Big|\right). \qquad\blacksquare$$
