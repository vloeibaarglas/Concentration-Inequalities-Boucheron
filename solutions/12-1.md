**Central idea.** Symmetrization replaces the unknown $P$ by an independent ghost sample $P_n'$ (this needs $n\varepsilon^2\ge 1/2$, otherwise the right-hand side exceeds $1$ and there is nothing to prove). Conditioning on the $2n$-sample, independently swapping each pair $(X_i,X_{n+i})$ randomizes the signs in $P_n(A)-P_n'(A)$; Hoeffding's inequality applied to each of the $2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}$ distinct labelings, followed by a union bound, gives the result.

**Erratum.** The hint's second step (the hint, not the statement) is false as printed: it claims $\mathbb{P}\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}|P_n(A)-P_n'(A)|\ge\varepsilon\}\le2\,\mathbb{E}[2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}]e^{-2n\varepsilon^2}$. Counterexample: the single-set class $\mathcal{C}=\{A\}$ with $P(A)=1/2$, so every sample has one trace and $2^h\equiv1$; take $n=450$, $\varepsilon=0.1$. Then $nP_n(A)$, $nP_n'(A)$ are independent $\mathrm{Bin}(450,1/2)$ and the exact probability is $\mathbb{P}\{|nP_n(A)-nP_n'(A)|\ge45\}=0.00299$, which exceeds $2e^{-2n\varepsilon^2}=2e^{-9}\approx2.47\times10^{-4}$. The correct exponent is $n\varepsilon^2/2$: the permutation and Hoeffding argument below proves $\mathbb{P}\{\sup_A|P_n(A)-P_n'(A)|\ge\varepsilon\}\le2\,\mathbb{E}[2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}]e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$ (here $2e^{-2.25}\approx0.211\ge0.00299$), which is exactly what the exercise's main bound needs. The main statement itself is correct.

**Solution.** Write $\nu_n=P_n-P$. If $n\varepsilon^2<1/2$, then since $h\ge 0$ (so $2^{h}\ge 1$ and $\mathbb{E}[2^{h}]\ge 1$), the right-hand side is at least $4e^{-1/4}>1$ and the claim is trivial. Assume henceforth $n\varepsilon^2\ge 1/2$.

*Symmetrization.* For fixed $A$, Chebyshev's inequality gives $\mathbb{P}\{|P_n'(A)-P(A)|>\varepsilon\}\le P(A)(1-P(A))/(n\varepsilon^2)\le 1/(4n\varepsilon^2)\le 1/2$. On $\{\sup_A|\nu_n(A)|\ge 2\varepsilon\}$, pick $A$ (depending only on the first sample) with $|\nu_n(A)|\ge 2\varepsilon$. Then $|P_n(A)-P_n'(A)|\ge |\nu_n(A)|-|P_n'(A)-P(A)|$, so
$$\mathbb{P}_{\text{ghost}}\{|P_n(A)-P_n'(A)|\ge\varepsilon\}\ge\mathbb{P}_{\text{ghost}}\{|P_n'(A)-P(A)|\le\varepsilon\}\ge 1/2.$$
Since $\sup_B|P_n(B)-P_n'(B)|\ge |P_n(A)-P_n'(A)|$, Fubini gives
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}|\nu_n(A)|\ge 2\varepsilon\Big\}\le 2\,\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}|P_n(A)-P_n'(A)|\ge\varepsilon\Big\}.$$

*Permutation bound.* The $2n$ variables are i.i.d., so independently swapping each pair $(X_i,X_{n+i})$ ($i=1,\ldots,n$) preserves the joint law. Conditional on $X_1,\ldots,X_{2n}$, for fixed $A$,
$$P_n(A)-P_n'(A)\ \stackrel{d}{=}\ \frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\sigma_i\big(\mathbf{1}_{\{X_i\in A\}}-\mathbf{1}_{\{X_{n+i}\in A\}}\big)$$
with $\sigma_i$ i.i.d. Rademacher. Only $m\le n$ pairs contribute (the rest have $\mathbf{1}_{\{X_i\in A\}}=\mathbf{1}_{\{X_{n+i}\in A\}}$), so by Hoeffding's inequality,
$$\mathbb{P}_\sigma\Big\{\Big|\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_i d_i\Big|\ge\varepsilon\Big\}\le 2\exp\!\Big(-\frac{n^2\varepsilon^2}{2m}\Big)\le 2e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2},\qquad d_i\in\{-1,1\}.$$
Conditional on the $2n$-sample there are at most $2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}$ distinct values of the signed sum as $A$ varies, so a union bound gives
$$\mathbb{P}_\sigma\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}|P_n(A)-P_n'(A)|\ge\varepsilon\,\Big|\,X_1,\ldots,X_{2n}\Big\}\le 2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}\cdot 2e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}.$$
Taking expectations and combining with symmetrization,
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}|\nu_n(A)|\ge 2\varepsilon\Big\}\le 4\,\mathbb{E}\big[2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}\big]\,e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}.$$

*Remark (improved bound).* The improved symmetrization of Exercise 11.5 replaces the ghost sample by Rademacher variables on the original $n$-sample, giving $\mathbb{P}\{\sup_A|\nu_n(A)|\ge 2\varepsilon\}\le 2\,\mathbb{E}[2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_n)}]e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$: one conditions on $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ and union-bounds over the $2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_n)}$ labelings of the $n$-sample, with Hoeffding's inequality supplying $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$ per labeling.
