**Central idea.** The operator norm of a sum of PSD matrices is monotone in each summand, so the leave-one-out differences satisfy $0 \le Z-Z^{(i)} \le \|X_i\| \le a$ and, via the maximizing unit vector, $\sum_i(Z-Z^{(i)}) \le Z$; thus $Z/a$ is self-bounding and Theorem 6.12 gives both claims after rescaling.

**Solution.** Let $S = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ and $Z = \|S\|$. For each $i$ let $S^{(i)} = \sum_{j \ne i} X_j$ and $Z^{(i)} = \|S^{(i)}\|$. Since $X_i \succeq 0$, $S^{(i)} \preceq S$, and the operator norm is monotone on the PSD order, $Z^{(i)} \le Z$. By the triangle inequality,
$$0 \le Z - Z^{(i)} = \|S\| - \|S^{(i)}\| \le \|S - S^{(i)}\| = \|X_i\| \le a \quad \text{a.s.}$$
Let $v$ be a unit vector with $Z = v^T S v$. Then $Z^{(i)} \ge v^T S^{(i)} v$, so
$$Z - Z^{(i)} \le v^T S v - v^T S^{(i)} v = v^T X_i v,$$
and summing over $i$,
$$\sum_{i=1}^n (Z - Z^{(i)}) \le v^T\Big(\sum_{i=1}^n X_i\Big) v = v^T S v = Z.$$
Hence $W := Z/a$ satisfies $0 \le W - W^{(i)} \le 1$ and $\sum_i (W - W^{(i)}) \le W$, i.e. $W$ is self-bounding. By Theorem 6.12,
$$\mathrm{Var}(W) \le \mathbb{E}W, \qquad \log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(W - \mathbb{E}W)} \le (\mathbb{E}W)\,\phi(\lambda) \quad \forall\,\lambda \in \mathbb{R},$$
where $\phi(\lambda) = e^\lambda - \lambda - 1$. Since $\mathrm{Var}(W) = a^{-2}\mathrm{Var}(Z)$ and $\mathbb{E}W = a^{-1}\mathbb{E}Z$, the first inequality gives $\mathrm{Var}(Z) \le a\,\mathbb{E}Z$. For the second, $\lambda(W - \mathbb{E}W) = (\lambda/a)(Z - \mathbb{E}Z)$; replacing $\lambda$ by $a\lambda$ yields
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z - \mathbb{E}Z)} \le (\mathbb{E}Z/a)\,\phi(a\lambda) \qquad \forall\,\lambda \in \mathbb{R},$$
as claimed.
