**Central idea.** Write $\{Z\ge(1+\eta)\mathbb{E}Z+t\}=\{Z\ge\mathbb{E}Z+s\}$ with $s=\eta\mathbb{E}Z+t$ and apply Bousquet's inequality (Theorem 12.5), then convert the Bennett function to Bernstein form via $h(u)\ge u^2/(2(1+u/3))$. Since $s\ge\eta\mathbb{E}Z$, the mean is absorbed into the variance proxy as $2\mathbb{E}Z+s/3\le(2/\eta+1/3)s$, leaving an exponent of at least $s^2/(2(\sigma^2+cs))$ with $c=2/\eta+1/3$. A two-case analysis on whether $cs\le\delta\sigma^2$ (Gaussian regime) or not (exponential regime) produces the two terms; the hint justifies passing from a maximum of exponentials to their sum.

**Solution.** Let $m=\mathbb{E}Z\ge0$ and $s=\eta m+t\ge0$. Then $\{Z\ge(1+\eta)m+t\}=\{Z\ge m+s\}$, so by Theorem 12.5 (Bousquet's inequality),
$$\mathbb{P}\{Z\ge(1+\eta)m+t\}\le\exp(-vh(s/v)),$$
where $v=\sigma^2+2m$ with $\sigma^2=\sup_{f\in\mathcal{F}}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathrm{Var}(f(X_i))$, and $h(u)=(1+u)\log(1+u)-u$.

*Lemma (hint).* For $u,v>0$ and $\lambda\in[0,1]$, $\exp(-(1/u+v))\le\max(e^{-\lambda/u},\,e^{-(1-\lambda)/v})$.
*Proof.* Since $1/u+v\ge1/u\ge\lambda/u\ge\min(\lambda/u,(1-\lambda)/v)$, negating gives $-(1/u+v)\le\max(-\lambda/u,-(1-\lambda)/v)$, and exponentiating yields the claim. ∎

For $u\ge0$, $h(u)\ge u^2/(2(1+u/3))$ (the standard Bennett-to-Bernstein bound, cf.\ Exercise 2.8), so
$$vh(s/v)\ge\frac{s^2}{2(v+s/3)}.$$
Since $s=\eta m+t\ge\eta m$, we have $m\le s/\eta$, whence
$$v+s/3=\sigma^2+2m+s/3\le\sigma^2+(2/\eta+1/3)s=:\sigma^2+cs,$$
with $c=2/\eta+1/3>0$. (Assume $\sigma^2>0$; if $\sigma^2=0$ the first term below is $0$ for $t>0$.) Put $E=vh(s/v)\ge s^2/(2(\sigma^2+cs))$.

If $cs\le\delta\sigma^2$, then $\sigma^2+cs\le(1+\delta)\sigma^2$, so using $s\ge t$,
$$E\ge\frac{s^2}{2(1+\delta)\sigma^2}\ge\frac{t^2}{2(1+\delta)\sigma^2}=:A.$$
If $cs>\delta\sigma^2$, then $\sigma^2+cs<(1+1/\delta)cs$, so
$$E\ge\frac{s^2}{2(1+1/\delta)cs}=\frac{\delta s}{2(1+\delta)c}\ge\frac{\delta t}{2(1+\delta)c}=:B.$$
In either case $\exp(-E)\le\max(e^{-A},e^{-B})\le e^{-A}+e^{-B}$, the last step being the content of the Lemma. That is,
$$\mathbb{P}\{Z\ge(1+\eta)\mathbb{E}Z+t\}\le\exp\!\left(-\frac{t^2}{2(1+\delta)\sigma^2}\right)+\exp\!\left(-\frac{\delta t}{2(1+\delta)(2/\eta+1/3)}\right),$$
as claimed. ∎
