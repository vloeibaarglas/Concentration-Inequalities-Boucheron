**Central idea.** Symmetrize with a ghost sample, using the binomial median bound $\mathbb{P}\{nP_n'(A)\ge\lfloor nP(A)\rfloor\}\ge1/2$ and the monotonicity of $f(x,q)=\frac{x-q}{\sqrt{(x+q)/2}}$. Done carefully, this reaches threshold $\sqrt2\,\varepsilon$ in the symmetrized event, not just the threshold $\varepsilon$ of the hint. Conditionally on the $2n$-sample, random swaps of the pairs $(X_i,X_{n+i})$ plus Hoeffding's inequality, using $\sum_ia_i^2\le2nD(A)$, give $e^{-n\eta^2/4}$ per trace at threshold $\eta$. With $\eta=\sqrt2\varepsilon$ this is exactly the printed $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$. **The printed bound is true.** Only the hint is too weak: its threshold $\varepsilon$ must be $\sqrt2\,\varepsilon$.

**Remark (hint erratum).** The hinted inequality, with threshold $\varepsilon$ on the right, is true whenever $n\varepsilon^2>1$ (the only nontrivial range): it follows from Step 1 below, since $\varepsilon<\sqrt2\varepsilon$. But it cannot lead to the printed exponent. Combined with the per-trace swap bound, threshold $\varepsilon$ only yields $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/4}$, and the per-trace estimate at threshold $\varepsilon$ really can exceed $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$. Example: $n=10$, $\varepsilon=1$, a $2n$-sample in which $A$ contains exactly one point of each of the $10$ pairs. Then $D(A)=1/2$, and after swaps $\frac{P_n'(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{10}\sigma_i}{\sqrt{50}}$, so the conditional probability of exceeding $1$ is $\mathbb{P}\{\sum_{i\le10}\sigma_i\ge8\}=11/1024\approx0.0107>e^{-5}\approx0.0067$. This refutes only the step "threshold $\varepsilon$ per trace $\le e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$", not the printed inequality. (An earlier version of this solution wrongly concluded from this example that the printed bound is false and "corrected" it to $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/4}$; that is retracted.) The fix is to symmetrize to threshold $\sqrt2\varepsilon$.

**Solution.** *Conventions.* As in Exercise 12.1, $X_1,\dots,X_n$ define $P_n$, the independent ghost sample $X_{n+1},\dots,X_{2n}$ defines $P_n'$, and $\mathcal{C}$ is countable (or regular enough that all suprema below are measurable). $h(X_1,\dots,X_{2n})$ is the VC-entropy, so $\{A\cap\{X_1,\dots,X_{2n}\}:A\in\mathcal{C}\}$ has at most $2^{h}$ elements ("traces"). Write $D(A)=(P_n(A)+P_n'(A))/2$. A ratio whose denominator vanishes is set to $0$: if $P(A)=0$ then $P_n(A)=0$ a.s., and if $D(A)=0$ then $P_n(A)=P_n'(A)=0$. For $x,q\ge0$ with $x+q>0$ let
$$f(x,q)=\frac{x-q}{\sqrt{(x+q)/2}},\qquad \partial_xf=\frac{x+3q}{\sqrt2\,(x+q)^{3/2}}\ge0,\qquad\partial_qf=-\frac{3x+q}{\sqrt2\,(x+q)^{3/2}}\le0,$$
so $f$ is nondecreasing in $x$ and nonincreasing in $q$.

*Trivial range.* Since $h\ge0$, the right-hand side is at least $2e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$, which is $>1$ when $n\varepsilon^2\le1$ (as $2e^{-1/2}\approx1.213$). Assume $n\varepsilon^2>1$.

*Binomial median fact.* If $S\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$, every median of $S$ lies in $[\lfloor np\rfloor,\lceil np\rceil]$ (Kaas and Buhrman, 1980). Hence $\mathbb{P}\{S\ge\lfloor np\rfloor\}\ge1/2$ and $\mathbb{P}\{S\le\lceil np\rceil\}\ge1/2$. (Both were checked numerically for all $n\le200$ on a grid of $400$ values of $p$.)

*Step 1 (symmetrization to threshold $\sqrt2\varepsilon$).* We claim
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}\frac{P(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{P(A)}}\ge2\varepsilon\Big\}\le2\,\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}\frac{P_n'(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\sqrt2\,\varepsilon\Big\}. \tag{1}$$
Fix $\varepsilon'<\varepsilon$ with $n\varepsilon'^2\ge0.86$; this is possible since $n\varepsilon^2>1$. Let $E'$ be the event that $\sup_A\frac{P(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{P(A)}}>2\varepsilon'$. It contains the event on the left of (1). On $E'$, let $A^*$ be the first set, in a fixed enumeration of $\mathcal{C}$, with $P(A^*)-P_n(A^*)>2\varepsilon'\sqrt{P(A^*)}$. Then $A^*$ is a measurable function of $X_1,\dots,X_n$ alone. Write $p=P(A^*)$, $q=P_n(A^*)$ and $x=P_n'(A^*)$. Then:

- $p>0$, and since $q\ge0$, $\sqrt p>2\varepsilon'$, i.e.\ $p>4\varepsilon'^2\ge3.44/n$;
- $q<q_0:=p-2\varepsilon'\sqrt p=\sqrt p(\sqrt p-2\varepsilon')$, where $q_0>0$;
- conditionally on $X_1,\dots,X_n$, $nx\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$. By the median fact and $\lfloor np\rfloor>np-1$, the event $x>x_0:=p-1/n$ has conditional probability at least $1/2$. Also $x_0>0$.

On that event, monotonicity of $f$ gives $f(x,q)\ge f(x_0,q_0)$. Moreover
$$f(x_0,q_0)=\frac{2\varepsilon'\sqrt p-1/n}{\sqrt{p-\varepsilon'\sqrt p-1/(2n)}} .$$
Here the numerator is positive, because $2\varepsilon'\sqrt p>4\varepsilon'^2\ge1/n$. The radicand lies in $(0,p]$, because $p-\varepsilon'\sqrt p=\sqrt p(\sqrt p-\varepsilon')>2\varepsilon'^2>1/(2n)$. Hence
$$f(x,q)\ \ge\ \frac{2\varepsilon'\sqrt p-1/n}{\sqrt p}=2\varepsilon'-\frac1{n\sqrt p}\ \ge\ 2\varepsilon'-\frac1{2n\varepsilon'}\ \ge\ \sqrt2\,\varepsilon' .$$
The last inequality is equivalent to $n\varepsilon'^2\ge\frac1{2(2-\sqrt2)}\approx0.8536$, which holds. Since $D(A^*)>0$ on this event, $\sup_A\frac{P_n'(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge f(x,q)\ge\sqrt2\varepsilon'$. By Fubini (integrate first over the ghost sample),
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_A\frac{P_n'(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\sqrt2\,\varepsilon'\Big\}\ge\mathbb{E}\Big[\mathbb{1}_{E'}\,\mathbb{P}\{x>x_0\mid X_1,\dots,X_n\}\Big]\ge\frac12\,\mathbb{P}(E')\ge\frac12\,\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_A\frac{P(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{P(A)}}\ge2\varepsilon\Big\}.$$
As $\varepsilon'\uparrow\varepsilon$, the events $\{\sup_A\cdots\ge\sqrt2\varepsilon'\}$ decrease to $\{\sup_A\cdots\ge\sqrt2\varepsilon\}$. Continuity from above gives (1). (A random check of the chain $x>p-1/n,\ q<p-2\varepsilon'\sqrt p\Rightarrow f(x,q)\ge\sqrt2\varepsilon'$ over $2\cdot10^5$ draws with $n\varepsilon'^2\ge0.86$ found no violation.)

*Step 2 (random swaps).* Let $\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_n$ be i.i.d.\ Rademacher signs, independent of the data. Swapping $X_i\leftrightarrow X_{n+i}$ whenever $\sigma_i=-1$ preserves the joint law of $(X_1,\dots,X_{2n})$. Put $a_i(A)=\mathbb{1}_A(X_{n+i})-\mathbb{1}_A(X_i)$. The swap turns $n(P_n'(A)-P_n(A))=\sum_ia_i(A)$ into $\sum_i\sigma_ia_i(A)$ and leaves $D(A)$ unchanged. So, for $\eta>0$,
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_A\frac{P_n'(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\eta\Big\}=\mathbb{E}\,\mathbb{P}_\sigma\Big\{\sup_{A:\,D(A)>0}\frac{\sum_i\sigma_ia_i(A)}{n\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\eta\Big\}.$$
Given $X_1,\dots,X_{2n}$, the quantities $a_i(A)$ and $D(A)$ depend on $A$ only through its trace, so the inner supremum is a maximum over at most $2^{h}$ traces. Fix a trace with $D>0$. Since $|a_i|\le1$ and $|a_i|\le\mathbb{1}_A(X_i)+\mathbb{1}_A(X_{n+i})$,
$$\sum_ia_i^2=\sum_i|a_i|\le\sum_{j=1}^{2n}\mathbb{1}_A(X_j)=2nD .$$
If $\sum_ia_i^2=0$, the event is empty. Otherwise Hoeffding's inequality for Rademacher sums gives
$$\mathbb{P}_\sigma\Big\{\sum_i\sigma_ia_i\ge n\eta\sqrt D\Big\}\le\exp\Big(-\frac{n^2\eta^2D}{2\sum_ia_i^2}\Big)\le\exp\Big(-\frac{n^2\eta^2D}{4nD}\Big)=e^{-n\eta^2/4}.$$
A union bound over traces, followed by expectation, gives
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_A\frac{P_n'(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\eta\Big\}\le\mathbb{E}\big[2^{h(X_1,\dots,X_{2n})}\big]e^{-n\eta^2/4}. \tag{2}$$

*Conclusion.* Taking $\eta=\sqrt2\varepsilon$ in (2) and combining with (1),
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}\frac{P(A)-P_n(A)}{\sqrt{P(A)}}\ge2\varepsilon\Big\}\le2\,\mathbb{E}\big[2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}\big]e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}.$$
This is the printed bound. (With the hint's threshold $\eta=\varepsilon$, (2) gives only $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/4}$. The example in the Remark shows that the per-trace step cannot do better at that threshold, so the gain has to come from Step 1.) ∎
