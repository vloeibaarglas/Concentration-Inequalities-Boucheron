**Central idea.** This mirrors Exercise 12.3 with the roles of $P$ and $P_n$ exchanged. Symmetrize with a ghost sample, using the upper binomial median bound $\mathbb{P}\{nP_n'(A)\le\lceil nP(A)\rceil\}\ge1/2$ and the monotonicity of $g(x,q)=\frac{q-x}{\sqrt{(q+x)/2}}$, to reach threshold $\sqrt2\,\varepsilon$ in the symmetrized event. Random pair swaps plus Hoeffding's inequality, using $\sum_ia_i^2\le2nD(A)$, then give $e^{-n(\sqrt2\varepsilon)^2/4}=e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$ per trace. **The printed bound is true as stated.** (An earlier version of this solution claimed that $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}$ is "unattainable" and weakened it to $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/4}$. That claim is retracted. The $n=10$ example of Exercise 12.3 only shows that symmetrizing to threshold $\varepsilon$ is too weak; it does not refute the printed inequality.)

**Solution.** *Conventions.* Use the notation of Exercises 12.1 and 12.3: the ghost sample $X_{n+1},\dots,X_{2n}$ defines $P_n'$, $\mathcal{C}$ is countable (or regular enough that all suprema are measurable), at most $2^{h(X_1,\dots,X_{2n})}$ traces $A\cap\{X_1,\dots,X_{2n}\}$ occur, and $D(A)=(P_n(A)+P_n'(A))/2$. Sets with $P_n(A)=0$ contribute $0$ to the left-hand supremum; a ratio with vanishing denominator is $0$. For $q,x\ge0$ with $q+x>0$ let
$$g(x,q)=\frac{q-x}{\sqrt{(q+x)/2}} .$$
By the derivative computation of Exercise 12.3 (with the variables exchanged), $g$ is nonincreasing in $x$ and nondecreasing in $q$.

*Trivial range.* Since $h\ge0$, the right-hand side is at least $2e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}\ge2e^{-1/2}>1$ when $n\varepsilon^2\le1$. Assume $n\varepsilon^2>1$.

*Step 1 (symmetrization to threshold $\sqrt2\varepsilon$).* We claim
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}\frac{P_n(A)-P(A)}{\sqrt{P_n(A)}}\ge2\varepsilon\Big\}\le2\,\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}\frac{P_n(A)-P_n'(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\sqrt2\,\varepsilon\Big\}. \tag{1}$$
Fix $\varepsilon'<\varepsilon$ with $r:=n\varepsilon'^2\ge1$. Let $E'$ be the event that some $A$ with $P_n(A)>0$ has $P_n(A)-P(A)>2\varepsilon'\sqrt{P_n(A)}$. It contains the event on the left of (1). On $E'$, let $A^*$ be the first such set in a fixed enumeration of $\mathcal{C}$; it is a function of $X_1,\dots,X_n$ only. Write $q=P_n(A^*)>0$, $s=\sqrt q$, $p=P(A^*)$ and $x=P_n'(A^*)$. Since $p\ge0$, $q>2\varepsilon's$, i.e.\ $s>2\varepsilon'$, and $p<q-2\varepsilon's$.

Conditionally on $X_1,\dots,X_n$, $nx\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$. Every median of $\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$ is at most $\lceil np\rceil<np+1$ (Kaas and Buhrman, 1980; checked numerically for all $n\le200$ on a $400$-point grid of $p$). So with conditional probability at least $1/2$,
$$x<p+\tfrac1n<x_1:=q-2\varepsilon's+\tfrac1n,\qquad x_1=s(s-2\varepsilon')+\tfrac1n>0 .$$
On that event, since $g$ is nonincreasing in $x$,
$$g(x,q)\ge g(x_1,q)=\frac{2\varepsilon's-1/n}{\sqrt{s^2-\varepsilon's+1/(2n)}}\ \ge\ F(s):=\frac{2\varepsilon's-1/n}{\sqrt{s^2+1/(2n)}} .$$
For the second inequality: the numerator is positive, because $2\varepsilon's>4\varepsilon'^2\ge4/n$, and $0<s^2-\varepsilon's+\frac1{2n}\le s^2+\frac1{2n}$, because $s>\varepsilon'$.

$F$ is increasing on $(0,\infty)$: a direct computation gives $F'(s)\,(s^2+\frac1{2n})^{3/2}=\frac{\varepsilon'+s}{n}>0$. Since $s>2\varepsilon'$, $F(s)\ge F(2\varepsilon')$. Now
$$F(2\varepsilon')=\frac{(4r-1)/n}{\sqrt{(8r+1)/(2n)}}=\sqrt{\frac2n}\,\frac{4r-1}{\sqrt{8r+1}},\qquad\sqrt2\,\varepsilon'=\sqrt{\frac2n}\,\sqrt r .$$
Both sides are positive because $r\ge1$, so
$$F(2\varepsilon')\ge\sqrt2\,\varepsilon'\iff(4r-1)^2\ge r(8r+1)\iff8r^2-9r+1\ge0\iff(8r-1)(r-1)\ge0,$$
which holds for $r\ge1$. Hence $g(x,q)\ge\sqrt2\,\varepsilon'$, and since $D(A^*)\ge q/2>0$,
$$\sup_A\frac{P_n(A)-P_n'(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge g(x,q)\ge\sqrt2\,\varepsilon'$$
with conditional probability at least $1/2$ on $E'$. (A random check of this chain over $2\cdot10^5$ draws with $r\ge1$ found no violation; the polynomial identity $(4r-1)^2-r(8r+1)=(8r-1)(r-1)$ was checked symbolically.) Integrating over the first sample (Fubini),
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_A\frac{P_n(A)-P_n'(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\sqrt2\,\varepsilon'\Big\}\ge\tfrac12\,\mathbb{P}(E')\ge\tfrac12\,\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_A\frac{P_n(A)-P(A)}{\sqrt{P_n(A)}}\ge2\varepsilon\Big\}.$$
As $\varepsilon'\uparrow\varepsilon$, the left events decrease to $\{\sup_A\cdots\ge\sqrt2\varepsilon\}$, and continuity from above gives (1).

*Step 2 (random swaps).* As in Exercise 12.3, let $\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_n$ be i.i.d.\ Rademacher signs independent of the data. Swapping $X_i\leftrightarrow X_{n+i}$ when $\sigma_i=-1$ preserves the joint law. With $a_i(A)=\mathbb{1}_A(X_i)-\mathbb{1}_A(X_{n+i})$, the swap turns $n(P_n(A)-P_n'(A))=\sum_ia_i(A)$ into $\sum_i\sigma_ia_i(A)$ and leaves $D(A)$ unchanged. Conditionally on $X_1,\dots,X_{2n}$, only the at most $2^h$ traces matter. For a trace with $D>0$, $\sum_ia_i^2=\sum_i|a_i|\le\sum_{j\le2n}\mathbb{1}_A(X_j)=2nD$. If $\sum_ia_i^2=0$ the event below is empty. Otherwise Hoeffding's inequality gives
$$\mathbb{P}_\sigma\Big\{\sum_i\sigma_ia_i\ge n\eta\sqrt D\Big\}\le\exp\Big(-\frac{n^2\eta^2D}{2\sum_ia_i^2}\Big)\le e^{-n\eta^2/4}.$$
A union bound over traces and expectation over the $2n$-sample give, for $\eta>0$,
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_A\frac{P_n(A)-P_n'(A)}{\sqrt{D(A)}}\ge\eta\Big\}\le\mathbb{E}\big[2^{h(X_1,\dots,X_{2n})}\big]e^{-n\eta^2/4}. \tag{2}$$

*Conclusion.* Taking $\eta=\sqrt2\varepsilon$ in (2) and combining with (1),
$$\mathbb{P}\Big\{\sup_{A\in\mathcal{C}}\frac{P_n(A)-P(A)}{\sqrt{P_n(A)}}\ge2\varepsilon\Big\}\le2\,\mathbb{E}\big[2^{h(X_1,\ldots,X_{2n})}\big]e^{-n\varepsilon^2/2}.$$
This is exactly the printed bound. (Symmetrizing only to threshold $\varepsilon$, as a literal transcription of the hint of Exercise 12.3 suggests, would give $e^{-n\varepsilon^2/4}$ after (2). The improvement comes entirely from Step 1.) ∎
