**Central idea.** Factor $e^{\lambda Z} - e^{\lambda Z_i} = e^{\lambda Z}(1 - e^{-\lambda(Z-Z_i)})$ and use the identity $1 - e^{-s} = s - \phi(-s)$ with $\phi(t) = e^t - t - 1$ to split the sum into a self-bounding term, controlled by $\lambda\mathbb{E}[Ze^{\lambda Z}]$, and a modified-log-Sobolev term, bounded below by the entropy $\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})$; the two $\lambda\mathbb{E}[Ze^{\lambda Z}]$ terms cancel, leaving $\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}$.

**Solution.** Let $\lambda \ge 0$ and assume $\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z} < \infty$, the inequality being trivial otherwise. In Theorem 12.5, $Z = \sup_{t \in \mathcal{T}}\sum_{i=1}^n X_{i,t}$ with $X_{i,t} \ge 0$ and $Z_i = \sup_{t \in \mathcal{T}}\sum_{j \ne i} X_{j,t}$, so $Z \ge Z_i \ge 0$ and the self-bounding property $\sum_{i=1}^n (Z - Z_i) \le Z$ holds.

Since $e^{\lambda Z_i} = e^{\lambda Z}e^{-\lambda(Z-Z_i)}$,
$$e^{\lambda Z} - e^{\lambda Z_i} = e^{\lambda Z}\left(1 - e^{-\lambda(Z-Z_i)}\right).$$
With $\phi(t) = e^t - t - 1$, setting $s = \lambda(Z-Z_i)$ gives
$$s - \phi(-s) = s - \left(e^{-s} + s - 1\right) = 1 - e^{-s},$$
hence $1 - e^{-\lambda(Z-Z_i)} = \lambda(Z-Z_i) - \phi(-\lambda(Z-Z_i))$. Summing over $i$ and taking expectations,
$$\sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda Z} - e^{\lambda Z_i}\right] = \lambda\mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda Z}\sum_{i=1}^n(Z-Z_i)\right] - \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda Z}\phi(-\lambda(Z-Z_i))\right].$$
By the self-bounding property and $\lambda \ge 0$,
$$\lambda\mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda Z}\sum_{i=1}^n(Z-Z_i)\right] \le \lambda\mathbb{E}\left[Ze^{\lambda Z}\right].$$
Theorem 6.6 (the modified logarithmic Sobolev inequality) gives
$$\sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda Z}\phi(-\lambda(Z-Z_i))\right] \ge \mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z}) = \lambda\mathbb{E}\left[Ze^{\lambda Z}\right] - \mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}.$$
Combining the two bounds,
$$\sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda Z} - e^{\lambda Z_i}\right] \le \lambda\mathbb{E}\left[Ze^{\lambda Z}\right] - \lambda\mathbb{E}\left[Ze^{\lambda Z}\right] + \mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z} = \mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z},$$
which is the claimed inequality. ∎
