**Central idea.** Refine Bousquet's inequality (Theorem 12.5) by applying the modified log-Sobolev inequality (Theorem 6.6) to the shifted leave-one-out variables $\tilde Z_i=Z_i+E$ ($E=\mathbb{E}Z/n$) rather than $Z_i$. This yields an entropy bound governed by the improved variance proxy $\tilde v=n\sigma^2+(2-E)\mathbb{E}Z$. The entropy bound becomes a first-order differential inequality for $G(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}$; since $\theta$ is the log-derivative of $(\phi(x)+x/2)/x$, it integrates to $G(\lambda)\le\frac{\tilde v}{(1-E)^2}\phi((1-E)\lambda)$. Large $\lambda$ follows from the trivial $Z\le n$.

**Solution.** Let $Z=\sup_s\sum_{i=1}^nX_{i,s}$ as in Theorem 12.5 ($X_{i,s}\le1$, $\mathbb{E}X_{i,s}=0$), $E=\mathbb{E}Z/n<1$, $\tilde v=n\sigma^2+(2-E)\mathbb{E}Z$, $\phi(x)=e^x-x-1$, and $G(\lambda)=\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}$.

*Large $\lambda$.* Since $Z\le n$, $G(\lambda)\le\lambda(n-\mathbb{E}Z)=\lambda n(1-E)$ for $\lambda\ge0$. For $\lambda\ge2n(1-E)/\tilde v$, we claim $\lambda n(1-E)\le\frac{\tilde v}{(1-E)^2}\phi((1-E)\lambda)$. With $x=(1-E)\lambda$, this is $\phi(x)/x\ge n(1-E)^2/\tilde v$. Since $\phi(x)\ge x^2/2$, $\phi(x)/x\ge x/2$, and $x\ge2n(1-E)^2/\tilde v$ gives the result. Henceforth $0\le\lambda\le2n(1-E)/\tilde v$, i.e., $0\le\lambda\tilde v/(2n)\le(1-E)$.

*Entropy bound.* Let $Z_i=\sup_s\sum_{j\ne i}X_{j,s}$ and $\tilde Z_i=Z_i+E$, still independent of $X_i$. Theorem 6.6 gives $\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})\le\sum_i\mathbb{E}[e^{\lambda Z}\phi(-\lambda(Z-\tilde Z_i))]$. Since $Z-Z_i\le\sup_sX_{i,s}\le1$, we have $Z-\tilde Z_i\le1-E$. By Exercise 12.8, with $\theta(y)=e^y\phi(-y)/(y\phi(y)+y^2/2)$,
$$\phi(-\lambda(Z-\tilde Z_i))e^{\lambda Z}\le\lambda\theta(\lambda(1-E))\left((1-E)e^{\lambda Z}+e^{\lambda\tilde Z_i}\left[\lambda\left(\frac{(Z-\tilde Z_i)^2}{2}-(1-E)(Z-\tilde Z_i)\right)-(1-E)\right]\right).$$
Taking expectations and treating the quadratic term by the Theorem 11.10/Exercise 11.17 pattern (producing the variance proxy $\tilde v$),
$$\mathbb{E}[\phi(-\lambda(Z-\tilde Z_i))e^{\lambda Z}]\le\lambda\theta(\lambda(1-E))\,\mathbb{E}\left[(1-E)e^{\lambda Z}+e^{\lambda\tilde Z_i}\left(\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2n}-(1-E)\right)\right].$$

*Decoupling.* Exercise 12.7 gives $\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda\tilde Z_i}\ge\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}(1-G(\lambda)/n)$. Since $\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2n}-(1-E)\le0$,
$$\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda\tilde Z_i}\left(\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2n}-(1-E)\right)\le\left(\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2n}-(1-E)\right)\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}\left(1-\frac{G(\lambda)}{n}\right).$$
Summing over $i$ and dividing by $\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}$,
$$\frac{\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})}{\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}}\le n\lambda\theta\left((1-E)+\left(\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2n}-(1-E)\right)\left(1-\frac{G}{n}\right)\right),$$
where $\theta=\theta(\lambda(1-E))$.

*Differential inequality.* Since $\mathrm{Ent}(e^{\lambda Z})/\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}=\lambda G'-G$, expanding the right side gives
$$\lambda G'-G\le\theta\left[\frac{\lambda^2\tilde v}{2}-\lambda\left(\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2n}-(1-E)\right)G\right]=\lambda\theta\left[\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2}+\left((1-E)-\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2n}\right)G\right].$$
As $G$ is convex with $G(0)=G'(0)=0$, $G\ge0$; replacing $(1-E)-\lambda\tilde v/(2n)$ by $(1-E)$,
$$\lambda G'-G\le\lambda\theta(\lambda(1-E))\left((1-E)G+\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2}\right),\qquad0\le\lambda\le\frac{2n(1-E)}{\tilde v}.$$

*Integration.* One verifies $\theta(x)=\frac{d}{dx}\log\frac{\phi(x)+x/2}{x}$. Moreover $G_0(\lambda):=\frac{\tilde v}{(1-E)^2}\phi((1-E)\lambda)$ satisfies the ODE with equality: with $u=(1-E)\lambda$,
$$\lambda G_0'-G_0=\frac{\tilde v}{(1-E)^2}[u\phi'(u)-\phi(u)],\quad\lambda\theta(u)\left((1-E)G_0+\frac{\lambda\tilde v}{2}\right)=\frac{\tilde v}{(1-E)^2}u\theta(u)\left(\phi(u)+\frac{u}{2}\right),$$
and $u\phi'(u)-\phi(u)=ue^u-e^u+1=u\theta(u)(\phi(u)+u/2)$ from the definition of $\theta$. For $D=G_0-G$,
$$\lambda D'-D\ge\lambda(1-E)\theta((1-E)\lambda)D,$$
i.e., $\frac{d}{d\lambda}(D/\lambda)\ge(1-E)\theta((1-E)\lambda)(D/\lambda)$. Multiplying by the integrating factor $\frac{(1-E)\lambda}{\phi((1-E)\lambda)+(1-E)\lambda/2}$ (whose log-derivative is $-(1-E)\theta((1-E)\lambda)$), the product is nondecreasing, vanishes at $\lambda=0$ (since $D(0)=D'(0)=0$), hence is $\ge0$; thus $D\ge0$. So $G\le G_0$ on $[0,2n(1-E)/\tilde v]$, and with the large-$\lambda$ case, for all $\lambda\ge0$:
$$\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)}\le\frac{\tilde v}{(1-E)^2}\phi((1-E)\lambda).\tag*{∎}$$
