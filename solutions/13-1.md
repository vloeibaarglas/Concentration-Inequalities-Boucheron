**Central idea.** Mimic Lemma 13.1's chaining with nets at the entropy-number scales $e_n(S)$, but extract a subsequence $n_k$ with $e_{n_{k+1}}\leq e_{n_k}/2$: then the drift ($a$-) terms sum geometrically to $O(a\delta)$, while each subsequence block's $\sqrt{v}$/$c$ contribution is dominated by the corresponding block of the full sums $\sum_ne_n2^{n/2}$, $\sum_ne_n2^n$ (geometric-series comparison). Each link is controlled by the Bernstein maximal inequality (Corollary 2.6).

**Solution.** We use the notation of Lemma 13.1: $S$ finite, $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(X_s-X_{s'})}\leq a\lambda d(s,s')+\frac{v\lambda^2d(s,s')^2}{2(1-c\lambda d(s,s')))}$ for $0<\lambda<(cd(s,s'))^{-1}$, $\delta=\sup_{s\in S}d(s,s_0)$, $e_n=e_n(S)$. (The sums in the statement mix indices $j$ and $n$; read $e_j(S)$, $2^{j/2}$, $2^j$. Universal constants are implied though not written.)

For $n\geq1$ with $e_n>0$, let $S_n\subset S$ be a minimal $(e_n+\eta_n)$-net, so $|S_n|=N(e_n+\eta_n,S)\leq2^{2^n}$ by definition of $e_n$ as an infimum, and choose $\Pi_n(s)\in S_n$ with $d(s,\Pi_n(s))\leq e_n+\eta_n$. (This is the rigorous form of the hint's maximal $e_n$-packing: maximality gives the net property, but the cardinality $|S_n|\leq2^{2^{n+1}}$ as stated needs this $\eta$-regularization, since $N(e_n,S)\leq2^{2^n}$ may fail at the infimum itself.) If $e_n=0$, take $S_n=S$, $\Pi_n=\mathrm{id}$ (valid as $S$ is finite). Put $S_0=\{s_0\}$, $\Pi_0\equiv s_0$.

Since $e_n\to0$, choose $1=n_1<n_2<\cdots$ with $e_{n_{k+1}}\leq e_{n_k}/2$ (stop if some $e_{n_k}=0$). Telescoping,
$$X_s-X_{s_0}=\sum_{k\geq1}\big(X_{\Pi_{n_k}(s)}-X_{\Pi_{n_{k-1}}(s)}\big),\qquad \Pi_{n_0}:=\Pi_0,$$
a finite sum since $\Pi_{n_k}=\mathrm{id}$ eventually. Hence
$$\mathbb{E}\sup_{s\in S}(X_s-X_{s_0})\leq\sum_{k\geq1}\mathbb{E}\max_{s\in S}\big(X_{\Pi_{n_k}(s)}-X_{\Pi_{n_{k-1}}(s)}\big).$$
At level $k$ there are at most $M_k:=|S_{n_{k-1}}|\cdot|S_{n_k}|\leq2^{2^{n_{k-1}}+2^{n_k}}$ distinct pairs, each at distance at most $\bar{d}_k:=e_{n_{k-1}}+e_{n_k}+2\eta\leq3e_{n_{k-1}}$ for $k\geq2$ (with $e_{n_0}:=\delta$), and $\bar{d}_1\leq\delta+e_{n_1}+\eta\leq2\delta$. Moreover $\log M_k\leq2(\log2)2^{n_k}$.

For fixed pairs with $\max d\leq\bar{d}$, $\mathbb{E}\max_{\text{pairs}}(X_t-X_{t'})\leq\max\mathbb{E}[X_t-X_{t'}]+\mathbb{E}\max(\text{centered})$. From the mgf bound, $\mathbb{E}[X_t-X_{t'}]\leq a\bar{d}$ (let $\lambda\downarrow0$), and centered increments satisfy $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda W}\leq2a\lambda\bar{d}+\frac{v\lambda^2\bar{d}^2}{2(1-c\lambda\bar{d})}$, whence by the Chernoff maximal bound $\mathbb{E}\max_iW_i\leq\inf_{0<\lambda<1/(c\bar{d})}[\frac{\log M_k}{\lambda}+2a\bar{d}+\frac{v\lambda\bar{d}^2}{2(1-c\lambda\bar{d})}]\leq2a\bar{d}+\sqrt{2v\bar{d}^2\log M_k}+c\bar{d}\log M_k$ (Bernstein optimization, as in Lemma 2.4). Altogether, level $k$ contributes at most $3a\bar{d}_k+C_1\sqrt{v}\,\bar{d}_k2^{n_k/2}+C_2c\,\bar{d}_k2^{n_k}$.

Summing: the $a$-terms give $3a\sum_k\bar{d}_k\lesssim a\delta$, since $\bar{d}_1\lesssim\delta$ and $\bar{d}_k\lesssim e_{n_{k-1}}\leq2^{-(k-2)}\delta$ for $k\geq2$ sum geometrically. For the $\sqrt{v}$-terms ($k\geq2$), $e_{n_{k-1}}2^{n_k/2}\leq2\sqrt{2}\sum_{n=n_{k-1}}^{n_k-1}e_n2^{n/2}$: indeed, minimality of $n_k$ gives $e_n>e_{n_{k-1}}/2$ for $n_{k-1}\leq n<n_k$, and $\sum_{n=n_{k-1}}^{n_k-1}2^{n/2}\geq2^{n_k/2}/\sqrt{2}$. Summing over the disjoint blocks, $\sum_{k\geq2}e_{n_{k-1}}2^{n_k/2}\lesssim\sum_{n\geq1}e_n2^{n/2}$; similarly $\sum_{k\geq2}e_{n_{k-1}}2^{n_k}\lesssim\sum_{n\geq1}e_n2^n$. Letting $\eta\downarrow0$,
$$\mathbb{E}\left[\sup_{s\in S}X_s-X_{s_0}\right]\lesssim a\delta+\sqrt{v}\,\delta+c\,\delta+\sqrt{v}\sum_{n\geq1}e_n2^{n/2}+c\sum_{n\geq1}e_n2^n.$$

*Remark (inaccuracy in the statement).* As written—sums from $j=1$ with no base-scale term—the inequality is false. Counterexample: $S=\{s_0,s_1\}$, $X_{s_1}-X_{s_0}\sim N(0,v\delta^2)$ (so $a=c=0$; the hypothesis holds as $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(X_{s_1}-X_{s_0})}=\frac{v\lambda^2\delta^2}{2}$). Here $e_n(S)=0$ for all $n\geq1$ (since $N(\varepsilon,S)\leq2\leq4=2^{2^1}$ for every $\varepsilon>0$), so the right-hand side is $0$, but $\mathbb{E}[\sup_sX_s-X_{s_0}]=\mathbb{E}[(X_{s_1}-X_{s_0})^+]=\sqrt{v}\,\delta/\sqrt{2\pi}>0$. The corrected form above (equivalently, sums from $n=0$ with $e_0(S):=\delta$) holds. ∎
