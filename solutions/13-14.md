**Central idea.** The layer-cake identity writes a $[0,1]$-valued $\phi$ as a pointwise limit of uniform averages of the super-level indicators $\mathbf{1}_{\{\phi>t\}}$, which lie in the VC-class $\mathcal{I}=\{\mathbf{1}_{\{g>t\}}:g\in\mathcal{F},\,t\in\mathbb{R}\}$. Decomposing $|f|\le1$ as a difference $g-h$ of two $[0,1]$-valued functions, the sub-level indicators needed for $h$ are handled by complements, $\mathbf{1}_{\{f<c\}}=\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{X}}-\lim_k\mathbf{1}_{\{f>c-1/k\}}$, at the cost of total absolute weight $2$; balancing the weights exhibits $f/3$ as a pointwise limit of signed combinations from $\mathcal{I}$ of total absolute weight at most $1$.

**Solution.** Let $M=\sup_{f\in\mathcal{F},\,x\in\mathcal{X}}|f(x)|<\infty$; dividing by $M$ we assume $M=1$. Put $\mathcal{C}=\{\{g>t\}:g\in\mathcal{F},\,t\in\mathbb{R}\}$, a VC-class of sets of dimension at most $V$ by hypothesis, and $\mathcal{I}=\{\mathbf{1}_C:C\in\mathcal{C}\}$, a VC-class of indicator functions. Let $\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})=\{\sum_k\nu_k\mathbf{1}_{E_k}:\mathbf{1}_{E_k}\in\mathcal{I},\ \sum_k|\nu_k|\le1\}$ and denote by $\overline{\mathrm{sconv}}(\mathcal{I})$ its pointwise closure.

For $0\le\phi\le1$, the hint gives $\phi(x)=\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^m\mathbf{1}_{\{\phi(x)>i/m\}}$ for every $x$: the sum counts $|\{1\le i\le m:i<m\phi(x)\}|=\max(0,\lceil m\phi(x)\rceil-1)$, and $(\lceil m\phi(x)\rceil-1)/m\to\phi(x)$.

Let $f\in\mathcal{F}$, $|f|\le1$, and write $f=g-h$ with $g=\frac{1+f}{2},\ h=\frac{1-f}{2}\in[0,1]$. Since $\mathbf{1}_{\{g>i/m\}}=\mathbf{1}_{\{f>2i/m-1\}}\in\mathcal{I}$, the averages $g_m=\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^m\mathbf{1}_{\{f>2i/m-1\}}$ lie in $\mathrm{conv}(\mathcal{I})\subseteq\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})$, and $g=\lim_m g_m$ pointwise, so $g\in\overline{\mathrm{sconv}}(\mathcal{I})$.

For $h$, $\mathbf{1}_{\{h>i/m\}}=\mathbf{1}_{\{f<1-2i/m\}}$. For any $c$, $\mathbf{1}_{\{f<c\}}=\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{X}}-\mathbf{1}_{\{f\ge c\}}$ with $\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{X}}=\mathbf{1}_{\{f>-2\}}\in\mathcal{I}$ and $\mathbf{1}_{\{f\ge c\}}=\lim_{k\to\infty}\mathbf{1}_{\{f>c-1/k\}}$ pointwise, each $\mathbf{1}_{\{f>c-1/k\}}\in\mathcal{I}$. Hence $\mathbf{1}_{\{f<c\}}$ is a pointwise limit of the signed combinations $\mathbf{1}_{\{f>-2\}}-\mathbf{1}_{\{f>c-1/k\}}$, of total absolute weight $2$; i.e.\ $\mathbf{1}_{\{f<c\}}\in\overline{2\,\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})}$. Thus $h_m=\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^m\mathbf{1}_{\{f<1-2i/m\}}\in\overline{2\,\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})}$ (a convex combination inside the convex set $\overline{2\,\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})}$) and $h=\lim_m h_m$ pointwise.

Finally $f/3=g/3-h/3$, and $\frac13\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})-\frac23\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})\subseteq\mathrm{sconv}(\mathcal{I})$, since for $a=\frac13\sum_i\nu_i\mathbf{1}_{E_i}$, $b=\frac23\sum_j\mu_j\mathbf{1}_{F_j}$ the difference has total absolute weight $\frac13\sum_i|\nu_i|+\frac23\sum_j|\mu_j|\le1$. Hence $f/3\in\overline{\mathrm{sconv}}(\mathcal{I})$. For general $M$, $\mathcal{F}\subseteq3M\cdot\overline{\mathrm{sconv}}(\mathcal{I})$. The closure is inherent in the hint's limit representation and is the form used in applications. (If $\mathcal{F}$ is symmetric, $\mathbf{1}_{\{f<c\}}=\mathbf{1}_{\{-f>-c\}}\in\mathcal{I}$ directly and the constant improves to $2M$; if $\mathcal{F}\ge0$, to $M$.)
