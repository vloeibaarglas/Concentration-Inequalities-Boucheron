**Central idea.** The proof of Exercise 13-15 carries over to $[0,1]$-valued functions once the chain starts at the zero function. Its Step 1 gives $\mathbb{E}Z^\pm\le A\max(\sigma,\sqrt{\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2})$, where $\delta_n^2=\sup_sP_nf_s^2$. The only new ingredient is a bound on $\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2$ through $\sigma^2$ and $\mathbb{E}Z^\pm$. Symmetrization and the contraction principle (Theorem 11.6, applied to $x\mapsto x^2/2$, which is $1$-Lipschitz on $[0,1]$) reduce $\mathbb{E}\sup_s(P_n-P)f_s^2$ to the uncentered Rademacher average $\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i)$. The functions are not centered, so this average has to be recentered before desymmetrizing. That gives $\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2\le\sigma^2+2\sigma/\sqrt n+4(\mathbb{E}Z^++\mathbb{E}Z^-)/\sqrt n$, and the fixed-point argument of 13-15 then closes. As in 13-15, **the comparable bound $K\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}$ cannot be uniform in $n$.** It holds under $n\sigma^2\ge\psi(\sigma/2)$, and in general one has $K(\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}+\psi(\sigma/2)/\sqrt n)$.

**Solution.**

*Setting.* $(f_s)_{s\in\mathcal T}$ is a countable family of measurable functions $\mathcal{X}\to[0,1]$ with $Pf_s^2\le\sigma^2$ for all $s$, where $\sigma\in(0,1)$. This is the analogue of $P(A_s)\le\sigma^2$. It is also implied by $Pf_s\le\sigma^2$, because $f_s^2\le f_s$, so the results below cover that reading too. $N(\delta,\mathcal T,Q)$ is the largest number of indices that are pairwise $\delta$-separated in $L_2(Q)$, and the universal entropy $H(\delta,\mathcal T)=\sup_Q\log N(\delta,\mathcal T,Q)$ (over finitely supported $Q$) satisfies $H\le\psi$. Here $\psi$ is as in 13-15, and we assume $\psi\ge1$ (see 13-15 for the reduction $\psi\to\psi\vee1$). Let
$$Z^\pm=\sup_s\pm\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\big(f_s(X_i)-Pf_s\big),\qquad\delta_n^2=\sup_sP_nf_s^2,\qquad S=\mathbb{E}Z^++\mathbb{E}Z^-.$$

**Erratum (the statement needs a condition linking $n$ and $\sigma$).** Indicator functions are $[0,1]$-valued, with $P\mathbb 1_A^2=P(A)$ and $\|\mathbb 1_A-\mathbb 1_B\|_{L_2(Q)}=Q(A\triangle B)^{1/2}$. So the counterexample of 13-15 (Erratum 1) applies verbatim. There, $P$ is uniform on $[0,1]$, the $f_s$ are the indicators of $[a,a+\sigma^2)$ with $a\in\mathbb{Q}$, and $\psi(\delta)=4\log(e/\delta)+\log(3e)$. With $n=1$, $\mathbb{E}Z^+\ge1-\sigma^2$, while $\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}=0.0522$ at $\sigma=10^{-2}$ and tends to $0$. So no bound $K\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}$ uniform in $n$ can hold. The hint's display (1) of 13-15 is false as printed (13-15, Erratum 2), and we do not use it.

**Corrected statement (the "comparable bound").** There is $K$ depending only on $\psi$ such that for all $n\ge1$ and $\sigma\in(0,1)$,
$$\max(\mathbb{E}Z^+,\mathbb{E}Z^-)\le K\Big(\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}+\frac{\psi(\sigma/2)}{\sqrt n}\Big),$$
and $\max(\mathbb{E}Z^+,\mathbb{E}Z^-)\le K\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}$ whenever $n\sigma^2\ge\psi(\sigma/2)$. These are the same bounds as for classes of sets.

*Step 1: chaining (as in 13-15, Steps 0–1).* Put $F(r)=\int_0^r\sqrt{\psi}$. By 13-15 Step 0, $F$ is finite, concave and nondecreasing on $[0,1]$, $F(\rho)/\rho$ is nonincreasing, and $F(\rho)\le K_1\rho\sqrt{\psi(\rho)}$ for some $K_1\ge1$. One-sided symmetrization (13-15 Step 1) gives $\mathbb{E}Z^\pm\le2\,\mathbb{E}\sup_sY_s$ with $Y_s=n^{-1/2}\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i)$. Given $X$, adjoin the zero function ($Y_0=0$). Hoeffding's lemma gives $\log\mathbb{E}_\varepsilon e^{\lambda(Y_s-Y_t)}\le\lambda^2\|f_s-f_t\|_{L_2(P_n)}^2/2$. Also $\|f_s-0\|_{L_2(P_n)}=(P_nf_s^2)^{1/2}\le\delta_n\le1$, and the packing numbers of a finite subfamily together with $0$ are at most $e^{\psi(u)}+1\le e^{2\psi(u)}$. Corollary 13.2, followed by monotone convergence over finite subfamilies, gives $\mathbb{E}_\varepsilon\sup_sY_s\le12\sqrt2\,F(\delta_n)$. Since $t\mapsto F(\sqrt t)$ is concave, Jensen gives $\mathbb{E}Z^\pm\le24\sqrt2\,F(\rho)$ with $\rho^2=\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2\le1$. Now let $R$ be any number with $R\ge\max(\sigma^2,\rho^2)$. Then $F(\rho)\le F(\max(\sigma,\rho))\le\frac{\max(\sigma,\rho)}{\sigma}F(\sigma)\le\frac{\sqrt R}{\sigma}K_1\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}$, so
$$\mathbb{E}Z^\pm\le A\sqrt R,\qquad A:=24\sqrt2\,K_1\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}\ \ (\ge1).\tag{1}$$

*Step 2: bounding $\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2$.* Since $Pf_s^2\le\sigma^2$,
$$\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2\le\sigma^2+\frac1n\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\big(f_s(X_i)^2-Pf_s^2\big)\le\sigma^2+\frac2n\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i)^2.$$
The second inequality is one-sided symmetrization (13-15 Step 1, with $g_s=f_s^2$). Let $\phi(x)=x^2/2$ for $|x|\le1$ and $\phi(x)=|x|-1/2$ for $|x|>1$. This $\phi$ is $1$-Lipschitz with $\phi(0)=0$, and $\phi(f_s(X_i))=f_s(X_i)^2/2$. Theorem 11.6, with the identity as the convex nondecreasing function (as in the proof of Theorem 11.8; the theorem is stated for $\Psi$ on $[0,\infty)$, but its proof via Lemma 11.7 works for any convex nondecreasing $\Psi:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$, so the sign of the supremum does not matter), applied conditionally on $X$ to finite subfamilies and extended by monotone convergence, gives
$$\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i)^2=2\,\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_i\phi(f_s(X_i))\le2\,\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i).$$
*Recentering.* Write $f_s(X_i)=h_s(X_i)+Pf_s$ with $h_s=f_s-Pf_s$. Since $0\le Pf_s\le(Pf_s^2)^{1/2}\le\sigma$,
$$\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i)\le\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_ih_s(X_i)+\sigma\Big(\sum_i\varepsilon_i\Big)_+,$$
and $\mathbb{E}(\sum_i\varepsilon_i)_+=\frac12\mathbb{E}|\sum_i\varepsilon_i|\le\frac{\sqrt n}2$. For the centered part, let $X'$ be an independent copy of the sample. Since $\mathbb{E}h_s(X_i')=0$, we can write $\sum_i\varepsilon_ih_s(X_i)=\mathbb{E}'\big[\sum_i\varepsilon_i(h_s(X_i)-h_s(X_i'))\big]$, and $\sup_s\mathbb{E}'\le\mathbb{E}'\sup_s$. Conditionally on $\varepsilon$, swapping $X_i\leftrightarrow X_i'$ for every $i$ with $\varepsilon_i=-1$ preserves the joint law. Hence
$$\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\varepsilon_ih_s(X_i)\le\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\big(h_s(X_i)-h_s(X_i')\big)\le\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_ih_s(X_i)+\mathbb{E}\sup_s\sum_i\big(-h_s(X_i')\big)=\sqrt n\,S.$$
Combining these,
$$\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2\le\sigma^2+\frac2n\cdot2\Big(\frac{\sigma\sqrt n}2+\sqrt n\,S\Big)=\sigma^2+\frac{2\sigma}{\sqrt n}+\frac{4S}{\sqrt n}.\tag{2}$$
(For indicators one has directly $\delta_n^2\le\sigma^2+Z^+/\sqrt n$. The extra terms appear because $Pf_s$ need not equal $Pf_s^2$. The hint's sharper $\mathbb{E}\delta_n^2\le\sigma^2+8\mathbb{E}Z^+/\sqrt n$ is neither proved nor refuted here, and it is not needed.)

*Step 3: the fixed point.* Take $R=\sigma^2+2\sigma/\sqrt n+4S/\sqrt n$, which is at least $\max(\sigma^2,\rho^2)$ by (2). Summing (1) over $\pm$ gives $S\le2A\sqrt R$, where $S\le2\sqrt n<\infty$.

*Under $n\sigma^2\ge\psi(\sigma/2)$.* Since $\psi\ge1$, $1/\sqrt n\le\sigma$, so $2\sigma/\sqrt n\le2\sigma^2$ and $\sqrt R\le\sqrt3\,\sigma+2n^{-1/4}\sqrt S$. With $y=\sqrt S$ this gives $y^2\le by+c$, where $b=4An^{-1/4}$ and $c=2\sqrt3A\sigma$. Hence $S\le b^2+2c=4\sqrt3\,A\sigma+16A^2/\sqrt n$. Also $A^2/\sqrt n=(24\sqrt2K_1)^2\psi(\sigma/2)/\sqrt n\le(24\sqrt2K_1)^2\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}$. So
$$\max(\mathbb{E}Z^+,\mathbb{E}Z^-)\le S\le\big(96\sqrt6\,K_1+18432\,K_1^2\big)\,\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}.$$
*In general.* $2\sigma/\sqrt n\le\sigma^2+1/n$, so $\sqrt R\le\sqrt2\,\sigma+n^{-1/2}+2n^{-1/4}\sqrt S$. The same quadratic argument gives $S\le4\sqrt2A\sigma+4A/\sqrt n+16A^2/\sqrt n\le4\sqrt2A\sigma+20A^2/\sqrt n$ (using $A\ge1$). This is at most $K(\sigma\sqrt{\psi(\sigma/2)}+\psi(\sigma/2)/\sqrt n)$ with $K=23040K_1^2$. ∎

*Note (repair, 2026-10-30).* An earlier version claimed a bound uniform in $n$ (false, by the Erratum). It also contained a false step: "desymmetrization gives $\mathbb{E}\sup_s\frac1n\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i)\le\frac2{\sqrt n}\mathbb{E}Z^+$", which fails for uncentered $f_s$. For the class $\{f\equiv\sigma,\ f\equiv0\}$, $Z^+\equiv0$, yet $\mathbb{E}\sup_s\frac1n\sum_i\varepsilon_if_s(X_i)=\sigma\,\mathbb{E}(\sum_i\varepsilon_i)_+/n$, which is about $0.398\,\sigma/\sqrt n>0$ (for example $0.0398\sigma$ at $n=100$, exact binomial computation). The recentering in Step 2 repairs this, following the independent audit.
