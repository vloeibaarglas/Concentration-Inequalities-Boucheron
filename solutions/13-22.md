**Central idea.** A volume comparison in both directions: a maximal $\varepsilon$-separated set in $B_p^d$ yields disjoint $\varepsilon/2$-balls packed inside $(1+\varepsilon/2)B_p^d$ (upper bound on the covering number), while any $\varepsilon$-cover of $B_p^d$ must have total volume at least that of $B_p^d$ (lower bound). Since $\mathrm{vol}(rB_p^d) = r^d\,\mathrm{vol}(B_p^d)$ and volume is translation invariant, both bounds follow from counting.

**Solution.** Let $N(\varepsilon) = N(\varepsilon, B_p^d, \|\cdot\|_p)$ be the minimal number of $\ell_p^d$-balls of radius $\varepsilon$ covering $B_p^d$, so $H(\varepsilon, B_p^d) = \log N(\varepsilon)$. Fix $\varepsilon > 0$.

*Upper bound.* Let $\{x_1, \ldots, x_M\} \subset B_p^d$ be a maximal $\varepsilon$-separated set, i.e. $\|x_i - x_j\|_p > \varepsilon$ for $i \ne j$; it is finite since $B_p^d$ is compact. The balls $B(x_i, \varepsilon/2)$ are pairwise disjoint by the triangle inequality. Moreover each is contained in $(1+\varepsilon/2)B_p^d$: if $\|y - x_i\|_p \le \varepsilon/2$ and $\|x_i\|_p \le 1$, then $\|y\|_p \le 1 + \varepsilon/2$. Comparing volumes (using translation invariance and $\mathrm{vol}(rB_p^d) = r^d\,\mathrm{vol}(B_p^d)$),
$$M \left(\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}\right)^d \mathrm{vol}(B_p^d) = \sum_{i=1}^M \mathrm{vol}(B(x_i, \tfrac{\varepsilon}{2})) \le \mathrm{vol}\!\left(\left(1+\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}\right)B_p^d\right) = \left(1+\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}\right)^d \mathrm{vol}(B_p^d),$$
so $M \le \left(\frac{1+\varepsilon/2}{\varepsilon/2}\right)^d = \left(1 + \frac{2}{\varepsilon}\right)^d$. A maximal $\varepsilon$-separated set is an $\varepsilon$-net (any $x \in B_p^d$ at distance $> \varepsilon$ from all $x_i$ could be adjoined, contradicting maximality), hence $N(\varepsilon) \le M$, and
$$H(\varepsilon, B_p^d) = \log N(\varepsilon) \le d\log\left(1 + \frac{2}{\varepsilon}\right).$$

*Lower bound.* Let $\{y_1, \ldots, y_K\} \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ with $K = N(\varepsilon)$ be centers of an $\varepsilon$-cover of $B_p^d$, so $B_p^d \subseteq \bigcup_{j=1}^K B(y_j, \varepsilon)$. By subadditivity and translation invariance of volume,
$$\mathrm{vol}(B_p^d) \le \sum_{j=1}^K \mathrm{vol}(B(y_j, \varepsilon)) = K\,\varepsilon^d\,\mathrm{vol}(B_p^d),$$
whence $K \ge \varepsilon^{-d}$ and
$$H(\varepsilon, B_p^d) = \log K \ge d\log\frac{1}{\varepsilon}.$$
Both bounds hold for every $\varepsilon > 0$ (and hence in particular for $0 < \varepsilon < 1$). ∎
