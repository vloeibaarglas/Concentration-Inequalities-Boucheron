**Central idea.** For $p\ge2$ the crude bound $K(p,d)=d^{1-2/p}$ of Exercise 13.23 comes from comparing $\ell_p$ directly to $\ell_2$; interpolating through an intermediate exponent $q\in[2,p]$ and using the sharp type-2 inequality $\mathbb{E}\|S_n\|_q^2\le(q-1)\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{E}\|X_i\|_q^2$ for $\ell_q$ ($q\ge2$) — proved by induction from a Hessian bound on $z\mapsto\|z\|_q^2$ — gives $\mathbb{E}\|S_n\|_p^2\le(q-1)d^{2/q-2/p}V$ for every $q\in[2,p]$, and optimizing over $q$ yields the infimum.

**Solution.** Let $p\ge2$, $\mathbb{E}X_i=0$, and $V=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{E}\|X_i\|_p^2<\infty$. We first prove a type-2 inequality for $\ell_q$, $q\ge2$.

Lemma. For $q\ge2$, $\mathbb{E}\|S_n\|_q^2\le(q-1)\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{E}\|X_i\|_q^2$.

*Proof.* It suffices that for deterministic $x$ and mean-zero $Y$ with $\mathbb{E}\|Y\|_q^2<\infty$,
$$\mathbb{E}\|x+Y\|_q^2\le\|x\|_q^2+(q-1)\mathbb{E}\|Y\|_q^2,\tag{*}$$
for then conditioning on $X_1,\dots,X_{n-1}$ and inducting gives the claim. Let $f(z)=\|z\|_q^2$. Where $f$ is $C^2$,
$$\nabla^2f(z)=2(2-q)\|z\|_q^{2-2q}vv^T+2(q-1)\|z\|_q^{2-q}\mathrm{diag}(|z_j|^{q-2}),\qquad v_j=|z_j|^{q-1}\mathrm{sgn}(z_j).$$
For $q\ge2$ the first term is negative semidefinite, and by Hölder,
$$h^T\nabla^2f(z)h\le2(q-1)\|z\|_q^{2-q}\sum_{j=1}^d|z_j|^{q-2}h_j^2\le2(q-1)\|h\|_q^2\qquad\forall\,h\in\mathbb{R}^d,$$
since $\sum_j|z_j|^{q-2}h_j^2\le\|z\|_q^{q-2}\|h\|_q^2$. Taylor's theorem with integral remainder gives, where defined,
$$f(x+y)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y\rangle+(q-1)\|y\|_q^2,$$
because $\int_0^1(1-t)\,dt=1/2$. Taking expectations and using $\mathbb{E}Y=0$ yields $(*)$; the points where $f$ fails to be $C^2$ form a null set, and $(*)$ extends past them by continuity and approximation. ∎

Now fix $q\in[2,p]$. Since $q\le p$, $\|z\|_p\le\|z\|_q$ for all $z$ (as $\sum_j|z_j|^p\le(\sum_j|z_j|^q)^{p/q}$), so $\mathbb{E}\|S_n\|_p^2\le\mathbb{E}\|S_n\|_q^2$. By the Lemma,
$$\mathbb{E}\|S_n\|_q^2\le(q-1)\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{E}\|X_i\|_q^2.$$
Since $q\le p$, $\|z\|_q\le d^{1/q-1/p}\|z\|_p$ (Hölder), so $\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{E}\|X_i\|_q^2\le d^{2/q-2/p}V$. Combining,
$$\mathbb{E}\|S_n\|_p^2\le(q-1)d^{2/q-2/p}V\qquad\forall\,q\in[2,p],$$
and taking the infimum over $q\in[2,p]$ gives $K(p,d)\le\inf_{q\in[2,p]}(q-1)d^{2/q-2/p}$. At $q=2$ this recovers the bound $d^{1-2/p}$ of Exercise 13.23, so it is a genuine improvement.

Remark. The book writes the infimum over "$q\in[2,p]\cup\mathbb{R}$", which equals $\mathbb{R}$ and is a typo; the argument gives the infimum over $[2,p]$.
