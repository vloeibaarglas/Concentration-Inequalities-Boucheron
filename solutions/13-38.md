**Central idea.** Write $Z=\sqrt n\,\|F_n-F\|_{L^2[0,1]}$ with $F_n(x)=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le x\}}$, $F(x)=x$; by Riesz–Fischer $\|g\|_2=\sup_{\|h\|_2\le1}\langle h,g\rangle$, so $Z$ is a supremum of the centered empirical process $(1/\sqrt n)\sum_i\int_0^1h(x)(\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le x\}}-x)\,dx$ over a countable dense subset of the $L^2$ unit ball. Then $\mathbb{E}Z^2=n\int_0^1\mathrm{Var}(F_n(x))dx=1/6$ gives $\mathbb{E}Z\le1/\sqrt6$, and Efron–Stein with $|\,Z-Z^{(i)}|\le\sqrt{|X_i-X_i'|/n}$ gives $\mathrm{Var}(Z)\le\frac12\mathbb{E}|X_1-X_1'|=1/6$.

**Solution.** The stated $P_n([0,x])=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le x\}}$ is missing the factor $1/n$: the empirical measure is $P_n=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_{X_i}$, so $P_n([0,x])=F_n(x):=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le x\}}$. With this correction,
$$Z^2=n\int_0^1(F_n(x)-x)^2dx,\qquad Z=\sqrt n\,\|F_n-F\|_2,\quad F(x)=x.$$
By Riesz–Fischer, $\|g\|_2=\sup_{\|h\|_2\le1}\langle h,g\rangle$ for $g\in L^2[0,1]$. Since $L^2[0,1]$ is separable, fix a countable dense $\mathcal{D}$ in the unit ball; by continuity of $h\mapsto\langle h,g\rangle$,
$$Z=\sqrt n\sup_{h\in\mathcal{D}}\int_0^1h(x)(F_n(x)-x)dx=\sup_{h\in\mathcal{D}}\sum_{i=1}^nY_{i,h},$$
where $Y_{i,h}=\frac1{\sqrt n}\int_0^1h(x)(\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le x\}}-x)dx$. For fixed $h$ the $Y_{i,h}$ are independent across $i$ and centered, since $\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le x\}}]=x$. Thus $Z$ is the supremum of an empirical process.

*Remark on the hint.* With $e_i(x)=\sqrt2\sin(2\pi ix)$ and $\mathcal{T}$ the rational unit sequences, $\sup_{s\in\mathcal{T}}\sum_{i\ge1}s_i\sqrt n\langle e_i,F_n-F\rangle=\sqrt n\,(\sum_{i\ge1}\langle e_i,F_n-F\rangle^2)^{1/2}=\sqrt n\,\|P(F_n-F)\|_2$, where $P$ projects onto $\overline{\mathrm{span}}\{e_i\}$. But $\{e_i\}$ is *not* complete in $L^2[0,1]$ (e.g.\ it is orthogonal to constants), and $F_n-F$ has nonzero mean a.s., so this supremum is *strictly smaller* than $Z$ in general: the stated identity is inaccurate as written (with $\sin(\pi ix)$ in place of $\sin(2\pi ix)$ the system $\{\sqrt2\sin(\pi ix)\}$ is complete and the identity would hold by Parseval).

*Mean.* $\mathbb{E}[(F_n(x)-x)^2]=\mathrm{Var}(F_n(x))=x(1-x)/n$, so
$$\mathbb{E}Z^2=n\int_0^1\frac{x(1-x)}{n}dx=\int_0^1x(1-x)dx=\frac16,$$
hence $\mathbb{E}Z\le\sqrt{\mathbb{E}Z^2}=1/\sqrt6$ by Jensen.

*Variance (Efron–Stein).* Let $X_i'$ be an independent copy of $X_i$ and $Z^{(i)}$ the statistic with $X_i$ replaced by $X_i'$. By the reverse triangle inequality,
$$|Z-Z^{(i)}|=\sqrt n\,\big|\|F_n-F\|_2-\|F_n^{(i)}-F\|_2\big|\le\sqrt n\,\|F_n-F_n^{(i)}\|_2.$$
Now $F_n-F_n^{(i)}=\frac1n(\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i'\le\cdot\}}-\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le\cdot\}})$, and since the difference of indicators takes values in $\{-1,0,1\}$,
$$\|F_n-F_n^{(i)}\|_2^2=\frac1{n^2}\int_0^1\big|\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i'\le x\}}-\mathbb{1}_{\{X_i\le x\}}\big|dx=\frac{|X_i-X_i'|}{n^2},$$
because $\int_0^1|\mathbb{1}_{\{a\le x\}}-\mathbb{1}_{\{b\le x\}}|dx=|a-b|$ for $a,b\in[0,1]$. Hence $(Z-Z^{(i)})^2\le|X_i-X_i'|/n$, and by Efron–Stein,
$$\mathrm{Var}(Z)\le\frac12\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{E}\big[(Z-Z^{(i)})^2\big]\le\frac12\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\mathbb{E}|X_i-X_i'|}{n}=\frac12\,\mathbb{E}|X_1-X_1'|.$$
For $X_1,X_1'\stackrel{iid}{\sim}\mathrm{Uniform}[0,1]$, $\mathbb{E}|X_1-X_1'|=\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^1|x-y|dxdy=1/3$. Therefore
$$\mathrm{Var}(Z)\le\frac16.$$
In particular $\mathrm{Var}(Z)\le\mathbb{E}Z^2=1/6$, with equality in the Efron–Stein bound up to the $(EZ)^2$ gap. ∎
