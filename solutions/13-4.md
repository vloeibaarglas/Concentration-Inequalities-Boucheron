**Central idea.** The metric $d(s,s')=\sqrt{1/\log s+1/\log s'}$ makes $\{s:s\geq e^{2/\delta^2}\}$ a single $\delta$-ball while $\{2,\dots,e^{2/\delta^2}\}$ needs $\asymp e^{2/\delta^2}$ balls, so $H(\delta,\mathbb{N})\asymp1/\delta^2$. Then Dudley's integral $\int_0\sqrt{H(u)}du\asymp\int_0du/u$ diverges (chaining gives nothing), Sudakov gives only $\mathbb{E}Z\gtrsim\delta\sqrt{H(\delta)}\asymp1$ (a constant), while slicing into dyadic blocks $\{2^m\leq i<2^{m+1}\}$—inside which the variances $1/\log i$ are comparable—and a union bound per block yields $\mathbb{P}\{Z>t\}\lesssim2^{-t^2/2}$ for $t\geq2$, hence $\mathbb{E}Z\leq10$.

**Solution.** Write $X_i=Y_i/\sqrt{\log\max(i,2)}$, so $Z=\sup_{i\geq1}X_i$ is a centered Gaussian process with natural distance $d(i,j)^2=\mathbb{E}[(X_i-X_j)^2]=1/\log\max(i,2)+1/\log\max(j,2)$ for $i\neq j$ (independence), i.e. $d(s,s')=\sqrt{1/\log s+1/\log s'}$ for $s\neq s'$, $s,s'\geq2$.

*Entropy.* Let $\delta<1/2$. If $s,s'\geq e^{2/\delta^2}$ then $1/\log s+1/\log s'\leq\delta^2$, so all such $s$ lie in one $\delta$-ball; covering $\{1,\dots,\lceil e^{2/\delta^2}\rceil\}$ needs at most $e^{2/\delta^2}+1$ further balls. Hence $N(\delta,\mathbb{N})\leq e^{2/\delta^2}+2$ and $H(\delta,\mathbb{N})\leq2/\delta^2+\log2\leq3/\delta^2=:\kappa'/\delta^2$ (using $\log2\leq1/\delta^2$ for $\delta<1/2$). Conversely, $S_\delta:=\{s:2\leq s\leq e^{2/\delta^2}\}$ is $(\delta/\sqrt{2})$-separated since $1/\log s\geq\delta^2/2$ there gives $d(s,s')\geq\delta/\sqrt{2}$; thus $N(\delta/\sqrt{2},\mathbb{N})\geq|S_\delta|\geq e^{2/\delta^2}-1$ and $H(\delta/\sqrt{2},\mathbb{N})\geq2/\delta^2-\log2\geq1/\delta^2$, i.e. $H(u,\mathbb{N})\geq\frac{1}{2u^2}=:\kappa/u^2$.

*Dudley (Corollary 13.2).* $\mathbb{E}Z\lesssim\int_0^{D}\sqrt{H(u,\mathbb{N})}du$ with $D=\mathrm{diam}(\mathbb{N})\leq\sqrt{2/\log2}$. Since $\sqrt{H(u)}\gtrsim1/u$ near $0$, the integral diverges: Dudley's bound is $+\infty$, i.e. vacuous. Classical chaining cannot even show $\mathbb{E}Z<\infty$ here.

*Sudakov (Theorem 13.4).* For $\varepsilon<D$, $\mathbb{E}Z\geq\frac{1}{2}\varepsilon\sqrt{H(\varepsilon,\mathbb{N})}\geq\frac{\sqrt{\kappa}}{2}$, a positive constant independent of everything—true but uninformative (no rate; consistent with $\mathbb{E}Z$ being bounded, as shown next).

*Finiteness via slicing.* $Z\leq\max_{m\geq0}W_m$ where $W_0=\max_{i\in\{1,2\}}|Y_i|/\sqrt{\log2}$ and $W_m=\max_{2^m\leq i<2^{m+1}}|Y_i|/\sqrt{m\log2}$ for $m\geq1$ (using $\log i\geq m\log2$ on the block, as hinted). By the union bound, for $m\geq1$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{W_m>t\}\leq2^m\cdot2e^{-t^2m\log2/2}=2\cdot2^{m(1-t^2/2)},$$
and $\mathbb{P}\{W_0>t\}\leq4\cdot2^{-t^2/2}$. For $t\geq2$, $1-t^2/2\leq-1$, so $\sum_{m\geq1}\mathbb{P}\{W_m>t\}\leq2\sum_{m\geq1}2^{-m}\cdot2^{1-t^2/2}/(1-2^{1-t^2/2})\leq8\cdot2^{-t^2/2}$ (using $2^{1-t^2/2}\leq1/2$). Hence $\mathbb{P}\{Z>t\}\leq12\cdot2^{-t^2/2}$ for $t\geq2$. Therefore
$$\mathbb{E}Z=\int_0^\infty\mathbb{P}\{Z>t\}dt\leq2+12\int_2^\infty e^{-(\log2)t^2/2}dt\leq2+12\cdot\frac{e^{-2\log2}}{2\log2}=2+\frac{3}{2\log2}\leq5<10,$$
using $\int_x^\infty e^{-at^2/2}dt\leq e^{-ax^2/2}/(ax)$. The point of the hint: the $X_i$ have wildly different variances ($1/\log2$ down to $0$), so a single-scale chaining argument drowns; slicing into blocks of comparable variance and bounding each block sharply recovers finiteness. ∎
