**Central idea.** By Cauchy–Schwarz, $\sup_{s\in\mathcal{T}}\sum_is_iX_i=(\sum_ia_i^2X_i^2)^{1/2}$ exactly, and Jensen gives $\mathbb{E}Z\leq(\sum_ia_i^2)^{1/2}$. For the modulus, differences $s-s'$ lie in $2\mathcal{T}$ ($\mathcal{T}$ convex symmetric) intersected with the Euclidean $c$-ball; since $\sum_it_i^2/\min(a_i^2,c^2)\leq\sum_it_i^2/a_i^2+\sum_it_i^2/c^2\leq5$, this set sits inside the ellipsoid $\{\sum_it_i^2/\min(a_i^2,c^2)\leq8\}$, whose Gaussian supremum is $\sqrt{8}(\sum_i\min(a_i^2,c^2)X_i^2)^{1/2}$. Both bounds are sharp up to universal constants, whereas Dudley's entropy bound (Corollary 13.2) is not sharp for ellipsoids in general.

**Solution.** Let $X=(X_1,\dots,X_n)$ be standard Gaussian and $\mathcal{T}=\{s:\sum_is_i^2/a_i^2\leq1\}$, $a_i>0$.

*Global bound.* For fixed $X$, $\sum_is_iX_i=\sum_i(s_i/a_i)(a_iX_i)\leq(\sum_is_i^2/a_i^2)^{1/2}(\sum_ia_i^2X_i^2)^{1/2}\leq(\sum_ia_i^2X_i^2)^{1/2}$ by Cauchy–Schwarz (equality for $s_i\propto a_i^2X_i$). Hence $Z=(\sum_ia_i^2X_i^2)^{1/2}$ a.s., and by Jensen ($\sqrt{\cdot}$ concave),
$$\mathbb{E}Z=\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big(\sum_ia_i^2X_i^2\Big)^{1/2}\Big]\leq\Big(\sum_ia_i^2\mathbb{E}X_i^2\Big)^{1/2}=\Big(\sum_ia_i^2\Big)^{1/2}.$$

*Modulus of continuity.* Put $t=s-s'$. Since $\mathcal{T}$ is convex and symmetric, $\{s-s':s,s'\in\mathcal{T}\}=\mathcal{T}-\mathcal{T}=2\mathcal{T}=\{t:\sum_it_i^2/a_i^2\leq4\}$. With $\|t\|\leq c$,
$$\sup_{\substack{s,s'\in\mathcal{T}\\\|s-s'\|\leq c}}\sum_i(s_i-s_i')X_i\leq\sup_{\substack{t\in2\mathcal{T}\\\|t\|\leq c}}\langle t,X\rangle.$$
Let $m_i=\min(a_i^2,c^2)$. For $t\in2\mathcal{T}$, $\|t\|\leq c$:
$$\sum_i\frac{t_i^2}{m_i}=\sum_it_i^2\max\Big(\frac1{a_i^2},\frac1{c^2}\Big)\leq\sum_i\frac{t_i^2}{a_i^2}+\frac1{c^2}\sum_it_i^2\leq4+1=5\leq8.$$
Thus $\{t\in2\mathcal{T}:\|t\|\leq c\}\subseteq\mathcal{E}:=\{t:\sum_it_i^2/m_i\leq8\}$, and $\sup_{t\in\mathcal{E}}\langle t,X\rangle=\sqrt{8}(\sum_im_iX_i^2)^{1/2}$ (maximizer $t_i\propto m_iX_i$). By Jensen,
$$\mathbb{E}\sup_{\substack{s,s'\in\mathcal{T}\\\|s-s'\|\leq c}}\sum_i(s_i-s_i')X_i\leq\sqrt{8}\,\mathbb{E}\Big[\Big(\sum_im_iX_i^2\Big)^{1/2}\Big]\leq\sqrt{8\sum_{i=1}^n\min(a_i^2,c^2)}.$$

*On "Theorem 13.2".* There is no Theorem 13.2 in the book (the numbering runs Lemma 13.1, Corollary 13.2, Theorem 13.3, Theorem 13.4); the intended reference is the Gaussian chaining bound, Corollary 13.2 (Dudley). With the natural metric $d(s,s')=\|s-s'\|_2$ (since $\mathbb{E}[(\sum_i(s_i-s_i')X_i)^2]=\|s-s'\|^2$), it gives $\mathbb{E}Z\leq12\int_0^{\max_ia_i}\sqrt{H(u,\mathcal{T})}du$. The volumetric estimate $N(\mathcal{T},\|\cdot\|_2,u)\leq\prod_{i:a_i>u}(Ca_i/u)$ yields $H(u,\mathcal{T})\leq\sum_{i:a_i>u}\log_+(Ca_i/u)$, i.e.
$$\mathbb{E}Z\lesssim\int_0^{\max_ia_i}\sqrt{\sum_{i:a_i>u}\log_+\frac{a_i}{u}}\,du.$$
As the book notes (p. 377), chaining is not sharp for ellipsoids in general; the sharp bound requires generic chaining (Talagrand, 1996a). By contrast, the two direct bounds above are sharp up to universal constants: e.g. $\mathbb{E}Z=\mathbb{E}[(\sum_ia_i^2X_i^2)^{1/2}]\geq\sum_ia_i^2\mathbb{E}|X_i|/(\sum_ja_j^2)^{1/2}=\sqrt{2/\pi}\,(\sum_ia_i^2)^{1/2}$, using $\|v\|_2\geq\langle v,a\rangle/\|a\|_2$ with $v_i=a_i|X_i|$. ∎
