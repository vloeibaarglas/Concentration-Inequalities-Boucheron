**Central idea.** Both suprema are over Gaussian processes on the same index set $K$; the Sudakov–Fernique inequality (Theorem 13.3) bounds $\mathbb{E}Z$ by $\mathbb{E}U$ provided the increments of the $Z$-process are dominated by those of the $U$-process, which follows from the identity $u^TXv=\langle X,vu^T\rangle_{HS}$ and the elementary estimate $\|vu^T-ts^T\|_{HS}^2\le\|u-s\|^2+\|v-t\|^2$ (Davidson–Szarek (2001)); the second inequality is Exercise 13.5.

**Solution.** Write $u^TXv=\sum_{i,j}u_iX_{ij}v_j=\langle X,vu^T\rangle_{HS}$, the Frobenius inner product, where $vu^T$ is the rank-one matrix with entries $v_iu_j$. Since the entries $X_{ij}$ are i.i.d. $N(0,1)$, the process $Z_{(u,v)}=u^TXv$ is centered Gaussian, and for $(u,v),(s,t)\in K$,
$$\mathbb{E}\big[(Z_{(u,v)}-Z_{(s,t)})^2\big]=\big\|\langle X,vu^T-ts^T\rangle_{HS}\text{ as a Gaussian form}\big\|^2=\|vu^T-ts^T\|_{HS}^2,$$
because $\langle X,M\rangle_{HS}\sim N(0,\|M\|_{HS}^2)$ for any fixed matrix $M$.

Now $\|vu^T\|_{HS}^2=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2=1$ (and likewise for $ts^T$), while $\langle vu^T,ts^T\rangle_{HS}=\mathrm{trace}(uv^Tts^T)=\langle u,s\rangle\langle v,t\rangle$. Hence
$$\|vu^T-ts^T\|_{HS}^2=2-2\langle u,s\rangle\langle v,t\rangle.$$
We claim this is at most $\|u-s\|^2+\|v-t\|^2$. Indeed, with $a=\langle u,s\rangle$, $b=\langle v,t\rangle$ (so $|a|,|b|\le1$),
$$\|u-s\|^2+\|v-t\|^2-\|vu^T-ts^T\|_{HS}^2=(2-2a)+(2-2b)-(2-2ab)=2(1-a)(1-b)\ge0.$$

The process $U_{(u,v)}=\langle u,Y\rangle+\langle v,Y'\rangle$ is centered Gaussian with, by independence of $Y,Y'$,
$$\mathbb{E}\big[(U_{(u,v)}-U_{(s,t)})^2\big]=\mathbb{E}\big[\langle u-s,Y\rangle^2\big]+\mathbb{E}\big[\langle v-t,Y'\rangle^2\big]=\|u-s\|^2+\|v-t\|^2.$$
Thus $\mathbb{E}[(Z_{(u,v)}-Z_{(s,t)})^2]\le\mathbb{E}[(U_{(u,v)}-U_{(s,t)})^2]$ for all $(u,v),(s,t)\in K$, and the Sudakov–Fernique inequality (Theorem 13.3) gives $\mathbb{E}\sup_K Z\le\mathbb{E}\sup_K U$.

Finally, $\sup_{(u,v)\in K}(\langle u,Y\rangle+\langle v,Y'\rangle)=\sup_u\langle u,Y\rangle+\sup_v\langle v,Y'\rangle=\|Y\|+\|Y'\|$, since $\sup_{u\in S^{n-1}}\langle u,Y\rangle=\|Y\|$. Hence $\mathbb{E}U=2\mathbb{E}\|Y\|\le2\sqrt{\mathbb{E}\|Y\|^2}=2\sqrt{n}$ by Jensen's inequality (Exercise 13.5), completing the proof. ∎
