**Central idea.** The largest GUE eigenvalue is $\sup_{u\in S^{n-1}}u^*Xu$, the supremum of a centered real Gaussian process. By the hint its canonical metric satisfies $\mathbb{E}[(u^*Xu-v^*Xv)^2]=\frac1n\|uu^*-vv^*\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\le\frac4n\|u-v\|^2$, so Slepian's lemma (Theorem 13.3) compares it with the isonormal process $H_u=\frac{2}{\sqrt n}\langle u,Y\rangle$ on $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$, whose supremum equals $\frac{2}{\sqrt n}\|Y\|$.

**Solution.** Let $X$ be an $n\times n$ GUE matrix: Hermitian with centered Gaussian entries. For $u\in S^{n-1}=\{u\in\mathbb{C}^n:\|u\|=1\}$, $G_u:=u^*Xu$ is real-valued (as $X$ is Hermitian) and, being a linear functional of the Gaussian entries of $X$, $(G_u)_{u\in S^{n-1}}$ is a centered Gaussian process. Its supremum $Z=\sup_{u\in S^{n-1}}G_u$ is the largest eigenvalue of $X$.

By the hint, $\mathbb{E}[(G_u-G_v)^2]=\frac1n\|uu^*-vv^*\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$. Since $\|uu^*\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=\mathrm{tr}(uu^*uu^*)=(u^*u)^2=1$ and $\langle uu^*,vv^*\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}=\mathrm{tr}(uu^*vv^*)=|u^*v|^2$,
$$\|uu^*-vv^*\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=2-2|u^*v|^2.$$
Writing $u^*v=re^{i\theta}$ with $0\le r\le1$, $1-r^2=(1-r)(1+r)\le2(1-r)\le2(1-r\cos\theta)$, whence $\|uu^*-vv^*\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\le8(1-\mathrm{Re}(u^*v))=4\|u-v\|^2$. Therefore
$$\mathbb{E}[(G_u-G_v)^2]\le\frac{4}{n}\|u-v\|^2\qquad u,v\in S^{n-1}.$$

Identify $\mathbb{C}^n\cong\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ and let $Y$ be a standard Gaussian vector in $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$. The process $H_u:=\frac{2}{\sqrt n}\langle u,Y\rangle_{\mathbb{R}^{2n}}$ is centered Gaussian with $\mathbb{E}[(H_u-H_v)^2]=\frac{4}{n}\|u-v\|^2\ge\mathbb{E}[(G_u-G_v)^2]$ for all $u,v$. By Theorem 13.3 (Slepian's lemma) applied to finite subsets, and separability of $S^{n-1}$,
$$\mathbb{E}\sup_{u\in S^{n-1}}G_u\le\mathbb{E}\sup_{u\in S^{n-1}}H_u.$$
But $\sup_{\|u\|=1}\langle u,Y\rangle=\|Y\|$, so $\mathbb{E}\sup_u H_u=\frac{2}{\sqrt n}\mathbb{E}\|Y\|\le\frac{2}{\sqrt n}\sqrt{\mathbb{E}\|Y\|^2}=\frac{2}{\sqrt n}\sqrt{2n}=2\sqrt2$.

Hence $\mathbb{E}Z\le2\sqrt2$, a universal constant $C=2\sqrt2\ge1$, independent of $n$.
