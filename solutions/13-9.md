**Central idea.** Chaining telescopes $\sup_{s}(X_s-X_{s_0})$ into increments $X_{\Pi_{j+1}s}-X_{\Pi_js}$ over refining nets. If the supremum exceeds $uE$, some increment must exceed its level threshold, and the thresholds are chosen to telescope to at most $uE$. Bernstein tails bound each increment; a union bound over at most $e^{2H(\delta_{j+1})}$ net pairs (as in Lemma 13.1) and summation over $j$ gives $\kappa e^{-u}$.

**Solution.** Work in the setting of Lemma 13.1 (the exercise's "Theorem 13.1"): $T$ finite, $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda(X_t-X_{t'})}\le a\lambda d(t,t')+\frac{v\lambda^2d^2(t,t')}{2(1-c\lambda d(t,t'))}$. Assume $\delta:=\max_{t}d(t,s_0)>0$ (if $\delta=0$ then $E=0$ and $X\equiv X_{s_0}$ a.s., a degenerate case). Let $\delta_j=2^{-j}\delta$, let $T_j$ be $\delta_j$-nets with $T_0=\{s_0\}$, $\Pi_j:T\to T_j$ with $d(t,\Pi_jt)\le\delta_j$, and $H(\delta)=\log N(\delta,T)$ nonincreasing. Since $T$ is finite, for $J$ large $\Pi_{J+1}(s)=s$ and $X_s-X_{s_0}=\sum_{j=0}^J(X_{\Pi_{j+1}s}-X_{\Pi_js})$ exactly, with $d(\Pi_{j+1}s,\Pi_js)\le3\delta_{j+1}$.

*Remark on the hint.* The threshold $\delta_{j+1}(3ua+2\sqrt{uvL}+cuL)$ in the hint has slightly optimistic constants in the $v,c$ terms to follow directly from Bernstein's inequality with $d\le3\delta_{j+1}$. We use the corrected threshold $\mathrm{thr}'_j:=3\delta_{j+1}(a+\sqrt{2uvL_j}+cuL_j)$ with $L_j:=j+2H(\delta_{j+1})$, which rigorously yields the per-pair bound below and still telescopes to at most $uE$.

*Per-pair tail.* From the mgf hypothesis, $Z:=X_s-X_t-ad(s,t)$ satisfies $\log\mathbb{E}e^{\lambda Z}\le\frac{(vd^2)\lambda^2}{2(1-(cd)\lambda)}$, so Bernstein's inequality (Chernoff bound) gives $\mathbb{P}\{X_s-X_t\ge ad(s,t)+d\sqrt{2v\ell}+cd\ell\}\le e^{-\ell}$. With $d\le3\delta_{j+1}$ and $\ell=uL_j$,
$$\mathbb{P}\{X_{\Pi_{j+1}s}-X_{\Pi_js}\ge\mathrm{thr}'_j\}\le e^{-uL_j},$$
since $\mathrm{thr}'_j\ge3a\delta_{j+1}+3\delta_{j+1}\sqrt{2uvL_j}+3c\delta_{j+1}uL_j$.

*Inclusion.* If $X_s-X_{s_0}\ge uE$ then some increment exceeds its threshold, because $\sum_{j=0}^J\mathrm{thr}'_j\le uE$: indeed, with $H_j:=H(\delta_j)$,
\begin{align*}
\sum_{j=0}^J\mathrm{thr}'_j
&=3a\sum_{j=0}^J\delta_{j+1}+3\sqrt2\sum_{j=0}^J\delta_{j+1}\sqrt{uv(j+2H_{j+1})}+3cu\sum_{j=0}^J\delta_{j+1}(j+2H_{j+1})\\
&\le3a\delta+3\sqrt2\,\delta\sum_{k\ge1}2^{-k}\sqrt{v(k+2H_k)}+3cu\,\delta\sum_{k\ge1}2^{-k}(k+2H_k)\\
&\le uE,
\end{align*}
reindexing $k=j+1$ (using $\sqrt{(k-1)+2H_k}\le\sqrt{k+2H_k}$) and, for $u\ge1$, $3a\le3ua$, $3\sqrt2<6\le6u$, $3c\le6cu$. Thus
$$\{\exists s:X_s-X_{s_0}\ge uE\}\subseteq\bigcup_{j=0}^J\bigcup_{s\in T}\{X_{\Pi_{j+1}s}-X_{\Pi_js}\ge\mathrm{thr}'_j\}.$$

*Union bound and summation.* For fixed $j$ there are at most $e^{H(\delta_j)+H(\delta_{j+1})}\le e^{2H_{j+1}}$ distinct pairs $(\Pi_{j+1}s,\Pi_js)$ (as in Lemma 13.1's proof), so with the per-pair bound,
$$\mathbb{P}\{\sup_sX_s-X_{s_0}\ge uE\}\le\sum_{j=0}^J e^{2H_{j+1}}e^{-uL_j}=\sum_{j=0}^J e^{-uj}e^{-2(u-1)H_{j+1}}.$$
For $u>1$: $=e^{-u}\big[e^{\,u-2(u-1)H_1}+\sum_{j\ge1}e^{-u(j-1)}e^{-2(u-1)H_{j+1}}\big]$. Since $H_{j+1}\ge0$, the sum over $j\ge1$ is at most $\sum_{j\ge1}e^{-u(j-1)}=\frac1{1-e^{-u}}\le\frac1{1-e^{-1}}$. Moreover $H_1=H(\delta/2)\ge\log2$: as $\delta>0$, two balls of radius $\delta/4$ (around $s_0$ and a point at distance $\delta$) are disjoint, so $N(\delta/2,T)\ge2$. Then $u\mapsto u-2(u-1)H_1$ is decreasing (as $H_1>\tfrac12$), hence $e^{\,u-2(u-1)H_1}\le e$ for $u\ge1$.

Therefore $\mathbb{P}\{\sup_{s}X_s-X_{s_0}\ge uE\}\le\kappa e^{-u}$ with the universal constant $\kappa=e+\frac1{1-e^{-1}}$. Up to constants, $E$ matches Lemma 13.1's expectation bound (the dyadic sums approximate the entropy integrals).
