**Central idea.** Mirror the proof of Theorem 14.6 with $\eta$ nonincreasing: the Bregman divergence $D_{\Phi}(\eta(y),\eta(x))$ is bounded by $\frac12(x-y)^2\varphi(\tilde z)$ where $\varphi=\eta'^2\Phi''(\eta)$ is nonincreasing (as $|\eta'|$, $|g'|$ are, for $g=\Phi'\circ\eta$ convex), evaluated at $\tilde z\le\min(x,y)$. Sub-additivity (Theorem 14.1) plus the variational formula (Lemma 14.4) give the $V$-bound; symmetrization gives the $V_+$/$V_-$ bounds. The stated hypothesis is insufficient as written: $\tilde Z$ must also lie below $Z$ (counterexample below).

**Solution.** Let $\Phi\in\mathcal C$ (so $\Phi''>0$, $\Phi'$ concave by Lemma 14.5) and let $\eta\ge0$ be nonincreasing, differentiable and convex on $I$; hence $\eta'\le0$ is nondecreasing and $|\eta'|$ is nonincreasing. Put $g=\Phi'\circ\eta$; since $\Phi'$ is nondecreasing and $\eta$ nonincreasing, $g$ is nonincreasing.

*Remark on the hypothesis.* The condition "$\tilde Z\le\min_iZ_i$" does not imply the conclusion. Counterexample: $n=1$, $\mathbb P\{X_1=0\}=\mathbb P\{X_1=10\}=1/2$, $Z=X_1$, $Z_1\equiv5$, $\tilde Z\equiv5$, $\eta(z)=e^{-z}$, $\Phi(x)=x\log x$. Then $\Phi\in\mathcal C$, $\eta$ satisfies the hypotheses, $g(z)=1-z$ is convex, and $\tilde Z\le\min_iZ_i$ holds, but $H_{\Phi}(\eta(Z))\approx0.346$ while $\frac12\mathbb E[V\eta'(\tilde Z)^2\Phi''(\eta(\tilde Z))]=\frac12\cdot25\cdot e^{-5}\approx0.084$. We therefore prove the theorem under the corrected hypothesis that $\tilde Z$ is an a.s. lower bound for $Z$ and all $Z_i$ (resp. $Z'_i$).

*Pointwise lemma.* Assume $g$ convex (hence $g'$ nondecreasing, $g'\le0$, so $|g'|$ is nonincreasing). Then $\varphi(t):=\eta'(t)^2\Phi''(\eta(t))=|\eta'(t)|\cdot|g'(t)|$ is nonincreasing, and for $x,y\in I$, $\tilde z\le\min(x,y)$,
$$D_{\Phi}(\eta(y),\eta(x)):=\Phi(\eta(y))-\Phi(\eta(x))-(\eta(y)-\eta(x))\Phi'(\eta(x))\le\tfrac12(x-y)^2\varphi(\tilde z).$$
Indeed, for $x<y$, with $h(t)=\Phi(\eta(y))-\Phi(\eta(t))-(\eta(y)-\eta(t))g(t)$, $h'(t)=-g'(t)(\eta(y)-\eta(t))$, so $D_{\Phi}=h(x)=\int_x^y|g'(t)|(\eta(t)-\eta(y))\,dt$; for $t\in[x,y]$, $|g'(t)|\le|g'(\tilde z)|$ and $\eta(t)-\eta(y)=\int_t^y|\eta'(s)|ds\le(y-t)|\eta'(\tilde z)|$, giving $\le\frac12(y-x)^2\varphi(\tilde z)$. For $y<x$, $D_{\Phi}=\int_y^x(\eta(y)-\eta(t))\Phi''(\eta(t))|\eta'(t)|dt$ (via $D_{\Phi}(a,b)=\int_b^a(a-s)\Phi''(s)ds$ and $s=\eta(t)$); for $t\in[y,x]$, $\eta(y)-\eta(t)\le(t-y)|\eta'(\tilde z)|$, $|\eta'(t)|\le|\eta'(\tilde z)|$, $\Phi''(\eta(t))\le\Phi''(\eta(\tilde z))$ (as $\Phi''$ is nonincreasing), giving $\le\frac12(x-y)^2\varphi(\tilde z)$.

*First inequality.* By sub-additivity (Theorem 14.1) and Lemma 14.4, $H_{\Phi}(\eta(Z))\le\sum_i\mathbb E[H^{(i)}_{\Phi}(\eta(Z))]$ with $H^{(i)}_{\Phi}(\eta(Z))\le\mathbb E^{(i)}[D_{\Phi}(\eta(Z),\eta(Z_i))]$ (variational formula at $u=\eta(Z_i)$). The lemma with $(x,y)=(Z_i,Z)$, $\tilde z=\tilde Z$ gives $\le\frac12(Z-Z_i)^2\varphi(\tilde Z)$; summing yields $\frac12\mathbb E[V\eta'(\tilde Z)^2\Phi''(\eta(\tilde Z))]$.

For the $\psi$-bounds, $\psi(x)=(\Phi(x)-\Phi(0))/x$ satisfies $\psi'(x)=[x\Phi'(x)-\Phi(x)+\Phi(0)]/x^2\ge0$ (by convexity of $\Phi$), so $\psi\circ\eta$ is nonincreasing; if convex, $|(\psi\circ\eta)'|$ is nonincreasing and $\chi(t):=\eta'(t)^2\psi'(\eta(t))=|\eta'(t)|\cdot|(\psi\circ\eta)'(t)|$ is nonincreasing. For $x<y$, $\tilde z\le x$,
$$(\eta(x)-\eta(y))(\psi(\eta(x))-\psi(\eta(y)))\le(y-x)^2\eta'(\tilde z)^2\psi'(\eta(\tilde z)),$$
since $\eta(x)-\eta(y)=\int_x^y|\eta'|\le(y-x)|\eta'(\tilde z)|$ and similarly for $\psi\circ\eta$.

*Second inequality.* By Lemma 14.4 and symmetry, $H^{(i)}_{\Phi}(\eta(Z))\le\mathbb E^{(i)}[(\eta(Z'_i)-\eta(Z))_+(\psi(\eta(Z'_i))-\psi(\eta(Z)))]$. If $Z'_i<Z$ this is $(\eta(Z'_i)-\eta(Z))(\psi(\eta(Z'_i))-\psi(\eta(Z)))\le(Z-Z'_i)^2\eta'(\tilde Z)^2\psi'(\eta(\tilde Z))$ (lemma with $x=Z'_i$); if $Z'_i\ge Z$ it is $0$. Hence $\le(Z-Z'_i)^2_+\eta'(\tilde Z)^2\psi'(\eta(\tilde Z))$, and summing gives $\mathbb E[V_+\eta'(\tilde Z)^2\psi'(\eta(\tilde Z))]$.

*Third inequality.* Unswapped, $H^{(i)}_{\Phi}(\eta(Z))\le\mathbb E^{(i)}[(\eta(Z)-\eta(Z'_i))_+(\psi(\eta(Z))-\psi(\eta(Z'_i)))]$; for $Z<Z'_i$ this is $\le(Z'_i-Z)^2\eta'(Z)^2\psi'(\eta(Z))$ (lemma at the left endpoint $x=Z$), i.e. $(Z-Z'_i)^2_-\eta'(Z)^2\psi'(\eta(Z))$, whence $\mathbb E[V_-\eta'(Z)^2\psi'(\eta(Z))]$. ∎
