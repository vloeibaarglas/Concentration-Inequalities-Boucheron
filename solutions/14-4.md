**Central idea.** For $r=1$, $\rho\equiv4$. For $r\in(1,2]$, write $\rho(z)=g(\sqrt z)$ with $g(t)=((1+t)^r+(1-t)^r)^{2/r}$; then $\rho''(z)=[t\,g''(t)-g'(t)]/(4t^3)$, so convexity is $t\,g''(t)\ge g'(t)$. Expanding with $u=1+t$, $v=1-t$ collapses this to $(v^{2r-2}-u^{2r-2})+4(r-1)t\,u^{r-2}v^{r-2}\ge0$, which after $a=u/v$, $p=r-1$ becomes $p(a^2-1)\ge a^{p+1}-a^{1-p}$, proved by two differentiations.

**Solution.** For $r=1$, $\rho(z)=((1+\sqrt z)+(1-\sqrt z))^2\equiv4$. Let $r\in(1,2]$ and $g(t)=A(t)^{2/r}$, $A(t)=(1+t)^r+(1-t)^r$, $t\in[0,1]$, so $\rho(z)=g(\sqrt z)$. Put $B(t)=(1+t)^{r-1}-(1-t)^{r-1}$, $C(t)=(1+t)^{r-2}+(1-t)^{r-2}$. Then $g'(t)=2A^{2/r-1}B$ and, with $A'=rB$,
$$g''(t)=2A^{2/r-2}\big[(2-r)B^2+(r-1)AC\big].$$
On $(0,1)$, $\rho'(z)=g'(\sqrt z)/(2\sqrt z)$; at $0$, L'Hôpital gives $\rho'_+(0)=\lim_{t\to0^+}g'(t)/(2t)=2^{2/r}(r-1)$ (since $B(t)/t\to2(r-1)$); at $1$, $\rho$ is left-differentiable with derivative $g'(1)/2$ by the MVT. Hence $\rho$ is differentiable on $[0,1]$.

Moreover, with $t=\sqrt z$, $\rho''(z)=[t\,g''(t)-g'(t)]/(4t^3)$ on $(0,1)$, so convexity follows from $t\,g''(t)-g'(t)=2A^{2/r-2}E_0\ge0$ where $E_0:=t(2-r)B^2+t(r-1)AC-AB$. With $u=1+t$, $v=1-t$:
\begin{align*}
(2-r)B^2+(r-1)AC&=(u^{2r-2}+v^{2r-2})-2(2-r)u^{r-1}v^{r-1}+(r-1)u^{r-2}v^{r-2}(u^2+v^2),\\
AB&=u^{2r-1}-v^{2r-1}-2t\,u^{r-1}v^{r-1},
\end{align*}
whence, using $t-u=-1$, $t+v=1$, $(u+v)^2=4$,
$$E_0=(v^{2r-2}-u^{2r-2})+4(r-1)t\,u^{r-2}v^{r-2}.$$
Put $p=r-1\in(0,1]$, $a=u/v\ge1$ (so $v=2/(a+1)$). Dividing by $u^{p-1}v^{p-1}>0$, $E_0\ge0\iff2p(a-1)\ge v(a^{p+1}-a^{1-p})\iff p(a^2-1)\ge a^{p+1}-a^{1-p}$. For $L(a)=p(a^2-1)-a^{p+1}+a^{1-p}$: $L(1)=L'(1)=0$ and $L''(a)=p\big[2-(p+1)a^{p-1}-(1-p)a^{-p-1}\big]\ge0$ on $[1,\infty)$, because with $b=1/a\in(0,1]$, $M(b):=(p+1)b^{1-p}+(1-p)b^{p+1}$ satisfies $M(1)=2$ and $M'(b)=(p+1)(1-p)(b^{-p}+b^p)\ge0$. Thus $L'\ge0$, $L\ge0$, so $E_0\ge0$, $\rho''\ge0$ on $(0,1)$, and $\rho$ (continuous on $[0,1]$) is convex. ∎
