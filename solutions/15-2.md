**Central idea.** Theorem 15.6 is proved by the same induction on $q$ as Theorem 15.5, but with $V$ in place of $V^+$: under the hypothesis $Z_i \le Z$ we have $(Z-Z_i)_+ = Z-Z_i$, so $V = \sum_i (Z-Z_i)_+^2$ is a genuine upper-tail variance proxy. Lemma 15.1's $V$-inequality carries the smaller coefficient $q/2$ (rather than $q-1$), which is exactly what removes the factor $2$ from the constant. The recursion $x_q \le (1-1/q)^{q/2} + \frac{1}{2\kappa_q}x_q^{1-2/q}$ is then closed by $x_q \le 1$ using strict concavity, just as in Theorem 15.5.

**Solution.** We prove Theorem 15.6: assume $Z_i \le Z$ for all $1 \le i \le n$. Then for any real $q \ge 2$,
$$\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q \le \sqrt{\kappa_q q\,\|V\|_{q/2}} \le \sqrt{\kappa q\,\|V\|_{q/2}},$$
where $V = \sum_{i=1}^n (Z-Z_i)^2$, $\kappa_1 = 1$, and for $q \ge 2$,
$$\kappa_q = \frac{1}{2\big(1-(1-1/q)^{q/2}\big)},$$
so that $\kappa_q \uparrow \kappa = \frac{\sqrt{e}}{2(\sqrt{e}-1)} < 1.271$ and in particular $\kappa_q \ge 1$.

Since $Z_i \le Z$, we have $(Z-Z_i)_+ = Z-Z_i \ge 0$, hence $V = \sum_i (Z-Z_i)_+^2$. We argue by induction on $k \ge 1$ that for all $q \in (k,k+1]$,
$$\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q^q \le \big(q\kappa_q\|V\|_{q/2}\big)^{q/2}, \tag{*}$$
i.e., with $x_q := \|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q^q/(q\kappa_q\|V\|_{q/2})^{q/2}$, that $x_q \le 1$.

For $k=1$ ($1 < q \le 2$), H\"older's inequality and Efron--Stein give
$$\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q^2 \le \|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_2^2 = \mathbb{E}[(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+^2] \le \mathrm{Var}(Z) \le \mathbb{E}V = \|V\|_1 \le \|V\|_{q/2},$$
using Lyapunov's inequality ($1 \le q/2$). Since $q\kappa_q \ge 1$, this yields $(*)$.

Let $k > 1$ and $q \in (k,k+1]$. Apply Lemma 15.1 (first inequality) with $\alpha = q-1$:
$$\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q^q \le \|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_{q-1}^q + \frac{q}{2}\,\mathbb{E}\big[V(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+^{\,q-2}\big].$$
By H\"older's inequality with exponents $q/2$ and $q/(q-2)$,
$$\mathbb{E}\big[V(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+^{\,q-2}\big] \le \|V\|_{q/2}\,\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q^{\,q-2}.$$
Dividing by $(q\kappa_q\|V\|_{q/2})^{q/2}$ and writing $\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_{q-1}^q = x_{q-1}^{q/(q-1)}\big((q-1)\kappa_{q-1}\|V\|_{(q-1)/2}\big)^{q/2}$, we obtain, using $\|V\|_{(q-1)/2} \le \|V\|_{q/2}$ and $\kappa_{q-1} \le \kappa_q$,
$$x_q \le x_{q-1}^{q/(q-1)}\Big(1-\frac1q\Big)^{q/2} + \frac{1}{2\kappa_q}\,x_q^{1-2/q}.$$
Indeed, the second term equals $\frac{q}{2}\|V\|_{q/2}\,x_q^{1-2/q}(q\kappa_q\|V\|_{q/2})^{(q-2)/2}/(q\kappa_q\|V\|_{q/2})^{q/2} = \frac{1}{2\kappa_q}x_q^{1-2/q}$; the absence of the factor $2$ (present in Theorem 15.5) is due to the coefficient $q/2$ in Lemma 15.1's $V$-inequality versus $q-1$ in the $V^+$-inequality.

By the induction hypothesis $x_{q-1} \le 1$ (as $q-1 \in (k-1,k]$), so
$$x_q \le \Big(1-\frac1q\Big)^{q/2} + \frac{1}{2\kappa_q}\,x_q^{1-2/q} = 1 - \frac{1}{2\kappa_q} + \frac{1}{2\kappa_q}\,x_q^{1-2/q},$$
where we used the defining identity $(1-1/q)^{q/2} = 1 - \frac{1}{2\kappa_q}$. The function $f_q(x) = 1 - \frac{1}{2\kappa_q} + \frac{1}{2\kappa_q}x^{1-2/q} - x$ is strictly concave on $[0,\infty)$ (since $0 < 1-2/q < 1$), satisfies $f_q(0) = 1-\frac{1}{2\kappa_q} > 0$ and $f_q(1) = 0$; hence $f_q(x_q) \ge 0$ forces $x_q \le 1$. This proves $(*)$.

Taking $q$-th roots gives $\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q \le \sqrt{\kappa_q q\,\|V\|_{q/2}}$, and $\kappa_q \le \kappa$ yields the second inequality. ∎
