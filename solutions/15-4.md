**Central idea.** Exercise 15.3 bounds the lower-tail moment by $\|V\|_{q/2}$; from $V\le WZ$, Hölder gives $\|V\|_{q/2}\le\|W\|_q\|Z\|_q$, and mimicking the proof of Corollary 15.8—Theorem 15.6 for the upper tail, then closing the loop on $\|Z\|_q$ with $\sqrt{ab}\le(a+b)/2$—yields $\|Z\|_q\le 2\mathbb{E}Z+2q\|W\|_q$, using $\kappa_q<1.271$ from Theorem 15.5. Substituting back into Exercise 15.3 gives the estimate.

**Solution.** Let $q\ge 2$ and assume $Z\ge 0$, the setting of Corollary 15.8. Since $0\le Z-Z_i\le M$ for all $i$, Exercise 15.3 applies and gives
$$\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_-\|_q\le\sqrt{C_2q\left(\|V\|_{q/2}\vee q\|M\|_q^2\right)},\qquad C_2<2.42.\tag{1}$$
Because $0\le V\le WZ$ with $W\ge 0$, Hölder's inequality (with $\frac{1}{q/2}=\frac1q+\frac1q$) yields
$$\|V\|_{q/2}\le\|WZ\|_{q/2}\le\|W\|_q\|Z\|_q.\tag{2}$$
Since $Z_i\le Z$ for every $i$, Theorem 15.6 applies to the upper tail. With $\kappa_q=\frac{1}{2\left(1-(1-1/q)^{q/2}\right)}\uparrow\frac{\sqrt e}{2(\sqrt e-1)}<1.271$,
$$\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q\le\sqrt{\kappa_q q\|V\|_{q/2}}\stackrel{(2)}{\le}\sqrt{\kappa_q q\|W\|_q\|Z\|_q}.\tag{3}$$
As $Z\ge 0$, $Z\le\mathbb{E}Z+(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+$ holds pointwise, so Minkowski's inequality gives
$$\|Z\|_q\le\mathbb{E}Z+\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_+\|_q\le\mathbb{E}Z+\sqrt{\kappa_q q\|W\|_q\|Z\|_q}.\tag{4}$$
Using $\sqrt{ab}\le\frac{a+b}{2}$ with $a=\|Z\|_q$ and $b=\kappa_q q\|W\|_q$,
$$\|Z\|_q\le\mathbb{E}Z+\tfrac12\|Z\|_q+\tfrac12\kappa_q q\|W\|_q,$$
and since $\kappa_q<1.271<2$,
$$\|Z\|_q\le 2\mathbb{E}Z+\kappa_q q\|W\|_q\le 2\mathbb{E}Z+2q\|W\|_q.\tag{5}$$
This is the first assertion of Corollary 15.8. Combining (2) and (5),
$$\|V\|_{q/2}\le\|W\|_q\left(2\mathbb{E}Z+2q\|W\|_q\right).\tag{6}$$
Inserting (6) into (1) yields
$$\|(Z-\mathbb{E}Z)_-\|_q\le\sqrt{C_2q\left(\|W\|_q\left(2\mathbb{E}Z+2q\|W\|_q\right)\vee q\|M\|_q^2\right)}.\tag{7}$$
*Remark on the stated form.* The inequality as printed has $\|M\|_q$ in place of the outer $\|W\|_q$ in (7); the two coincide when $\|W\|_q\le\|M\|_q$, e.g. when $W\le M$ a.s., as in Theorem 15.13 where the choice $W=M$ is made. Equation (7) is the precise form delivered by combining Exercise 15.3 with the proof of Corollary 15.8. ∎
